# A six-fifths time bound for the five-instruction DIMACS machine

For a fixed word-width coefficient $D\ge1$, give the separate-operation RAM word width $w=16\lceil D\log_2(N+2)/16\rceil$ on an $N$-bit file. Assume the width is uniformly computable from $N$ in polylogarithmic time, as it is for every fixed rational $D=p/q$ by comparing $2^{wq}$ to $(N+2)^p$. No fixed program with at most five stored instructions decides ordinary ASCII DIMACS 3-CNF in $O(N^{6/5})$ time. A shape-preserving change of decimal variable names strengthens the memory bound on the hard outputs. The declared variable bound can be polynomially larger than the number of occurring variables. The DIMACS Challenge's suggested CNF format explicitly permits declared variables to be unused. [DIMACS format, section 2.1](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~babic/doc/dimacs_cnf.pdf) · [Machine and classification](five-instruction-loops-on-dimacs-sat.md)

## Renaming variables

Start with the locally accessible three-literal clauses for a fixed nondeterministic linear-time language and an input of $n$ bits. There are at most $n\operatorname{polylog}n$ clauses and variable identifiers. Choose a public binary field width $K=\Theta(\log n)$ sufficient for all source identifiers, including any padding. For a fixed integer $t\ge2$, replace the $K$-bit binary identifier $b_1\cdots b_K$ by the positive **decimal** integer whose digits are

$$1\,(0^{t-1}b_1)\,(0^{t-1}b_2)\cdots(0^{t-1}b_K).$$

This is an injective relabeling. The leading one avoids leading zeros. Declare the all-ones-bit identifier's image as the variable bound in the header. It has at most $1+tK$ decimal digits and is $n^{O(t)}$. Every assignment on the original variables extends to the declared universe, and restriction recovers its values on the occurring variables. Thus satisfiability is preserved, including the empty and inconsistent cases. The binary digits of a source index are used as decimal characters without conversion of a large integer. [Construction of the source clauses](packed-band-transfer.md)

Emit each literal as one sign byte (space or minus), the fixed-width decimal name, and one trailing space. Each field occupies $tK+3$ bytes. Three fields followed by `0` and newline give a clause of stride $R=3(tK+3)+2$. Given a byte offset in the body, division by $R$ identifies the clause and within-clause offset. A second division by the field length identifies the literal. Its sign or one selected binary bit is locally available from the source generator; zeros, separators and the leading one are fixed. The header is public. Generating an entire $w$-bit guest word takes polylogarithmic time and workspace. No array proportional to the declared variable bound is initialized. The output has $N=n\log^{O(1)} n$ bits as an upper bound, and $\log N=\Theta(\log n)$ on the padded hard construction.

## Word alphabet at fixed width coefficient

In the body the consecutive variable **digits** of a field have distance $t$. Across two adjacent fields a sign can approach the last variable digit of its predecessor. Bound these sign and boundary effects explicitly. Each field has length at least $tK$, while one guest word spans $W=w/8=O_D(\log n)$ bytes. Such a window intersects $O_{D,t}(1)$ fields. Within any field fragment of $b$ bytes, at most $b/t+1$ selected digits and at most one sign vary. The fragments are disjoint, so over the entire window at most $W/t+O_{D,t}(1)$ independent binary positions vary, even when it crosses a clause terminator or multiple fields. For a given starting offset modulo $R$, every other byte is fixed. There are $R=O_t(\log n)$ starting phases. The header occupies $O_t(\log n)$ bytes, thus affects $O_{D,t}(1)$ aligned words. The zero scratch word and the final padded word contribute at most two more. On every such file the image $H$ of original memory satisfies

$$|H|\le O_{D,t}\big((\log n)2^{W/t}\big)=N^{D/(8t)+o(1)}.$$

Choose $t=2\lceil D\rceil$. This gives $|H|\le N^{1/16+o(1)}$ for every fixed $D\ge1$. The argument allows the hot positions at two adjacent field boundaries to lie closer than $t$.

## Transferring the clock

On a public crossed scan with a surviving fixed increment $d\ne0$, the period is at least $2^w/|d|\ge N/|d|$ for all sufficiently large inputs. Under a native $O(N^{6/5})$ clock, the number of passes is $O_d(N^{1/5})$. The last-writer theorem bounds distinct writes by $1+(P+1)^2|H'|$, with $|H'|\le|H|+O(1)$ allowing initialization. A trie of these writes, virtual word generation and bit-cost arithmetic give deterministic simulation in time $n^{6/5+o(1)}$ and space $n^{1/16+2/5+o(1)}=n^{0.4625+o(1)}$. The other small-space cases in the classification use $O(|H|+1)$ saved words or a fixed number of public counters. Even for $D>1$, the periodic family still has bounded initial alphabet, $\Theta(N/w)$ sensitive consecutive tail words, and even metadata. The episode lower bound is $\Omega((N/w)^2)$; a fixed-size excursion through high addresses costs $\Omega(2^w)$ per episode. Both exceed the proposed clock. The parity and word-algebra exclusions persist at this width. [Scan ancestry bound](ancestry-of-crossed-stores.md) · [Other instruction allocations](five-instruction-loops-on-dimacs-sat.md)

Corollary 5.8 of Fortnow, Lipton, van Melkebeek and Viglas rules out deterministic simulation of all nondeterministic linear-time languages in time $O(n^c)$ and space $o(n^{g(c)})$, where $g(c)=((c+2)/c^2-c)/2$. Take $c=1.201$. Then $g(c)>0.509$, whereas the simulated space is eventually $O(n^{0.464})$ and its time is $O(n^{1.201})$. This yields the stated contradiction. For other fixed gaps $t$, a sufficient condition for excluding a native $O(N^a)$ clock with $1<a<2$ is that some $c>a$ satisfy $D/(8t)+2\max(a-D,0)<g(c)$, using $2^w\ge N^D$ in counting passes. The constants $D,t$ are fixed before the source length grows.
