# Resolution complexity survives the one-NAE-triple reduction

Let $G$ be an unsatisfiable proper 3-CNF with $m$ clauses and let $T(G)$ be the [degree-four formula with one exceptional not-all-equal triple](sat-with-one-not-all-equal-triple.md). Use $S$ for the minimum number of lines of a general DAG resolution refutation and $W$ for its minimum maximum clause width. For both the at-most-four and exactly-four occurrence constructions,
$$
 S(G)\le S(T(G))\le O(S(G)+m+1),\qquad
 W(G)\le W(T(G))\le \max\{W(G),3\}+15.
$$
Width and size each denote their own minimum. The proof in fact translates one specified refutation while controlling both quantities. All three root values can also be fixed to any specified nonconstant triple before measuring the complexity. Proof length can count all input axioms if the chosen convention does so, since $T(G)$ has $O(m+1)$ clauses and the projection contains all the source axioms.

### Projecting the axioms clause by clause

Choose any nonconstant assignment to the three roots. Set every positive candidate and its chain values to true, and set all negative candidates and their chain values to false. In the paired initialization clauses the chain already takes its desired value, so the extra guard bit can have either constant value. The two intermediate aggregation bits for each chain group and each forced padding variable take their indicated constant. Every root, propagation and aggregation clause now contains a true constant.

Map each ordinary occurrence-copy variable back to its source variable, so each signed occurrence $A,B,C$ becomes the corresponding signed literal. Every copy link projects to a complementary pair. For the gadget
$$
 (f,A,\neg u),\quad (u,B,\neg v),\quad(v,C,\neg t)
$$
set $u$ to the **literal** $A$ and $v$ to the **complement of the literal** $C$. With $f=0$ and $t=1$, the first and last clauses are tautologies and the middle clause is exactly the source clause. These substitutions use only constants and literals and commute with complementation on negative occurrences. For each missing occurrence the regularization adds fresh variables and clauses including $(x,a,\neg b)$. Set every fresh regularization variable to zero. This satisfies that clause regardless of $x$, and the remaining all-fresh clauses are mixed. After deleting tautologies, false constants and repeated literals, the projected axioms are precisely $G$.

### Projecting a refutation

Treat clauses as sets. A projected clause containing a true constant or a complementary pair is tautological and can be skipped. For each other line $D$, keep a derived subclause of its normalized projection. An input axiom either vanishes or gives a source axiom. A weakening from a nontautological projected conclusion has a nontautological projected premise, whose stored subclause suffices.

Consider parents $A\lor p$ and $B\lor\neg p$ producing $A\lor B$. When the conclusion survives, the images of $A$ and $B$ contain no conflicting literals. Write their union after simplification as $U$. If $p$ projects to a constant, the parent where its pivot literal is false gives a subclause of $U$. Otherwise write its image as the signed literal $\ell$. A tautological left parent must have $\neg\ell$ in the image of $A$. Then the right parent survives and its entire projection is in $U$. The opposite case is symmetric. With two surviving parents, reuse either stored subclause if it is already contained in $U$. Otherwise their only literals outside $U$ are respectively $\ell$ and $\neg\ell$. Resolving them gives a subclause of $U$ in one line. The last empty clause must be stored as empty. This induction introduces at most one line per old line and never increases width. It permits several source pivots to be identified by the substitution and permits either parent to become tautological. The same subclause induction with the identity substitution eliminates weakening from an ordinary refutation.

### Simulating source clauses in the other direction

Split on the three roots and the twelve positive or negative candidates adjacent to them. A constant root triple falsifies a root clause. In each remaining branch either an adjacent clause is already false, or the assigned candidates include an active true source for a positive chain and an active false source for a negative chain. Work for the moment in the formula restricted by that branch. Resolving the two initialization clauses forces the first descendant of an active chain. Unit resolution along its consecutive triples produces all the required descendant values. In a group containing any active chain two such descendants force each of its intermediate bits, and its two forced intermediates force the group's padding value. Apply this once to obtain the false or true *unit* for every padding variable used by the data clauses and copy links. All these derivations cost $O(m+1)$ lines of width at most three. In a branch with an empty restricted clause, that clause already finishes the refutation.

Use the two padding units and two resolutions on $u,v$ to obtain each three-literal clause on its signed occurrence copies. Use its false padding unit to obtain each binary implication around a copy cycle. Choose one representative on each cycle. Traverse the cycle once forwards from that representative to derive its implication to each copy by transitivity, then once backwards towards the representative to derive every converse implication. Transitivity resolves two binary clauses to a binary clause. A singleton occurrence serves as its own representative. Replacing each signed occurrence by its equally signed representative in the occurrence clause costs at most one resolution per literal using the required direction of the implication. Properness guarantees distinct source variables in each triple. The branch now has all axioms of $G$ on consistently renamed variables with $O(m+1)$ work and width at most three. Replay any source refutation on those representatives.

To discharge a branch assignment $\rho$, adjoin its blocking clause (the disjunction of the negations of the assigned literals) to every line of the restricted refutation. For each restricted axiom that survives, the lifted clause is a weakening of its original axiom. A restricted proof only resolves on unassigned pivots. The lifted derivation therefore obtains the blocking clause with width increasing by at most fifteen. Merge the blocking clauses of all assignments by a binary resolution tree. The number of branches is at most $2^{15}$ independently of $m$. Weakening can be removed as above. When a nonconstant assignment of the roots is already supplied, only the remaining candidate bits need be split. The extra regularization axioms can be ignored in this simulation.

### A hard subfamily and the scope of the bound

Use the Tseitin parity contradiction on a connected 3-regular simple expander with $t$ vertices and a vertex charge of odd total parity. There are $3t/2$ edge variables and four proper clauses per vertex. Theorem 4.4 of Ben-Sasson and Wigderson gives refutation width at least their edge-expansion parameter, which is $\Omega(t)$ on these expanders. Their Corollary 4.5 gives general resolution size $2^{\Omega(t)}$, citing Urquhart's earlier result. The projection transfers both bounds to $T(G)$, including the exactly-four variant, whose only two homogeneous clauses share the same three root names. These formulas have $\Theta(t)$ distinct names and clauses, and $N=\Theta(t\log(t+2))$ encoded DIMACS bits. Thus their resolution width is $\Omega(N/\log N)$ and their resolution size is $2^{\Omega(N/\log N)}$. The original parity contradiction is recognizable by linear algebra. The unrestricted deterministic RAM time question about SAT remains open. [Ben-Sasson and Wigderson, *Short proofs are narrow*, Theorem 4.4 and Corollary 4.5](https://people.inf.ethz.ch/emo/SatSem05/Papers/BensassonWidgerson01.pdf)

The [Lean proof](ProjectedResolutionStep.lean) verifies the local resolution-line transformation for arbitrary projected subclauses and the constant-pivot and weakening cases. The [finite audit](nae_literal_projection_audit.py) checks the projection of 609,408 parsed physical clauses on all 256 subsets of the three-variable catalog in both occurrence variants, along with 1,410 local resolution and subclause cases. The global refutation translations and their asymptotic bounds are manuscript proofs. Priority for this normal form has not been established.
