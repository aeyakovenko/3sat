# How wide must a folded certificate be on the physical forecast?

Choose the slot period $P$ in the physical controller compilation to be an odd prime larger than three times its greatest slot mark. The construction permits this choice before fixing the two-bank recurrence or any SAT trial. Let $d$ be the multiplicative order of $2$ modulo $P$. On **every sufficiently large canonical width**, any locally inductive partial certificate for its zero verdict has matrix width at least $d$. This holds independently of whether any fast SAT solver exists. In particular the finite exhaustion of certificate widths $1,\ldots,d-1$ on each of the resulting SAT-program prefixes has a negative answer at all sufficiently large widths. The result applies to this particular choice of physical encoding. The proof uses a sharp one-sided statement for ordered binary matrix computations. The matrices may vary with every bit position and input length.

## Positive density while avoiding one residue

Fix an odd prime $P$ and a width $w<d$. Take words of $h$ bits encoding $x$ least significant bit first. Consider any binary row vector, any two $w\times w$ matrices over $\mathbb F_2$ at each layer selected by the bit, and any final column. If their scalar product vanishes on every $x\equiv r\pmod P$, its support has density at most $C\theta^h$, with $C<\infty$ and $0<\theta<1$ depending only on $P,w$. The omitted residue and all matrices may vary with $h$.

Call a reachable row live at a layer if some continuation has output one. For each live row $y$, let $R(y)$ be the residues of all prefixes that reach it, with bits weighted by their original binary place values. The set is nonempty and proper. If it contained all residues, an accepting continuation would also accept an input in the omitted class. Along a live transition the old set, translated by the contribution of the new digit, is contained in the new set. Its cardinality can only grow.

Let $L=\lceil\log_2 P\rceil$ and $M=(P+1)^{2^w}$. From any live row at a position with at least $b=(M+1)L$ remaining bits, there is a $b$-bit continuation reaching a dead row or strictly increasing residue-set size. Suppose otherwise. Death is permanent and sizes cannot shrink on live paths, so every intermediate row in that whole subtree is live with its residue set an exact translate of the starting set $A$. Distinct translates of $A$ are different, since a nonzero translation generates $\mathbb Z/P$ and $A$ is nonempty and proper. At the boundaries after $L,2L,\ldots,(M+1)L$ bits, label each row reached from this start by the translation residue, leaving unreached rows unlabelled. All $P$ labels occur: the first $L$ bits alone can realize every residue and the remaining bits can be zero. Each boundary configuration is a function from at most $2^w$ rows into $P$ labels plus absence. Two of these $M+1$ configurations coincide.

Between these two boundaries choose one word whose contribution is $1\bmod P$. This is possible since the segment has at least $L$ bits and its starting place value is invertible modulo $P$. The product matrix $T$ of this word sends every row labelled $a$ into the class labelled $a+1$ in the **same** configuration. Algebraically iterate this one matrix on any labelled row. Its eventual cycle length must be divisible by $P$. The eventual image of $T$ is a vector space of dimension $j\le w$ on which $T$ is invertible. Hence $P$ divides the order of the group of invertible $j\times j$ binary matrices. Choosing an ordered basis gives that order as $2^{j(j-1)/2}\prod_{i=1}^j(2^i-1)$. Primality forces $d\le j$, a contradiction.

A uniformly random $b$-bit block now has conditional probability at least $2^{-b}$ to reach death or strict growth from a live row. There are only $P-1$ possible live sizes, from $1$ to $P-1$. In a chunk of $(P-1)b$ bits, the probability of death is at least $2^{-(P-1)b}$ by choosing a progress word at each block while the row stays live. Iterating on successive chunks proves the claimed exponential bound. The constants are coarse. The proof needs no fixed automaton across lengths. Lean verifies the path-counting step from a bounded integer credit, an absorbing dead state and one strict-progress extension per live state. Use credit $P-|R(y)|$ for a live row and zero on death. The existence of a progressing block, the matrix-order argument and their forecast application are manuscript proofs. [Lean counting lemma](PrimeSlotProgress.lean)

Conversely a width-$d$ family has positive density while avoiding any prescribed residue $r$. In $\mathbb F_{2^d}$ choose $\alpha$ of order $P$. Starting from one, the matrices at layer $i$ multiply by one or by $\alpha^{2^i}$, giving $\alpha^x$ at the end. Use a binary linear functional vanishing on $\alpha^r$ and nonzero on $\alpha^{r+1}$. These two elements are distinct nonzero vectors over $\mathbb F_2$ and hence linearly independent. This accepts at least one full residue class and omits $r$. The threshold is exact for avoiding a single residue with positive density. This construction need not satisfy the local forecast equations. Programs over finite monoids provide related lower-bound methods. Priority for this one-sided density formulation is unestablished. [Barrington and Thérien, *Finite Monoids and the Fine Structure of NC¹*](https://doi.org/10.1145/48014.63138)

## Every inductive certificate has positive density on an actual slab

Put $B=2^h$, with physical width $W=w_0 B$ and the six-block representation of timestamps. In the complete interval $B\le t<2B$ the five high rows are fixed. Each folded pin $K_0$ or $K_1$ therefore restricts to a width-$w$ ordered matrix computation on the $h$ low bits alone. A regenerated constant-copy slot is one at every one of its ticks, and a slot residue outside the marks is zero at every tick. Thus each honest pin omits at least one entire prime-period residue in this interval: $K_0$ omits a one residue and $K_1$ omits a zero residue. Subtracting the interval's lower endpoint just shifts the omitted class. If $w<d$, their combined support density tends to zero.

Local inductivity forces the opposite. At a pinned zero time, a whole parity bank must have its predecessors pinned with forced parity zero. If neither bank is forced zero, each could attain one and the two choices are compatible by disjointness. At a pinned one time both banks must be forced one. Each bank contains at least one fixed short lag, for instance from the constant-copy wiring. Choose one short lag per bank and call the larger one $K$. Starting at the pinned observation $H\gg2B$, one can repeatedly go to a pinned predecessor between one and $K$ ticks earlier, until reaching the fixed negative initialization. Such a decreasing chain visits at least $\lfloor B/K\rfloor-1$ distinct times inside $[B,2B)$. This positive density contradicts the preceding bound. Only the two guaranteed slot values and local closure were used. It applies to every valid proof on an actual zero width, including widths of the designated SAT guest. An arbitrary truthful subset without the closure check need have no such chain.

For width one the obstruction has a shorter proof. Fixing the five upper rows makes the support an ordinary subcube of the low bits. With $f$ free positions it meets at least $\min(P,f+1)$ residues: each free bit adds a nonzero power of two, and adjoining a nontrivial translate grows any nonempty proper subset by at least one. A pin omitting a class has at most $2^{P-2}$ elements in the slab, incompatible with the required chain at large $B$.

The period can be chosen large enough to exclude any preassigned constant certificate width, since only finitely many primes divide $\prod_{i\le w}(2^i-1)$. That changes the physical construction. With one fixed prime the bound $d$ is constant, and the searches at width $d$ and above remain open. A period that is a power of two is also legal. In that implementation an individual slot residue is specified by low bits, and the prime-period exclusion does not apply. The unrestricted SAT clock would still require an actual accepting lift on its own fixed program prefix. [Finite test and conditional SAT implication](finite-lifts-for-forecast-certificates.md)

## Searching the native gates before expanding the physical tick schedule

There is a positive change of variables for any fixed legal period. Work in logical block time $u$ with one pair of pin functions $K_{i,0}(u),K_{i,1}(u)$ per physical gate slot $i$ at residue $s_i$. Keep separate copies where the compilation has separate slots. At each claimed bit check the actual gate against arbitrary settings of its unpinned intended parents, including the exact block delay of each edge and the two negative seeds. Only demand closure for $0\le Pu+s_i\le H$, and pin the designated terminal output zero. This formulation omits the ticks between gate slots.

These local checks produce a physical certificate. At $t=Pu+s_i$ use the two pins from slot $i$. At every unmarked residue pin zero. Three-sum isolation of the slot marks ensures that at an unmarked destination at least one of the two banks has **no** tap arriving from any marked source. All its inputs are pinned zero. At a marked destination two-sum isolation identifies every possible nonzero arrival with an intended gate parent, and the local gate checks apply. Conversely, from any valid physical pins keep the pins at the marked times. Strengthen the physical assertions by fixing all off-slot bits to zero. The new off-slot requirements are preserved by the preceding argument, and the stronger antecedents preserve all the old implications. Restriction to the marked times now gives the gate checks.

For a fixed period this translation preserves existence of bounded folded width, with an explicit constant overhead depending on the compiler. On the six-row arithmetic encoding the equation $t=Pu+s_i$ has a constant-size digit transducer: multiply each digit of $u$ by the fixed $P$, add it and the fixed offset into signed carries, subtract the digits of $t$, then glue the block-boundary carries uniquely as in the finite-lift note. For a given $t$ at slot $i$, exactly one $u$ contributes. Conversely for each in-range $u$ the forward $t$ is unique. Thus a slot representation can be shifted to physical time with width at most a compiler constant times its width, and conversely. Direct sums over slots implement the pin unions since different slots are disjoint. The off-slot indicator is one plus the sum of the disjoint slot indicators. Interval checks remove times beyond the prescribed horizon. Initial and endpoint vectors account for the seeds and cutoff. Hence one can search for a finite lift on the gate-level trace of the designated SAT guest without constructing every off-slot test or allowing the slot period to inflate the trial's initial certificate width. The guest program and its actual initialized gate computations must still be specified. Changing the guest's verifier can change its self-referential prefix.

For a power-of-two slot period, the single-residue test uses just the low bits of the timestamp. For prime period the additional representation cost proved above can be large, even if the logical pin functions are simple. The existence equivalence concerns a fixed logical trajectory and corresponding observation. It does not identify the runs of two self-referential SAT guests constructed with different verifiers.
