# Balanced splits for 3-SAT with missing clause supports

Let \(H\) be the hypergraph of three-variable supports in a proper 3-CNF on \(v\) occurring variables. Signs and multiple clauses on a support are arbitrary. Let \(E\) count its independent vertex subsets of size at least three. Given the dense support and sign table, there is a deterministic word-RAM algorithm listing every satisfying assignment in
\[
                 O(v^3+vE+1)
\]
steps when \(E\le Cv^2\log(v+2)\) for any fixed \(C\). In particular it takes \(O_C(N)\) native steps on such proper DIMACS files of \(N\) bits. For \(E\le Cv^2\), the same solver takes \(O_C(1+N/\log(N+2))\) steps on well-formed comment-free packed files, including reading the clauses and explicitly writing the solution bit vectors. These statements concern the named variables. The input condition is recognizable within the respective clock, allowing a fallback on other inputs. The improvement over scoring field permutations comes from charging each independent set only constantly many times **summed over all dyadic levels**. ## One representative per independent set

Assign every independent set of size at least three to one of its triples, for example its three least original coordinates. Denote the resulting nonnegative triple weights by \(w_t\); their total is \(E\). Pad the vertex set to \(p=2^h\) positions, with \(v\le p<2v\) for \(v>1\). A dummy position has value zero on every candidate. The algorithm chooses the binary partition tree itself; its final leaf order gives the physical coordinate order for the internal constraint table.

At a level with \(b=2^j\) equal-sized blocks let \(A_j\) sum the weights of triples entirely in one block. Let \(B_j\) sum the weights of the pairs in these triples that have both ends in one block: each representative supplies its three individual pairs, each of weight \(w_t\), even if the third coordinate is elsewhere. Put \(P_j=bA_j+B_j\). A triple contained in one block belongs to the union of exactly \(2b-1\) ordered block pairs. A triple using two different blocks occurs in two ordered block pairs and has one internal pair. A triple on three blocks occurs in none. The total weighted count of representative occurrences in block-pair unions at this level is consequently at most \(2P_j\).

Pair up the vertices arbitrarily within each current nonsingleton block. Independently send the two members of each pair to opposite children, using one fair orientation bit per pair. Both children are exactly half the parent's size. A formerly internal pair has probability at most \(1/2\) of remaining internal after the split, with probability zero if its endpoints are paired. A formerly internal triple has probability at most \(1/4\) of remaining internal: either it contains a chosen pair and separates for certain, or its three vertices are controlled by three independent fair bits. The factor in front of \(A\) doubles at this step. Linearity gives
\[
    \mathbb E[P_{j+1}\mid\text{current partition}]
       \le 2bA_j/4+B_j/2=P_j/2 .
\]
Choose orientations successively by the method of conditional expectations. For each flip only summands incident to its two vertices can change. For a remaining pair or triple use at most eight assignments to its still-undecided orientation variables to evaluate either proposed choice. Previously separated summands remain zero for the appropriate pair or triple test. Use a common denominator eight. Form the incidence lists at each level in \(O(v+M)\) time for \(M\) distinct representatives of positive weight; each occurrence appears constantly many times. Pair terms can be kept with multiplicity. Weighted values fit a constant number of logarithmic words under the stated cap. This picks all orientations at the level in \(O(v+M)\) steps and achieves \(P_{j+1}\le P_j/2\). The procedure takes \(O((v+M)\log(v+2))\) steps. Since \(P_0=4E\), the total representative score over the whole layout is at most
\[
                  2\sum_{j=0}^h P_j\le16E .
\]
In particular no independence assumptions on the triples charged by different sets are used.

## Computing the weights within the clock

Store for each pair \(x,y\) a packed length-\(v\) bit vector whose bit for \(z\) records the absence of support \(\{x,y,z\}\) on distinct vertices. Construct this from the dense table within \(O(v^3+1)\) steps. Enumerate independent sets in increasing order. Maintain the bit set \(D(S)\) of vertices larger than the largest selected vertex that extend \(S\) independently. Initially all vertices are possible. For a child using \(x\in D(S)\), intersect \(D(S)\) with the suffix above \(x\) and with the missing-support vector of \((x,y)\) for each \(y\in S\). This gives \(D(S\cup\{x\})\) exactly, because the newly possible violations are the triples using both \(x\) and the next vertex. Each sorted independent set occurs once. Weight accumulation needs only its three first vertices.

There are \(1+v+{v\choose2}+E\) nodes. Every visited independent \(s\)-set has \(2^s\) independent subsets. Enforce the public cap on visited nodes or depth if the promise is being recognized. Before either cutoff the depth is \(O_C(\log(v+2))\). Each child formation uses \(O((1+|S|)\lceil v/w\rceil+1) = O_C(v+1)\) native word operations for \(w=\Omega(\log(v+2))\). Scanning a candidate bit vector for the next children costs at most \(O(v+1)\) per node even without a hardware bit scan. The total is \(O_C(v^3+vE+1)\) on promised inputs. On dense inputs failing the cap, replace \(E\) in this runtime by the public bound. Test the number of distinct supports first to reject sparse inputs before allocating a cubic table. With the public cap \(Cv^2\log(v+2)\), the number of missing triples is \(o(v^3)\), so every passing dense input has \(N=\Omega_C(v^3\log(v+2))\) as in the earlier argument. The packed recognizer at cap \(Cv^2\) uses the fixed-width normalization procedure and its count of distinct supports. [Packed normalization](packed-normalization-of-dimacs-clauses.md)

## Enumerating satisfying assignments

Use the packed recent-variable block algorithm with block length \(q=\Theta(\log(v+2))\) aligned to the chosen power-of-two layout. Skip both wrong-value extensions at an infeasible prefix and force zero at each dummy. On a fixed union \(U\) of two dyadic blocks the number of actual prefix patterns is bounded by the number of independent subsets of \(U\): the elementary Sauer inequality bounds a family by the number of its shattered sets, and a shattered set containing a clause support would realize the forbidden signing of that clause. Unassigned or dummy coordinates cannot be shattered.

Sets of size at most two give the same \(O(v^2\log v)\) full-rectangle count per block start and \(O(v^2)\) mixed-rectangle count per stage as for complete triples. For larger sets, an independent set contained in a two-block union brings its own representative with it. All levels of the full dyadic computation therefore add **at most \(16E\) distinct-key allowances in total**, using the chosen layout. Restricting to the mixed rectangles preserves the bound. The admissibility applies to the actual canonical old-node identifiers and recent subwords; those are determined by the projected current bit pattern at their fixed location. A single-word packed OR across the upcoming variables uses \(O(q)\) lookups at a full-rectangle unit leaf. Its union has at most two coordinates and hence contributes no size-three independent sets to that cost.

The individual mixed checks and extension steps cost \(O(v^2+E)\) per stage. There are at most \(O(v^2+E)\) current roots. Batched persistent updates cost \(O((v^2+E)\log(v+2))\) per block end, and the whole expensive path installation occurs once in \(q\) stages. Full-rectangle block starts cost \(O(v^2\log(v+2)+E)\). There are at most \(p<2v\) physical stages, even with the zero gaps retained. It follows that the total including decoding the final models is \(O_C(v^3+vE+1)\). Distinct supports are cubic up to lower-order terms under either cap; consecutive long names have the same total spelling lower bound when arbitrary names replace them, since only \(3E\) of their complete-incidence occurrences can be missing. Thus the asserted encoded bounds follow from the corresponding parsers and \(v^3=O_C(1+N/\log(N+2))\). The conclusions allow arbitrary signs on the given supports.
