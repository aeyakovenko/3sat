# A one-clause successor that forces one more cycle at almost every size

Start with $(x_1\lor x_2\lor x_3)$. At size $h$, append exactly one clause
$$
                       (x_i\lor x_j\lor x_{h+1}),
$$
where $i<j\le h$ each currently occur at most three times. Every old clause stays verbatim. Write $b_h$ for the minimum cycle rank of *any* ternary Boolean table network computing the formula on every assignment of its $h$ labeled terminals. Each terminal is supplied just once. Copies, alternative wiring and arbitrary existential auxiliaries are allowed. Cycle rank means $|E|-|V|+c$, where $c$ is the number of connected components. [Network model, normalization and earlier encoding bounds](cycles-needed-to-represent-a-ternary-formula.md)

**Theorem.** Every finite seed obtained by these rules admits a computable sequence of single-clause extensions on which
$$
             h-O(h/\log h)\ \le\ b_h\ \le\ h-3
$$
at every sufficiently large $h$. On this sequence all but $O(h/\log h)$ of the individual steps through size $h$ increase the optimum by **exactly one**, even when the new network may be rebuilt from scratch. On every legal sequence $b_{h+1}-b_h$ is zero or one. At three and four variables it is possible to use a tree for every admitted formula. Five is the first size at which one cycle can be forced.

Every clause has three distinct variables, every variable occurs at most four times, and there are $h-2$ clauses. All closed formulas in this construction are satisfiable. Even arbitrary changes to the signs of individual literal occurrences leave a satisfying assignment that is computable in one forward pass. The growing invariant concerns the predicate on externally imposed assignments.

## The finite obstruction

At size four the two clauses have two common literals $a,b$. The read-once formula $(a\lor b)\lor(c\land d)$ gives a tree. At size five take the triples $123,124,135$. Any forest representation could be joined to a tree with independent dummy wires. To join components split one wire in each with a ternary table passing its original value and ignoring its extra port, then connect the extra ports. After pruning, a subcubic tree with five labeled leaves has a pair separated from the other three by one edge. Fixing that edge to zero or one expresses its acceptance matrix on the cut as a union of at most two rectangles.

For these three clauses every one of the ten pair cuts fails that test. For example, order the row variables $1,2$ and the other three increasingly, with the first bit least significant in each part. The row/column pairs $(0,3),(1,0),(2,1)$ are accepted; for every two of them at least one cross-pair is rejected. A rectangle contained in the accepting set can cover at most one. The same test on the remaining cuts is checked in Lean. The previous tree and the one-cycle upper step below give $b_5=1$. Among the 18 labeled histories at size five, three have $b_5=0$ and fifteen have $b_5=1$. The three trees use the same two literals in all three clauses. The audit excludes every five-leaf tree for the other fifteen by evaluating its possible edge splits. A single chain attains optima $0,1,2,3$ at four through seven variables. Its clauses in order are $123,124,345,156,367$. At six variables all pair cuts require three rectangles, while only two of the ten unordered three-against-three cuts admit four. This excludes a tree and a single cycle. At seven variables the proof also excludes arbitrary tables on the remaining two-cycle topologies. It uses twelve independently replayed resolution refutations after the topological reduction. [Exact seven-terminal proof and finite certificates](seven-terminals-need-three-independent-cycles.md)

Extend once more by $168$. At eight variables every pair cut requires at least three rectangles. Only the triples $367$ and $168$ admit four rectangles and their intersection is the single variable $6$. In a two-cycle core, distributing eight terminal sites among three paths either puts four on one path or at least three on each of two paths. The former gives two consecutive triples meeting in two sites, the latter two disjoint triples. The exceptional cuts allow neither. Thus three cycles are necessary, also by pinning $x_8$ true. Three are sufficient: group $123,124$ as $(x_1\lor x_2)\lor(x_3\land x_4)$ and $156,168$ as $(x_1\lor x_6)\lor(x_5\land x_8)$. Each grouped constraint has a tree presentation. The four factor scopes are $1234,345,1568,367$. Their connected incidence graph has 14 edges on $8+4=12$ vertices, hence three independent cycles. Replacing factors and variable sites by their ternary trees preserves this count. The exact sequence from four through eight variables is therefore $0,1,2,3,3$. At this parent at least ten of the 21 choices for the next pair give a plateau: each pair in $345,156,367$ can be shared with its existing ungrouped clause, and the pair $12$ already serves the two grouped clauses. In the last case the grouped relation becomes $(x_1\lor x_2)\lor(x_3\land x_4\land x_8)$, still a tree. [Lean cut certificates and Boolean identities](../lean/EightTerminalCycle.lean)

## The two inequalities for a successor

Pinning $x_{h+1}$ true in any network for the successor gives a network for its parent with the same or smaller cycle rank. In the other direction start with an optimal parent, fork the terminal wires of $x_i,x_j$ using two equality tables, and attach one ternary OR table to the two copies and the new terminal. Both old wires retain their values. Counting the new edges and vertices adds one cycle if the two terminals were in the same component and zero otherwise. This proves the claimed increment bounds for *the minima*, regardless of old network size. Iterating from $b_3=0$ proves $b_h\le h-3$. It also shows that the exact number of zero increments between sizes $3$ and $h$ is $h-3-b_h$.

## Why many successive increases are unavoidable

Choose the next unordered pair uniformly among all eligible pairs. No isolated input names occur, and the sum over all variables of their degree minus one equals $3(h-2)-h=2h-6$. Each saturated degree-four variable accounts for three units. Thus there are at least $(h+6)/3$ eligible names and at least $h^2/18$ choices per step. A predicate determines its set of triples: setting just the bits of a proposed triple to zero tests whether it occurs. Each triple's largest index determines when it was added. Consequently, given any fixed seed at size $s$, the probability of a specified predicate at size $h\ge s$ is at most
$$
                   18^{h-s}\left(\frac{(s-1)!}{(h-1)!}\right)^2 .
$$

The forest normalization gives at most
$$
       \sum_{j=0}^B C^{h+2j+1}\frac{(h+2j)!}{2^j j!}
$$
external predicates with cycle rank at most $B$. Here $C=2^{32}$ suffices. To see an explicit encoding, use ordered rooted trees joined to an artificial root. After suppressing nonterminal degree-two paths there are at most $2L$ ordinary vertices with $L=h+2j$ terminal positions. A parenthesis representation requires two bits per vertex. Per ordinary vertex reserve one terminal flag, eight local table bits in incident order, and four bits for the binary path relation to its parent. A global bit covers constant rejection. The arity and actual table bits are recovered from the forest shape and flags. Unary masks on isolated terminals fit in their local bits. Assign labeled terminals and pair the remaining positions as in the normalization. [Detailed forest count](cycles-needed-to-represent-a-ternary-formula.md)

For $0\le B\le h$ the count is at most $(h+1)2^{32(3h+1)}(3h)^{h+B}$. Put $l_h=\lfloor\log_2 h\rfloor$ and declare a level bad if $b_h\le B_h=h-\lceil160h/l_h\rceil$. Negative cutoffs give no bad level. Multiplying the two preceding bounds and using $(h-1)!\ge(h/e)^h/h$ gives
$$
 \log_2\Pr(\text{bad level }h)
 \le (B_h-h)\log_2 h+125h+2\log_2((s-1)!)+40
$$
when $B_h\ge0$. For all sufficiently large $h$ this is at most $-h-2$. These generous constants serve only to make the tail estimate effective. The union bound over all large levels yields simultaneous near-maximal cycle rank, without requiring independence between levels. The plateau identity gives the claimed count of strict increases. In particular there are runs of $\Omega(\log h)$ consecutive strict increases inside every sufficiently large interval $[h,2h]$.

For computability, first choose an effective cutoff $H$ so the total probability of a bad level from $H$ onward is less than $1/2$. At each finite level, the existence of a presentation of rank at most $B_h$ is decidable: enumerate the finite reduced forests with at most $B_h$ pairs of cut ports, and evaluate each restored predicate on every external assignment. Only valid network encodings enter this decision. For a candidate next prefix, sum the exact rational probabilities of its extensions avoiding bad levels through a finite horizon $J\ge H$. Subtract the unconditional tail bound beyond $J$, using probabilities in the original distribution conditioned only on the seed. The resulting lower bound approaches that prefix's mass of infinite good extensions. Whenever the current prefix has positive such mass, at least one child does too. Increase $J$ until one child has a positive certified lower bound. Repeating this rule computes the infinite branch. No feasible running time is asserted for finding it.

## Tests on ordinary SAT executions

The unpinned family has a stronger escape than positivity alone. For any independent signs on its literals, set each newly introduced variable to make its literal in its own clause true. Choose the initial assignment to satisfy the first clause. At no stage does a later assignment change an earlier clause. There can still be exponentially many *different candidate assignments required in advance* across sign choices. The clause hypergraph has a matching of at least $(h-2)/10$ disjoint clauses: selecting a triple deletes at most $1+3(4-1)$ triples. At most a $(7/8)^{(h-2)/10}$ fraction of assignments satisfy the positive formula. Flipping a fixed subset of variable signs translates this satisfying set and preserves the minimum cycle rank. A preselected list meeting every such translation needs at least $(8/7)^{(h-2)/10}$ assignments by averaging. Reading the signs lets a program construct a witness immediately.

For a genuine two-verdict test, append a pin for each variable. Make each pin independently editable at one ASCII bit. Use names $10i+2,10i+3$ for a variable and its complement, enforce complementarity by two padded clauses, and select which name is true by a padded unit clause. Three private guard variables are forced false by the seven proper ternary clauses forbidding their other assignments. All variable references and rows have fixed widths of order $\log h$ bits. The resulting proper DIMACS has length $N=\Theta(h\log h)$ in bits.

Every accepting run of any correct deterministic SAT RAM must read true pins covering **every** source clause. Otherwise change the unread pins of an uncovered clause to false: this preserves all read replies and makes the formula unsatisfiable. Each true pin covers at most four clauses, hence at least $(h-2)/4$ true pins must be read. At a minimal satisfying assignment every true pin is individually pivotal. Flip the true pin whose containing word is first read last among those pins. The accepted and rejected files differ in exactly one bit and follow the same run until that read. Since each aligned word meets only a constant number of the spaced pin sites, **both real executions take $\Omega(h)=\Omega(N/\log N)$ steps** with unlimited workspace and the entire native instruction set. The fixed template has a matching $O(h)$-step evaluator, including validation on these files. Decode each fixed-width decimal field in a constant number of chunks of at most $\lfloor(\log_2 h)/16\rfloor$ bytes. An ASCII chunk fits in half a logarithmic word, and a table for its value and lexical validity takes $O(\sqrt{h}\log h)$ setup steps. Fixed punctuation and the public guards can be checked in the same passes, then evaluate each triple using the pinned array. An unrestricted fallback handles other layouts. The header gives the size parameter on the prescribed files and can be validated before sizing the tables.

Thus even a density-one sequence of strictly necessary cycles, a large preselected witness list, and adjacent opposite verdicts forcing a real scan can coexist with a packed scan-time solver. The missing SAT induction would have to lower-bound the elapsed work of deciding the file after that scan. The finite certificate and counts here do not measure that work. Priority for the nested cycle-rank statement has not been established.
