# The original minus-to-space DIMACS template at power-of-two widths

For the original homogeneous DIMACS record ending in a minus byte, every correct program on the separate-operation RAM with the twenty-operation native catalog takes at least $(7m-2463)/4$ instructions on the base file whenever the byte width $W\ge32$ is a power of two. The file contains $m$ marked copies and can be filled to $m=\lceil N/w\rceil-O(1)$ for $N$ encoded bits, $w=8W$. Under the width rule $w=16\lceil D\log_2(N+2)/16\rceil$ these widths occur exactly for $8W-16<D\log_2(N+2)\le8W$, one window of lengths for each power of two $W$. The program may even be chosen separately for each length. On another infinite sequence of widths, $W=60+510t$, the identical template admits a five-instruction test per triple of records on its edit promise, so $5/3$ is the exact coefficient on that promise there. The coefficients distinguish two width behaviors of this *fixed* physical record. The count is in packed words and stays below a scan: a program specialized to the file accepts it in $2\lceil N/w\rceil+O(1)$ instructions, so the bound is $\Theta(N/\log N)$ in encoded bits. The argument uses only that every nonempty set of edits flips the verdict. It applies unchanged to the conjunction "every marked word equals $A$" and does not pass the SAT-specificity test. The recolored ordinary file already gives the larger coefficient $2-o(1)$ at every width on a different template. Let $A$ be the native word whose consecutive little-endian bytes are `3 1 2 0` and newline, spaces and a final minus. In $B$ the minus becomes space. Their high bytes are $45,32$ respectively. The aligned word boundaries put the minus immediately before the first literal of the following clause. The public prefix forces all three variables false, so every nonempty set of replacements by $B$ is a valid, unsatisfiable proper formula. All branch types are equality or unsigned/signed order with either fixed successor taken; the twenty arithmetic and numerical-comparison operations are as in the native catalog.

Use the acyclic, literal-free component count on the actual trace. For at most three source records a tree has at most two charged factors. We show there is always an edit preserving its branch comparisons once the free marked-address pins are omitted. If all comparisons involve raw operands, flip every source: each such comparison sees equal arguments on both configurations. A component without comparisons imposes no constraint at all. If a comparison consumes a calculated value, the only remaining case is an operation $f(X,Y)$ compared with a third source $Z$. Reuse of its own input by this comparison would form a cycle. Inputs and comparisons can be named in either order. It suffices to exhibit one altered triple giving the base comparison result.

* For AND or OR, flip all three inputs together. Idempotence preserves the equality of the compared words. XOR and subtraction give zero on both diagonals, so flipping $X,Y$ leaves their numerical result unchanged. All numerical comparisons likewise agree on equal operands.
* For full shifts both possible counts are at least $w$. For modulo shifts and rotations, $A-B=13\cdot256^{W-1}$ is divisible by $w$ at power-of-two $W$ for all sufficiently large $W$. Changing only the count from $A$ to $B$ thus preserves the output.
* Modular addition on $A,A$ has high byte $90$ or $91$; neither lies in $[32,45]$. The modular square of $A$ has high byte outside this interval as well, as established below. Leaving $X,Y$ untouched and changing only $Z$ cannot change equality or either order outcome. If the arithmetic result is signed negative it is less than both positive raw words in signed order and above both in unsigned order.

## Eight possible high bytes of the square

Let $P$ be the little-endian integer of the eight-byte prefix including newline, $Q=256^W$, $R=Q/256$, $U=255P-32\cdot256^8$ and $V=255\cdot45+32$. Then $255A=U+VR$. Here $U$ is negative; only $U^2$ and $2UV \bmod 256$ enter. Write $D=255^2$. Modulo $Q$ the $R^2$ term in the square vanishes. To obtain $U^2/D$ modulo $Q$, choose $0\le t<D$ such that $U^2+tQ$ is divisible by $D$. For $U^2<R$, its integer quotient has high byte $\lfloor256t/D\rfloor$: the added fraction is less than $1/D$ and cannot carry to an integer. The cross term contributes $2UV D^{-1}$ modulo 256 to the high byte. Since $D\equiv1\pmod{256}$, this contribution is $2UV\bmod256$.

Now $Q\equiv1+255W\pmod D$ by the binomial theorem. At $W=2^h$, $W\bmod255$ has period eight because $2^8\equiv1\pmod{255}$. For $h=5,6,\dots,12$, reducing $t=-U^2 Q^{-1}\bmod D$ gives the eight high bytes $182,55,56,58,62,70,86,118$. All lie outside $[32,45]$ and $U^2<256^{31}$ already. This proves the required statement at every power-of-two byte width $W\ge32$. [Integer and all-opcode audit](../audits/power_width_homogeneous_forest_audit.py)

Each tree of at most three sources must now have a free pin on a marked address. Its wires in the base run are values of constant-free unary formulas on $A$ with at most two binary operations. There are at most $E=1+20+2\cdot20^2=821$ such values. Distinct chosen pins correspond to distinct marked loads, so at most $E$ small trees occur. The other unanchored trees each have at least four sources. The actual trace therefore satisfies $4T+3E\ge7m$ by the common incidence count. Unbounded writes and indirection enter through charging every other address observation. [Native replay and forest accounting](charging-native-addresses-in-a-fixed-ram-trace.md)
