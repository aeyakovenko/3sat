# Exact cost of resolving two disputed SAT restriction verdicts

**26 November 2026.** In the same-length RAM census, two candidate solvers can disagree on a formula even when one is correct on every padded restriction. The usual way to settle the disagreement asks for a witness one bit at a time. The following game gives an exact tradeoff between restriction verdicts and direct assignment checks. Honest-oracle selection and removing short advice from SAT have a classical literature. No priority claim is made for this counting argument. [Three-file census and same-length issue](three-files-for-the-unrestricted-sat-clock.md) · [Hirahara, *Identifying an Honest EXPᴺᴾ Oracle Among Many*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.07258) · [Karp and Lipton](https://doi.org/10.1145/800141.804678)

Let a known domain contain $M\ge1$ assignments. The hidden subset $W$ contains the satisfying ones. Two advisers have already given opposite verdicts on the entire domain. At least one answers every query $S$ truthfully by the bit $[S\cap W\ne\varnothing]$. Allow **every subset** as a query. The selector can also test membership of a single assignment directly and receive its true bit. Its only information about $W$ comes from these two operations. Subsequent adviser queries and direct checks may be interleaved adaptively.

**Theorem.** A deterministic selector decides nonemptiness on every input using at most $q$ further binary adviser replies and $b$ direct checks on every execution if and only if

$$
                         b\,2^q\ \ge\ M .
$$

For sufficiency, repeatedly ask the adviser who initially said YES whether the left half of the current domain meets $W$. Keep the left half for YES and the right half for NO, stopping at one point or after $q$ questions. Check every surviving assignment. Their number is at most $\lceil M/2^q\rceil$. When $W$ is nonempty the YES adviser is the honest one, so a witness survives. On an empty input every direct check fails regardless of the replies. For $M=2^v$ the splitting queries can be ordinary variable-prefix restrictions.

For necessity, fix $u$ in the domain. The NO adviser always returns NO. The YES adviser answers every subset exactly as for the singleton $\{u\}$. Use exactly these same two advisers for an empty instance and for an instance with sole witness $u$. Each world has an honest adviser. They give identical observations until a direct check tests $u$.

In the selector's binary decision tree force every direct reply to be negative. A direct check then has only one child. Each adviser question has at most two. The tree with all unary steps contracted has at most $2^q$ leaves, even if different paths terminate after different numbers of questions. Indeed, complete the shorter leaf bitstrings by all their extensions to length $q$. These extension sets are disjoint. For each leaf list the at most $b$ points checked along its uncontracted path. The union of these lists contains at most $b2^q$ points. One may enforce the two budgets by stopping at a budget violation on any unrealizable transcript. If $u$ lies outside this union, the actual empty and singleton runs reach the same leaf. Exact decision requires the union to cover the entire domain.

**One-sided randomized version.** With the same worst-case budgets, a selector which always rejects empty inputs can achieve worst-case success probability exactly $\min(1,b2^q/M)$ on nonempty inputs. Pick a uniform subset of $h=\min(M,b2^q)$ assignments and apply the same splitting strategy inside it. It succeeds whenever the sample contains a witness, an event of probability at least $h/M$. For $h=0$ always reject. Conversely, for each random tape, the negative-reply tree covers at most $b2^q$ of the $M$ singleton candidates. Almost every tape rejects every one of the $M$ coupled empty instances, because this is a finite family. Averaging over a uniform $u$ gives the matching upper bound for some singleton. Random subset selection here belongs to the abstract game.

For $v$ variables and $1\le b\le2^v$, exact decision requires $q\ge v-\lfloor\log_2 b\rfloor$. Thus even polynomially many direct checks in $v$ save only $O(\log v)$ disputed verdicts in the worst case. The bound already holds under a promise of at most one solution. At $v\ge3$ one can realize each singleton by fixing every variable with a unit condition, expanded into four proper ternary clauses using two other variables as guards. An inconsistent pair of pins realizes the empty set. These formulas also illustrate the hypothesis that matters for lifting the bound to a RAM. Their clause bytes disclose the pins, and an algorithm with the actual formula can inspect them without asking either adviser. Multiple executions on related formulas therefore cannot be charged as work inside one RAM execution by this theorem. The Lean proof checks indistinguishability of the two runs for every point outside the queried list and bounds the size of a list covering all distinguishable points by $b2^q$. The finite audit searches every possible subset question and direct check in the coupled empty/singleton game. With domains of sizes one through seven it reproduces the threshold on 140 budget triples. The splitting algorithm passed 3,965 nonempty runs and 369 empty-input adviser transcripts. The upper strategy and randomized conclusion are manuscript proofs.
