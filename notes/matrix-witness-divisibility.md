# Local collisions among modular witness counts for explicit 3-SAT

Take an arbitrary list of $N=2^w$ *distinct* records of $2w$ bits each, and form the journal's explicit dense-name 3-CNF that is satisfiable exactly when two records coincide. Suppose a bounded-fan-in Boolean verifier of size $O(Nw)$ accepts some witness exactly for satisfiable members of this family. Fix a prime $p$. There are constants $\sigma_p>0$ and $c>0$ such that, for any fixed $0<\alpha<\sigma_p$ and all sufficiently large $w$, changing at most $N/w^\alpha$ records yields a satisfiable formula on which the number of accepting witnesses is **positive and divisible by a power of $p$ of size at least $c w^{\sigma_p-\alpha}$**. The constant $c$ can depend on the size bound, $p$ and $\alpha$. A simultaneous version applies to several verifiers. There is also a pair of opposite SAT verdicts indistinguishable by an arbitrary joint function of sufficiently inexpensive witness counts modulo powers of the same prime, even for verifiers with no one-sided guarantee.

The external input is the pinned November 24 preprint's lower bound on trivalent tensor-network volume over $\mathbb F_p$ for list distinctness. We use its one-list statement. This note does not independently establish that external theorem or priority for the local consequences. The circuit normalization matters: a verifier has $\ell$ explicitly counted binary witness bits. Charge $S=1+\ell+\text{gate and wire count}$, including uses of input bits. One uniform bound $S\le C Nw$ must hold at each length with the same $C$. The formula length is $\Theta(Nw)$ bits. Its only variable fields can be wired to record bits and constants by using aligned name banks in the cited construction. Inputs outside this family impose no requirement. ## Which records to change

For an integer $1\le u\le w$, put $M=2^u$ and partition the record universe by the first $2(w-u)$ bits into $(N/M)^2$ blocks. Each block contains $M^2$ words. At least one block contains at most $M^2/N\le M$ of the distinct seed records. Release $M$ slots including every record in that block. Keep all other seed records fixed. In each released slot put an arbitrary $2u$-bit word after the selected common prefix. The frozen rows stay distinct and disjoint from the released rows. Therefore the restricted function on the $2uM$ free bits is precisely list distinctness $D_u$. Every resulting list differs from the original seed in at most $M$ positions. The seed itself need not meet the variable slots' prefix condition.

The seed also admits $N/M$ disjoint sets of $M$ editable positions, each of which supports such a restricted copy on its own. Write $d=N/M$. For $d\ge2$, fewer than $d$ of the $d^2$ alphabet blocks can contain more than $M$ seed positions. Select $d$ of the other blocks. Assign all positions whose seed value lies in a selected block to that block's group. These compulsory sets are disjoint and each has size at most $M$. Distribute the unassigned positions to fill each group to exactly $M$. Varying just one group over its own alphabet leaves all frozen records outside that alphabet. For $d=1$ use the full list. It follows that the simultaneous-divisibility witness constructed below exists separately in each of these disjoint groups of edits.

On this slice the asserted source bound excludes trivalent networks for $D_u$ of volume at most $2Mu\,u^{\sigma_p}$ for all sufficiently large $u$. Original frozen bits are constants, and duplicating a free bit at many uses costs volume proportional to its uses. We charge $O(2uM)$ for exterior bits, even when a proposed computation ignores some of them.

## Counting sorted subsets of witnesses

Let $m_i(x)$ be the number of accepting $\ell_i$-bit witnesses for verifier $i$ on the explicit formula of list $x$, and let $Q_i=p^{b_i}$ with $b_i\ge1$. For a fixed rank $0\le r<Q_i$, guess $r$ distinct accepting witnesses in strictly increasing lexicographic order. Each accepted $r$-element subset contributes exactly once. A single Boolean circuit exposes all ranks for a total cost $O(Q_i S_i)$. Use $Q_i-1$ slots with a prefix of active bits, pin each inactive witness to the all-zero string, check acceptance on every active slot and check strict order on adjacent active slots. Empty lists have one encoding; a genuine all-zero witness is distinguished from padding by the active bit. Consecutive flags and the end conventions expose the prefix length by a one-hot list of $Q_i$ flags. The internal gates have unique evaluations. A tensor contraction over the witness and flag bits consequently gives $\binom{m_i(x)}{r}$ when the exposed length is $r$. Standard copy and Boolean gate tensors have arity at most three. The flags can also weight each active slot by $-1\in\mathbb F_p$ using unary tensors.

For $Q=p^b$ the signed sum is exactly the required indicator:
$$
\sum_{r=0}^{Q-1}(-1)^r\binom m r=[Q\mid m]\quad\text{in }\mathbb F_p .
$$
Indeed $(1+z)^Q=1+z^Q$ in this field, so coefficients of degrees below $Q$ in $(1+z)^m$ equal those in $(1+z)^{m\bmod Q}$. Evaluating the latter polynomial at $z=-1$ gives one for residue zero and zero for each other residue. Thus the network for one divisibility test has volume $O(QS)$ in addition to the free exterior bits. This proof directly tracks the size needed here. Relations between bounded ambiguity, parity and prime-power counting criteria are classical. [Beigel and Gill, *Counting classes*](https://cis.temple.edu/~beigel/papers/bg-mods-tcs.html) · [Hemaspaandra et al., *Gaps, Ambiguity, and Establishing Complexity-Class Containments*](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.MFCS.2022.57)

## Simultaneously divisible counts

Suppose each verifier is **sound** in the sense that $m_i=0$ on every distinct list. No lower bound on $m_i$ for duplicate lists is needed yet. On the slice, multiply the single-test tensors, wiring copies of each free input bit to its uses. Over any duplicate list where at least one count is nonzero modulo its $Q_i$, the product is zero. On every distinct list it is one. If each duplicate list had at least one such informative residue, the product would compute $D_u$, in volume at most
$$
 C_p\left(2Mu+\sum_i Q_iS_i\right).
$$
The constant is uniform over the number of verifiers and the moduli. This contradicts the source whenever the displayed bound is below $2Mu\,u^{\sigma_p}$. Consequently some duplicate list within the $M$ released positions has **all** $m_i(x)\equiv0\pmod{Q_i}$. Each verifier complete on all duplicate lists has a positive count there. A preselected family of predicates that keep only some witnesses can have counts zero.

For the opening statement choose $u=w-\lceil\alpha\log_2 w\rceil$, so $N/(2w^\alpha)\le M\le N/w^\alpha$ and $u/w\to1$. For a single $S\le C Nw$ verifier take the largest power of $p$ below $\eta w^{\sigma_p-\alpha}$, with a sufficiently small fixed positive $\eta$. The volume comparison holds for all sufficiently large $w$ and $Q\ge (\eta/p) w^{\sigma_p-\alpha}$. In particular every linear-size complete verifier has accepting instances of high witness multiplicity in each such record neighborhood. The argument works separately for each fixed characteristic; the instance can depend on $p$.

## Even arbitrary two-sided readouts collide

Here the verifiers may count arbitrary witness sets on either kind of list. Let $B=\prod_i Q_i$. Every function
$$
 f:\prod_i\{0,\ldots,Q_i-1\}\longrightarrow\{0,1\}
$$
of the residues has an $\mathbb F_p$ trivalent realization on the slice of volume
$$
 O_p\left(2Mu+\sum_i Q_i S_i+B\right).
$$
For one coordinate the $Q_i$ by $Q_i$ matrix with entries $\binom{s}{r}\bmod p$, for $0\le s,r<Q_i$, is lower triangular with diagonal one. Hence the products over $i$ of these binomial functions form a basis for all field-valued functions on the residue tuples. Choose the expansion coefficients of $f$ nonuniformly. Guess the sorted witness lists as above and weight their contribution by the one coefficient indexed by their exposed ranks. Because coefficients of degrees below $Q_i$ depend only on $m_i\bmod Q_i$, the contraction computes $f$ at the actual residue tuple.

For the lookup bound, encode each field coefficient in the fixed $\lceil\log_2p\rceil$ bits of its canonical representative. Select that entry of the mixed-radix table with the one-hot rank flags. A nested $Q_i$-way Boolean selection tree costs $O_p(B)$ including uses and copies of flags: each node with $Q_i$ children uses $O_p(Q_i)$ gates and wires, and an internal node has at least two children. For each of the $p$ possible coefficients $a$, compare the selected code with the code of $a$ and attach a unary tensor with weights $1$ on false and $a$ on true. The product of those $p$ weights is the selected coefficient. The deterministic part of the lookup has a unique evaluation. This avoids a factor $B$ on the witness-verification cost.

If the displayed total volume bound is below $2Mu\,u^{\sigma_p}$, then on this slice **two lists, one distinct and one with a duplicate, have exactly the same entire tuple of residues**. Otherwise a truth table separating the two disjoint signature images would give the excluded network. Both lists are obtained from the same arbitrary distinct seed by editing only the $M$ released slots. The two-sided form allows any readout, even one selected separately for that very slice. Its common signature need not be the signature of the seed. The one-sided result has no $B$ charge because it only needs the product of individual zero tests.

## What the bound measures

Linear-size nondeterministic verifiers of duplicate existence do exist. Guess two indices in increasing order and check equality of the selected records with two $O(Nw)$-size multiplexer trees. The witness count is the number of equal unordered pairs of indices. The paper's volume lower bound forces heavy divisibility for **every** sufficiently small complete verifier, including different encodings of the evidence. The result concerns the circuit size of a verifier and the number of its witnesses. A deterministic logarithmic-word RAM can sort these records in $O(N)$ steps by a constant number of radix passes. Transferring the count obstruction to unrestricted SAT time would require a way to turn such adaptive computations into verifiers within the stated size and residue budgets. In particular this argument alone charges no RAM time for achieving high multiplicity.
