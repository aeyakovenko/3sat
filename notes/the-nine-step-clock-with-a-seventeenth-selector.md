# Adding a genuine seventeenth selector preserves the nine-step clock

The original 16-selector catalog has a worst-case optimum of nine instructions on its four-bit RAM. With the word width raised to five, the same optimum holds on the following four-word inputs. Code size and register count are unrestricted, all 32 data cells are writable, and both literal and register-indirect memory access are allowed. [Original theorem and machine conventions](exact-nine-step-clock-for-four-sat-words.md)

| Independent selector bits | Five-bit words in address order | Number of files |
|---:|---|---:|
| 16, with a fresh variable forced false | $(P,Q,16+M,N)$ with old nibbles $P,Q,M,N$ | 65,536 |
| 17 | $(P,Q,16z+M,N)$ with $z\in\{0,1\}$ | 131,072 |
| 18 | Positive and negative selectors for nine variables, in separate word-aligned sign planes of five and four variables | 262,144 |

The new variable occupies position five in the fixed positive disjunction. At 17 bits it has one optional negative unit, selected by $z$, and no positive unit. Setting $z=1$ forces the new variable false and preserves every old verdict. Setting $z=0$ makes the old file with zero positive selectors and all negative selectors satisfiable. The extra selector is therefore genuine. At 18 bits the variable's positive unit can also be chosen. Physical storage is four five-bit words in each row. A tightly concatenated 18-bit string crosses a sign-plane boundary inside a word and gives a different machine question. [Physical layouts, embeddings and separate tight-layout tests](forcing-an-early-cross-pair-read-at-five-bit-width.md)

For the largest catalog, write its words as $A,B,C,D$. It is satisfiable exactly when $A\mathbin{\&}C=B\mathbin{\&}D=0$ and $(C,D)\ne(31,15)$. The old nine-step program tests $C=31$, chooses the required pair checks and works on all 262,144 inputs. The lower bound below uses the first row of the table. It transfers to the other two rows by restriction of the physical inputs. Every correct program on any of the three catalogs takes at least nine instructions on some satisfiable file among
$$
S=\{(p,q,31-p,15-q):4\le p,q\le11\}.
$$

## Coupling the time of the first branch

Each of the four original words on a target is essential. Replacing its underlying old nibble by 15 introduces a conflict with its nonzero mate. A correct path therefore needs four fresh original reads before their first overwrite. Suppose all 64 targets finish by instruction eight. Their accepting paths then have at most four binary comparisons. The support of a leaf is the number of members of $S$ that reach it.

* A four-comparison leaf has support at most one. The four reads must use literal original addresses on a target, since every raw target word is at least four. On the high suite there are 122 distinct eligible raw guard masks and 3,772 negative files. All 2,016 pairs of targets fail the separating four-guard test.
* A three-comparison leaf has support at most eight. The four fresh reads leave at most one ALU, store or surplus load. Without arithmetic the high suite reduces the path to raw words and constants. With an ALU, the five possible direct or single-indirect layouts give 7,360 distinct register tables. Across 39,817,571 pairs of candidate guard masks, no triple separates nine targets from the negative suite.
* A two-comparison leaf has support at most eighteen. For direct addressing the stronger capacity is sixteen, checked on 2,628 wiring templates and 203,851,164 operation/literal assignments. With at most one ALU, high-band addresses reduce to a finite collection of raw, read-only and store cases. The three high-band censuses use all 1,497 first arithmetic tables and cap the corresponding supports at eight or sixteen. For two ALUs, four fresh loads and two comparisons leave no store. Move literal loads to the start of this fixed path, and each computed-address load directly after its ALU. The 14 genuinely indirect layouts account for both orders of two missing addresses on different targets. The primary search exhausted all 16,417 first tables and 11,945,866 fast second tables. It performed 5,767 concrete comparison-pair tests past the quick negatives. No path reached nineteen targets.

Each branch alternative includes both outcomes of equality, signed order and unsigned order. Full-table representatives agree on the entire tested set. Partial tables are reused only after every eligible guard fails on those same partial lanes. For two guards, a positive control of support eight was replayed on all 65,536 physical files. [Word operations, scalar tests and raw guards](../audits/adjacent_width_native_clock_audit.py) · [Address and store classification for two comparisons](exhausting-two-alu-indirect-accepting-leaves.md)

Now use the position of the first comparison, shared by all input files because the instruction labels have not yet branched. If it comes by step seven, every accepting path with just this comparison may drop its later data-only tail and repeat its guard once. The same sound leaf fits the eight-step two-comparison census. Its support is therefore at most eighteen. Applying the marked-rejection recurrence with one-, two-, three- and four-comparison caps $(18,18,8,1)$ gives at most 63 accepted targets, contradicting 64. [Marked-tree recurrence](../lean/EightStepMarkedCapacity.lean)

If the first comparison comes at step eight, every target must immediately enter an accepting terminal. The branch outcome taken by a fixed negative file cannot lead to such a terminal. Consequently all 64 targets must take the *same* outcome. This is the only one-comparison case left to exclude. The required threshold is 64. [Shared first comparison and clock in Lean](../lean/SharedFirstComparisonAtDeadline.lean)

## The last comparison at instruction eight

On an accepting target path, the seven previous instructions contain four fresh reads and at most three other instructions. All target words are high, so a raw input cannot itself address an original data cell. A read-only calculation can hoist the direct loads and retain every previous result. Scratch contains zero until written. Genuine negative files for the tests use the actual contents at the requested five-bit address.

With zero ALUs, fixing the last writer at each surplus read partitions the high files into at most four cases of raw equality tests. The four-raw-guard bound excludes support 64. With one ALU and no store, repeated read-only lookups yield only $r,Lr,L^2r,L^3r$ in addition to literals and raw words. The high-band lookup and guarded-store censuses also treat four fresh reads with up to two stores. For the remaining arrangement of five reads, one store and one ALU, the liberal one-store simulation checked 2,395,200 address/value choices and 3,325,946,985 comparison-operand pairs. If a pre-ALU read first becomes nonsimple after the store, partition on equality of its two earlier addresses. This was the only surplus read, so at most eight targets survive on each piece. [Low-arithmetic reductions and the mixed-store partition](marked-leaves-and-high-word-lookups.md)

With two ALUs and no store, take all 28,855 embedded negative files satisfying $L(A)=L(B)=L(C)=L(D)=0$. Each arithmetic result again yields at most three further read-only iterates. Across 1,725 first tables and 4,768,183 second tables, no single guard covers even 37 targets. Two ALUs, one store and four direct reads admit a guarded store erasure. For the store's address $t$, the set $J$ of later direct addresses must include $t$ on every passing negative file, and every covered target must have $t\notin J$. The smallest suitable $J$ maximizes support. Exhausting 1,561 first and 3,012,702 second tables on 20,412 high negatives eliminates this case. The same adaptive erasure permits one computed read by imposing $t\ne r$ when the address $r$ is read after the store. [Adaptive erasure in Lean](../lean/FreshReadStoreErasure.lean) · [One-store and terminal-layout reductions](erasing-one-store-in-the-eight-step-native-census.md)

For an unprocessed last fresh input word, vary the underlying old nibble while fixing all others. Its physical values lie either in $0,\ldots,15$ or in $16,\ldots,31$. Both signed and unsigned five-bit order are increasing in the old nibble on this fiber. Among complementary values $x=4,\ldots,11$, an accepting initial interval can contain only $x=7$ and no accepting final interval is possible. A disequality guard accepts an overlapping modification. An order guard therefore covers at most 28 targets, the maximum number having the old value 7 in some coordinate. Equality with a literal has the same bound. Equality with a different physical input requires an equal underlying nibble across different mate pairs and covers at most sixteen. A previous fresh address below four cannot supply the high endpoint. This eliminates paths whose every ALU supplies one of the fresh addresses. If an earlier ALU supplies a genuinely computed equality endpoint, the residual census keeps it.

The residual one-guard search has eight one-store layouts, sixteen terminal-read layouts and fourteen terminal-arithmetic layouts. In the latter it mutates the last fresh word to the representation of underlying 15, retaining the fixed high bit of negative word $C$. The primary run covered 948,090,755 first-table/second-operation pairs in 1,848 rectangles. The areas for the three cases were 243,032,860, 345,290,265 and 359,767,630. All potential guards already failed on target support or on the quick genuine negatives. These checks exhaust the last comparison case. The combination of the marked recurrence and these terminal exclusions proves the lower bound.

## Reproduction

Run the raw and scalar truth-table checks with `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 adjacent_width_native_clock_audit.py`. To materialize the five-bit versions of the earlier finite census kernels, compile them and run all the enumerations use the following command from this checkout with Python 3, NumPy, a C++20 compiler and the patch utility.
 
```sh
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 five_bit_selector_clock_replay.py --selectors 18 --output /tmp/wider-selector-clock
```

The replay retains the generated C++ sources, compiler options, hashes and completed intervals under the supplied directory. The patch preserves the four-bit source files in the checkout and makes all changed literal ranges, data widths, support thresholds and physical loads available for inspection. A second run compiled the kernels with Clang 15 at `-O2`, shuffled the negatives with seed 20680722, and split the first-table ranges at width 36. It exhausted 464 pointer rectangles and 1,238 residual rectangles with the same capacities as the GCC 11 run. Both compiler runs use the same underlying enumeration algorithms. An independent scalar checker reconstructs every first-table cardinality and second-operation area, and verifies the full rectangle covers. Run `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 five_bit_selector_clock_replay.py --receipt-bundle five-bit-selector-clock-receipts.json.gz` to audit the two sets of archived receipts. [Finite census receipts](../audits/five-bit-selector-clock-receipts.json.gz)

The marked-tree, store-erasure and first-comparison lemmas have Lean proofs. Classification of machine paths and the finite enumeration are separate proof obligations. The theorem concerns four physical words and supplies a constant plateau across a change of native word width.
