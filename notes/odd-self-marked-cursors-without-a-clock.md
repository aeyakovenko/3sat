# Odd markers at the read cursor cannot support an unlimited-time SAT solver

In each continuing turn let the scan read $x=M[p]$, mark that very address by $M[p]\gets c$, and set its next read address to $p+x$, $p-x$, or $x-p$ modulo $Q=2^w$. The marker $c$ is a fixed odd program integer. The return address uses the value observed before the write. Arbitrary register comparisons can leave this fixed instruction cycle. A bounded acyclic prologue and exit path are allowed in the five-slot model. **None of the three updates decides ordinary DIMACS SAT, for any amount of time.** The earlier distant-excursion estimate gave a running-time lower bound for these cases. [Five-slot mixed arithmetic](five-instruction-loops-on-dimacs-sat.md)

Use the [proper ASCII parity-pin files](orbit-orders-and-parity-pinned-sat.md) on the infinitely many lengths $N$ divisible by the machine word width. For the stated rule $w=16\lceil D\log_2(N+2)/16\rceil$, each sufficiently large permitted width $16j$ stays constant on an interval of $N$ of exponential length in $j$, which includes a multiple of $16j$. The construction applies at every sufficiently large byte-aligned length. On the chosen lengths the physical words $I=\{0,\ldots,L-1\}$, $L=N/w$, are completely filled with nonzero whitespace or printable ASCII bytes of value at most 127. In particular their high byte is at least 9. Both the word and its negative have circular distance at least $9Q/256$ from zero. All addresses outside $I$ initially contain zero.

Write $\|p\|=\min(p,Q-p)$ and color the low and reflected-low region $J=\{p:\|p\|\le L\}$ one color, its complement the other. Since $L=o(Q)$, every fresh read of an original physical word at $p\in I$ results in a **jump from $J$ to its complement**, for all three opcodes. All other ordinary transitions can be described by a path or a cycle independent of the input.

* For $p+x$ and $p-x$, a previously marked word gives the translation by $c$ or $-c$. A fresh zero outside $I$ gives a self-loop. The undirected graph of the translation is a cycle because $c$ is odd. At most $2|c|$ of its edges cross the color boundary, since $J$ is an interval on the physical residue circle and the translation has displacement at most $|c|$.
* For $x-p$, a fresh zero gives $R_0(p)=-p$ and a marker gives $R_c(p)=c-p$. Make an undirected graph from these two involutions, dropping loops. Its maximum degree is two. It is connected because $R_cR_0$ is translation by $c$, which generates all residues. The vertex zero has degree one, so the graph is a path. Reflection $R_0$ preserves $J$. The image $R_c(p)$ is at distance $|c|$ along the physical circle from $-p$, hence the number of path edges crossing the two colors is again $O(|c|)$. The use of $J$, including the negatives of physical addresses, keeps this bound independent of $L$.

Deleting the bichromatic edges yields $O(1+|c|)$ monochromatic path segments, which are public at the given length. At a moment just after an original physical read and its compulsory write, count visited regular load addresses up to and including that read. The next address, already computed if needed, has not been visited merely by computing it. In every one of these path segments, the visited set is a prefix and a suffix of that segment, with at most one additional interval across all segments for the initial part of the run.

To see this, break the sequence into maximal consecutive visits to a single monochromatic segment. Inside a visit all transitions move to a neighbor or stay put. The visited positions in that segment form an interval. A visit in $J$ starts at a segment endpoint unless it is the initial visit, because special jumps never land in $J$. A visit outside $J$ ends at an endpoint, because special jumps never start there, and the chosen stopping moment is in $I$. Every such interval therefore touches an endpoint except possibly the initial interval. Arbitrarily many intervals touching one endpoint have union a single interval touching it. This reasoning is independent of the number of turns. A bounded preliminary STORE of the same $c$ just adds a publicly described marked exception. More generally finitely many exceptional prologue values can trigger only finitely many extra jumps, since the first regular read of each is followed by the usual mark.

It takes $O_P(w)$ bits to record all these interval endpoints, the initial exceptions, the constant-size register tuple and the control position. At that boundary, each regularly visited word contains the same fixed $c$. Apply the [prefix/suffix splice argument](adaptive-five-slot-prefixes-without-a-clock.md) at the first completed iteration in which the union of the prologue addresses and the regular read addresses covers half the graph-vertex sign words. This last read must be a new original physical read. Every yes input must have all its sensitive words observed before their first overwrite, and a bounded exit suffix cannot supply half the signs. Two yes inputs having the same description induce the same partition into visited and unvisited sign words. Mixing the first input's visited words with the other's remaining words preserves the accepting verdict, because all visited words have been marked or their prologue contents explicitly recorded. The crossing matching fixes $\Omega(k)$ vertex bits in each resulting class, where the family has $k=\Theta(N/\log^2 N)$ independent vertex bits. Only $2^{O_P(w)}$ classes are available. This contradicts correctness at every sufficiently large selected length.

The constant-marker condition matters for both the public path and the cheap recovery of the written values. This proof makes no claim for a STORE at the cursor plus an offset or with a varying payload.

The [finite audit](../audits/odd_marked_cursor_audit.py) checked the endpoint-interval representation at 39,712 fresh physical reads across 15,360 complete finite executions, including both signs of the marker, all three cursor updates and every starting cursor in the tested domains. The [Lean checks](../lean/ColorRunMarks.lean) establish the prefix/suffix union formulas and the underlying composition identity for the two reflections. The geometric description of arbitrary traces is proved above.
