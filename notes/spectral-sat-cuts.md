# How much of the first half of a SAT input must a solver read again?

There are fixed-layout 3-CNFs with two kinds of public pins. A seed specifies a polynomial. The other pins hold a string of $2m$ independent data bits. Put $a=\lceil\log_2(m+2)\rceil$, $g=m/a^2$, and $p=\lfloor m/2-m/a\rfloor$. With probability at least $1-2^{-3m}$ a uniform seed has both properties below, for every sufficiently large $m$. Throughout this note the input length in bits is $N=m\operatorname{polylog}(m)$. No SAT algorithm or workspace hypothesis is needed.

* Every correct deterministic solver reads more than $2m-\lceil g\rceil$ distinct data pins on **every** member of the family. Consequently every run has a genuine cut just after it first learns its $m$th distinct data bit.
* At that cut let $S$ be the set of the $m$ bits already read, and $C$ the actual resumable configuration (controller and writable memory). Under uniform data, for every prescribed pair $(S_0,C_0)$, the probability that $(S,C)=(S_0,C_0)$ **and** that the rest of the run inspects at most $p$ distinct bits of $S$ is at most $2^{-g/4}$. In particular, if a fraction $\lambda>0$ of all runs has this few rereads, the conditional min-entropy of $(S,C)$ on that event is at least $g/4+\log_2\lambda$ bits. The set of positions may depend on the entire genuine prefix. The bound also holds for a catalog of arbitrarily chosen equivalent suffix procedures, paying for its size as below.

This is a cost for information that can affect a SAT verdict under changes of the unread data. The descriptions can replace the solver's memory completely and use unlimited local computation. The two signs are nearly balanced. The bound applies separately after conditioning on either sign, up to division by its probability. There is also a linear-time solver on a padded version of this family. The result puts a constraint on a possible fast solver's read order, retained state and later input reads. It gives no contradiction to an unrestricted linear clock.

## A test allowing reads from both halves and a cut chosen afterward

Fix any balanced partition into $x,y\in\{0,1\}^m$. A native prefix has free access to $x$ and may read up to $r$ distinct bits of $y$. For any cut on its actual path within that allowance, a description may propose a replacement suffix. This suffix sees all $y$ and may inspect up to $p$ distinct $x$ bits. The public seed is free throughout. For a fixed description, truncate the suffix to abstention on exceeding its probe cap or a specified time cap. An unbounded partial computation can equivalently be treated as abstaining on diverging paths. A description is sound at a cut if its asserted answers are correct for **every** $y'$ producing that prefix with the same $x$. It may abstain on any of these continuations. It must assert on the input being counted. Thus hindsight alone cannot pass the true verdict across the cut.

For any catalog of $M$ descriptions, the probability that *some* entry succeeds is at most $M\epsilon$, where $\epsilon=2^{-g/4}$ and $r,p\le\lfloor m/2-m/a\rfloor$. Each entry may have its own balanced partition, fixed relative to the public seed. The bound holds for every such catalog on the same good seeds, including nonuniform catalog choices made after seeing the seed. The event allows the cut and the entry to be selected after the run. The probability is over data for a fixed catalog. If a description may err on admissible continuations, the same per-entry estimate bounds its mass of correct assertions minus its mass of wrong ones on selected whole prefix cylinders; a hindsight assertion of correctness on one input alone has no such guarantee.

Here is the cut argument. Use signs $f(x,y)\in\{1,-1\}$ for the true verdict. For each entry define $A(x,y)$ to be the indicator that some eligible cut along the path admits that entry as sound. For fixed $x$, this has a depth-$r$ bit decision tree in $y$: follow the prefix until halt or just before its $(r+1)$st distinct $y$ read. At each leaf the entire history through that point is fixed, including all candidate cuts and their cylinders. Soundness on a cylinder is a mathematical predicate with no time restriction. Repeated reads cost no extra depth. Let $B(x,y)\in\{-1,0,1\}$ be the truncated suffix answer, with zero for abstention. For fixed $y$ this has a depth-$p$ decision tree in $x$. Wherever $AB$ is nonzero it equals $f$. Therefore $\mathbb E[fAB]$ is the probability that the entry succeeds. Summing the single-entry estimates proves the catalog bound. For the genuine half-read cut take the partition $(S_0,S_0^c)$ and the prefix condition that the actual cut has seen exactly $S_0$ and reached $C_0$. For fixed $x$ either this happens for every $y$ or it happens for none, since the prefix only sees $S_0$. Start the suffix in $C_0$ and use the exact ordinary solver. This even gives $r=0$ in the spectral test.

## A random sign table with few coefficients

Let $L=2^m$, $\mathcal I=\{I\subseteq[m]:|I|\le r\}$, $\mathcal J=\{J\subseteq[m]:|J|\le p\}$ and $D=\max(|\mathcal I|,|\mathcal J|)$. Write $\chi_I(y)=(-1)^{\sum_{i\in I}y_i}$. Form a rectangular real matrix
$$
 K_{(x,I),(y,J)}= f(x,y)\chi_I(y)\chi_J(x).
$$
A decision tree of depth $r$ is a polynomial of degree at most $r$ on Boolean inputs (expand its leaf indicators). Fourier expansion of $A$ in $y$ and $B$ in $x$, followed by Parseval and Cauchy–Schwarz, gives
$$
 |\mathbb E[fAB]|\le \frac{\|K\|}{L}\,\|A\|_2\|B\|_2.
$$
The norms of functions use uniform expectations. Indeed the two coefficient vectors in the bilinear form have Euclidean lengths $\sqrt L\|A\|_2$ and $\sqrt L\|B\|_2$, and its normalization is $L^{-2}$. The bound includes signed predictions and arbitrary bounded functions of the specified slice degrees.

For fully independent signs, write $K=\sum_{x,y}f(x,y)U_{xy}$ with $U_{xy}$ the outer product of the row-block vector $(\chi_I(y))_I$ and the column-block vector $(\chi_J(x))_J$ at the indicated $x,y$. Their squared norms are $|\mathcal I|, |\mathcal J|$. Character orthogonality gives $\sum UU^\top=L|\mathcal J|\,\mathrm{Id}$ and $\sum U^\top U=L|\mathcal I|\,\mathrm{Id}$. Put $d_0=L(|\mathcal I|+|\mathcal J|)$. The rectangular matrix Rademacher tail bound gives $\Pr(\|K\|\ge t)\le d_0\exp(-t^2/(2LD))$. Integrating the tail bounds $\mathbb E\|K\|^{2q}$ by $d_0(2LD)^q q!$. The trace of $(KK^\top)^q$ costs at most one more factor of $d_0$. Its expectation is exactly preserved by $2q$-wise independent unbiased signs, since every monomial has at most $2q$ distinct entries. The trace dominates $\|K\|^{2q}$. Markov's inequality now gives
$$
 \Pr(\|K\|/L>\epsilon)\le d_0^2\left(\frac{2qD}{L\epsilon^2}\right)^q .
$$
This argument applies without change after multiplying the signs by *any fixed truth table* independent of the sampled seed. [Tropp, Theorem 1.5](https://tropp.caltech.edu/papers/Tro11-User-Friendly-preprint.pdf)

Take $q=16a^2$, both radii equal to $p$, and use $D/L\le\exp(-2m/a^2)\le 2^{-2g}$ by the binomial tail. For large $m$, $2q\le2^{g/2}$, so the parenthesized quantity is at most $2^{-g}$. Since $d_0\le2L^2$, the failure probability for one split is at most $2^{4m+2-16m}$. There are at most $2^{2m}$ balanced splits. Smaller probe caps give submatrices. Even a prescribed list of $2^{2m}$ seed-independent truth tables can be included in the union bound. The almost-balanced verdict assertion takes $A=B=1$. A usual query-with-sketch algorithm gives a related one-sided data access model. The bound here allows adaptive probes into the opposite half on both sides, selection among universally sound partial suffixes and arbitrary balanced coordinate splits. [The query-with-sketch model](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol383-ccc2026/html/LIPIcs.CCC.2026.41/LIPIcs.CCC.2026.41.html)

For the every-input claim, fix a coordinate subcube with $d=\lceil g\rceil$ free positions and $v=2^d$ points. Under independent signs, the $2q$th moment of their sum is at most $(2qv)^q$. In the expansion, every nonzero term has its positions covered by a pairing of identical labels. There are at most $(2q)^q$ pairings and at most $v^q$ assignments. The same moment holds with our independence. Monochromaticity has probability at most $(2q/v)^q$. There are at most $3^{2m}$ coordinate subcubes. As $\log_2(2q)=o(g)$, this second union bound is also below $2^{-4m}$ for large $m$. A halting path that left $d$ bits unqueried would be constant on one of these subcubes. This proves that every run crosses the half-read boundary.

## Expenses on an ordinary formula

Take a binary extension field of degree $w=2\cdot3^{\lceil\log_3 m\rceil}$ with $2m\le w<6m$ (allow equality at the lower endpoint). The public polynomial basis modulus is $X^w+X^{w/2}+1$. It is the cyclotomic polynomial of index $3^{1+\lceil\log_3 m\rceil}$ and is irreducible over $\mathbb F_2$. Indeed the order of $2$ modulo $3^{k+1}$ is $2\cdot3^k$, from $v_3(4^{3^j}-1)=j+1$ by cubing; the conjugate orbit of a primitive root then has the full degree. Regard the data bits in their fixed physical order as one field element $u$ by zero extension. Select $2q$ independent uniform field coefficients. The low bit of the degree-less-than-$2q$ polynomial at $u$ supplies the verdict. Distinct inputs are distinct field elements, and Vandermonde interpolation gives the required independence even after projection to one bit.

The seed has $O(m\log^2 m)$ bits. Horner evaluation uses $O(q)$ field multiplications. For a quasilinear bit bound, pack binary polynomial coefficients into integer digits of $\lceil\log_2(w+1)\rceil$ bits, multiply, and take each coefficient modulo two. No digit carries arise. The fixed trinomial reduces a product by a constant number of shifts and XORs of blocks of $w/2$ coefficients, using $X^{2(w/2)}=1+X^{w/2}$. Integer multiplication and the conversions take $w\operatorname{polylog}w$ multitape steps. Encode the resulting deterministic bounded computation with functional local transition gates and a deterministic sorting network for any RAM memory it uses. Pad and pin inputs using the clocked unique-assignment layout. The size in encoded bits is $m\operatorname{polylog}m$. All variant-dependent physical bits are literal signs forcing individual seed or data pins. Space out the data pin clauses with fixed filler so that a packed input word discloses at most one data pin. For any fixed word-width coefficient, gaps of $\Theta(\log^2 m)$ bits suffice for large $m$. All other input words are determined freely from public layout and seed. Use native ordered load/store slots, at most one physical input-word fetch per slot; on a multi-access instruction include the fixed transient registers and subinstruction position in the resumable configuration. One pin for the target sign gives complementary UNIQUE-SAT/UNSAT formulas. [Harvey and van der Hoeven](https://doi.org/10.4007/annals.2021.193.2.4) · [Unique clock encoding](unique-checksum-witnesses.md)

The truth-table multiplier permits another use. Suppose a fixed verifier template has two complementary SAT predicates on $u$, on a fixed layout accessed through individual data pins. A shared formula can guess a branch, check its verifier and connect the corresponding branch bit to the computed polynomial bit (or its opposite). This masks the base truth table. The same cut estimate holds regardless of the seed-independent table, with the template's encoded cost added. If complementary branches have multiple witnesses, uniqueness is not needed. Any additional public encoding that discloses complicated functions of the private bits would have to be charged as probes.

The [native work-read bound](spectral-work-reads.md) also charges adaptive queries that a suffix makes to the true cut memory. The [half-read reuse theorem](half-read-reuse.md) removes the need to prescribe the input order.

The explicit polynomial can itself be evaluated after one full read of the data. A globally correct solver can recognize this family and then compute its verdict in $O(N/\log N)$ time for a suitable fixed polylogarithmic padding. Use an easily checked total clause count and repeat a tautology on an existing variable. Generate and compare the core (including its header, except for the recovered pins), scan the periodic padding word by word, and run the quasilinear evaluation. On any failed recognition test fall back to an ordinary complete SAT algorithm. Choose the padding to dominate all fixed polylogarithmic factors of core generation and field evaluation, as in the paired-formula journal. Collect the data while checking the core, and keep them thereafter in work memory. A single native run can thus satisfy the alternative by retaining information with no additional reads of the input bank. A lower bound from repeated cuts would have to account for the dependence between the information retained at successive cuts.
