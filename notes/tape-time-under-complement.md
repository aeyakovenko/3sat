# Transpose equality separates nondeterministic linear tape time from its complement if one characteristic has no ports

Fix the model of a uniform nondeterministic Turing machine with a fixed finite number of sequential one-dimensional tapes, a finite alphabet, and one head per tape, and two-way sequential access to the input. Let $L$ consist of the binary strings $1^h0AB$, where $h\ge1$, $t=2^h$, $A,B$ are exactly $t\times t$ bits each in row-major order, and $B=A^{\mathsf T}$. Write $n_h=h+1+2t^2$ for their bit length.

**Theorem.** The complement of $L$ is in nondeterministic linear time. If the spectral wire metrics of **any one characteristic** have no ports, then every sound nondeterministic recognizer accepting all of $L$ has a member at each sufficiently large $n_h$ on which its shortest accepting run takes $\Omega(n_h\sqrt{\log n_h})$ steps. In particular $\mathrm{NTIME}_{\rm tape}(n)\ne\mathrm{coNTIME}_{\rm tape}(n)$ under this hypothesis.

Consequently at least one of the following holds.

1. Some fixed $0<\delta<1$ permits deterministic 3-SAT in $2^{O(v^{1-\delta})}\operatorname{poly}(m)$ bit operations for $v$ variables and encoded length $m$.
2. The explicit language $L$ witnesses the linear-time complement separation above, with the stated lower bound.

Indeed the November tape preprint proves that ports in characteristic zero imply the first conclusion. If characteristic zero lacks ports, the theorem here gives the second. An affirmative instance of the first conclusion is compatible with the second. The only case left uncovered by the geometric proof of the second is ports in **every** characteristic. The statement about the 3-SAT algorithm uses characteristic zero specifically. There is also a counting version of the first alternative. In the upstream characteristic-zero proof, each leaf contraction already returns its exact model count. To make the leaves disjoint, carry a set of deferred unit constraints along the same sparsification tree. Whenever a branch enforces all petals, record that **every literal of the center must be false**. The other branch already enforces the center. Ignore the deferred constraints when selecting subsequent branches, and intersect them with the leaf formula when contracting it. Each model now follows exactly one path, determined by the center tests. There are at most $2v$ distinct deferred units per leaf, including possibly conflicting ones. They preserve the volume bound and the original number of leaves. Summing the disjoint counts gives an exact #3-SAT algorithm in the stated time if characteristic zero has ports. This uses the disjoint version of sunflower branching. ## The easy side on the full binary language

A malformed header or a length other than $n_h$ is grounds for acceptance by the complement machine. Count the payload in binary and compare it with $2^{2h+1}$, whose representation takes $O(h+1)$ cells. Binary counting with the head returning to the low bit costs amortized constant time per increment. On a well-formed length, guess $h$-bit row and column indices $i,j$ and compare bit $ij$ of $A$ with bit $ji$ of $B$. The two zero-based offsets in the payload have binary forms $i\Vert j$ and $1\Vert j\Vert i$, with leading zeroes in the fixed-width index fields. Two countdown tapes, initialized from these offsets, locate the bits in one sequential payload scan. The scan can check its length at the same time. Over $k$ decrements a countdown needs $O(k+h+1)$ head moves, by summing the lengths of the borrow chains. A mismatch accepts. All branches terminate in $O(n)$, including on malformed inputs with a large unary header.

## Removing cancellation at the cost of one bit of density per bundle

Consider a finite network of local zero-one *feasibility* tables. Each internal bundle $e$ carries a label $s$ from an arbitrary finite alphabet. Let $E$ be the number of these bundles. For one fixed external input $x$, write $H_x$ for the set of complete internal labelings that satisfy every table. Each tuple at an epoch records the **existence** of an internal path consistent with its boundaries. Multiple internal paths realizing the same tuple contribute one. Consistent tuples glue across epochs to a path of the original machine.

For each possible label introduce a separate Boolean filter $u_{e,s}$. At one endpoint of each bundle, multiply the local entry by the filter for the label presented. For a fixed input the contraction over any field is
$$
 Q_x(u)=\sum_{\lambda\in H_x}\prod_e u_{e,\lambda_e}.
$$
Distinct labelings yield distinct squarefree monomials of degree $E$, each with coefficient one. If $H_x$ is nonempty, fix one labeling and arbitrarily assign all variables outside its $E$ factors. The coefficient of their full product stays one, since every other monomial omits one of them. A multilinear polynomial with that coefficient cannot vanish on the whole Boolean cube over any field. Iteratively subtract its two restrictions in each coordinate to recover the coefficient. Thus independent fair filter bits leave $Q_x$ nonzero with probability at least $2^{-E}$. If $H_x$ is empty, every evaluation is zero.

Averaging fixes **one** filter for all inputs that preserves at least a $2^{-E}$ fraction of any specified family of supported inputs. The modified local tables still have entries in $\{0,1\}$, with identical geometry. The unfiltered table can count two histories as zero in characteristic two. This explains both the need for the filter and why charging the bundle width for each filter would give the wrong bound. The number of local table entries can be exponential in the width. The upstream vertex estimate permits arbitrary tables at its bounded-degree vertices. The polynomial fact also follows from the Boolean-grid nonvanishing bound. [Alon–Füredi grid bound and extensions](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.06020)

## Short accepting runs yield a partial transpose

Put $V=t^2$ and use epochs and tape blocks of $b=t$ cells. Suppose every equal pair has an accepting path within $T$ steps. Pad an accepting halt with a stationary transition, and select one path for each equal pair. An epoch can move each head by at most one block index. Thus there are $2^{O_M(1+T/b)}$ head records from the fixed initial positions. One fixed record covers at least a $2^{-O_M(1+T/b)}$ fraction of the $2^V$ possible $A$.

For this record, give each epoch a table checking the state, offsets, and contents of the constantly many blocks that its heads can reach. Pass each block's contents to its next possible visit. Pad the final epoch only to the chosen deadline, or round the deadline to a multiple of $b$ while retaining the absorbing halt. Each row of either array occupies $b$ contiguous cells and intersects at most two input blocks. Supply the fixed header and blank cells locally and leave separate row terminals for each of the two arrays. As in the recorded-head reduction there are $P=O_M(t+T/b)$ vertices, bounded degree, maximum bundle width $O_M(b)$, and hence $E=O_M(t+T/b)$ internal bundles. All feasible boundary labelings for a given external pair glue to genuine accepting paths. Soundness makes this set empty on every off-diagonal input. Apply the filter lemma to this record in the chosen field. For $T\le V\sqrt{\log t}$ the two density losses together are $2^{-O_M(t+T/b)}=2^{-o(V)}$. After pinning any retained $A$ the output is a nonzero scalar multiple of the basis vector for $A^{\mathsf T}$. Thus we meet the partial-transposition threshold $2^{-\rho V}$ for any fixed $0<\rho<1-1/\sqrt2$ at all sufficiently large $t$.

Here is why the partial-transposition proof applies over the chosen field $F$. The published tape theorem is stated over $\mathbb Q$. Its short-edge placement uses the logarithmic Poincaré obstruction for **any** class of Hadamard spaces without ports, applied to the compatible spectral spaces over $F$. Every tuple appears once, with the same scalar $c_*=\prod_w c(w)^{r p(w)}\ne0$. Multiplication by $c_*^{-1}$ gives each coefficient one over $F$. The rational probabilities used to choose the type are ordinary real probabilities, independent of the characteristic of the tables. The metric and Shannon entropy estimates are unchanged. The companion paper defines ports for each field and proves dependence only on its characteristic. This gives $P\ge c_{M,\rho}t\sqrt{\log t}$ on the filtered record. ## Related results and scope

Paul, Pippenger, Szemerédi and Trotter separated deterministic and nondeterministic linear time on multitape machines. Maass, Schnitger, Szemerédi and Turán considered a sparse transpose language, proved a nondeterministic lower bound with **one** work tape and gave a linear-time algorithm on two. Li, Longpré and Vitányi proved a complement separation for nondeterministic linear time on a **single queue**. The present corollary concerns the union over all fixed finite tape counts and uses an upstream geometric hypothesis. Priority for this specific conditional corollary has not been established.
