# A linear-size 3-SAT reduction with a private model for every clause

Every proper explicit 3-SAT file has a satisfiability-equivalent proper 3-CNF with $q$ clauses and $O(n)$ encoded bits, constructible on the word RAM in $O(n)$ time. Here $n$ is the source bit length. For **each** output clause $D$, a supplied rule gives an assignment falsifying exactly $D$, on positive and negative files alike. After linear preprocessing, an arbitrary packed word of any one of these $q$ assignments takes constant word time. In particular every negative output is minimally unsatisfiable. This family preserves the unrestricted deterministic linear, quadratic and cubic SAT thresholds even if a program receives random word access to the entire table of neighboring models.

Papadimitriou and Wolfe reduced 3-UNSAT to minimal unsatisfiability. Burjons, Frei, Hemaspaandra, Komm and Wehner's Theorem 2 supplies models after every clause deletion in proper 3-CNF. Their displayed first-stage construction in §2.2.1 has a clause per pair of selectors and disjunctions listing all selectors except one. The tree below gives the neighbor guarantee with linearly many constant-width clauses and constant-time indexed access to each model bit. Priority for those quantitative bounds has not been established. [Burjons et al., Theorem 2 and §2.2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00453-023-01204-1)

## Messages on a binary tree

Compact names and remove tautologies, repeated literals and duplicate clauses. The remaining clauses have one to three distinct variables. An empty source clause can be handled by the fixed contradiction $(x),(\neg x)$. Put all-positive clauses in one group and all clauses with a negative literal in another. All ones satisfies the first group, and all zeros satisfies the second. Add a positive and a negative unit on two distinct fresh names, one per group, so both groups are nonempty. They preserve satisfiability. Make a rooted full binary tree with one leaf per clause. The two children of the root subtend the two groups. Bisect each group recursively so the tree has logarithmic height. A group of size one uses its leaf directly.

For each tree edge introduce two distinct Boolean variables $m_{uv},m_{vu}$ for its two directions. At leaf $i$ put $y_i=m_{i,p(i)}$ and $p_i=m_{p(i),i}$. For its clause $C_i$ add the following rows, one $B$ for each literal:
$$
       A_i=C_i\lor p_i,\qquad
       B_{i,\ell}=\neg y_i\lor p_i\lor\neg\ell\quad(\ell\in C_i).
$$
At each internal node $u$ with children $l,r$ add
$$
       H_u=\neg m_{lu}\lor\neg m_{ru}.
$$
For every incident neighbor $v$ of this internal node add its relay row
$$
       R_{uv}=\neg m_{uv}\ \lor\!
                \bigvee_{w\sim u,\ w\ne v} m_{wu}.
$$
The rows have width two to four. Suppose an intact subtree receives no signal from its parent. Inductively it sends no upward signal and every leaf payload in it holds. At a leaf, $A_i$ requires its payload; if it emitted, its $B$ rows would falsify every literal. At an internal node, if its left child sends up, the sibling exclusion forces right-up false. The relay into the left child then has no possible support. By induction that child cannot send up, a contradiction. With left-up false the right child also has no possible downward input, so it sends no upward signal and its payloads hold. The same now follows for the left child. The defining upward relay has no support either. At the root the parent input is absent; equivalently take it false along with a dummy false root output. Thus all messages vanish and all clauses hold. Conversely satisfying payloads extend with all messages zero. [Lean proof of the subtree inference](../lean/PrivateClauseTree.lean)

## Making each row the unique failure

A broadcast from a leaf sets exactly the arcs directed away from that leaf to one. A signal starting at a directed edge $u\to v$ sets that arc and continues outwards into the component of $v$ after deleting $\{u,v\}$. In unions of such signals take the Boolean OR.

| Sole failed row | Assignment |
|---|---|
| $A_i$ | Broadcast from $i$. Falsify all literals of $C_i$. Every other leaf receives a signal. |
| $B_{i,\ell}$ | Broadcast from $i$. Make exactly $\ell$ true in $C_i$. |
| $H_u$ | Broadcast from one leaf in each child subtree of $u$. Every leaf receives, including both emitters. The only node receiving upward signals from both children is $u$. |
| $R_{uv}$, where $v$ is a child of $u$ | Start a signal at $u\to v$ and broadcast from one leaf in the subtree of $v$. Every leaf receives. Only the real leaf sends upward signals. At $u$ its signal arrives from $v$ and cannot support $m_{uv}$. |
| $R_{uv}$, where $v$ is the parent of $u$ | Start just the signal at $u\to v$. It reaches every leaf outside the subtree of $u$. The leaves inside it all belong to the same root group, so assign its known all-one or all-zero model. The upward signals follow a single path. |

For the protected root groups the two fresh units have the stated signs. In the first two cases the literal assignments exist since the leaf variables are distinct. All unspecified payload bits can be zero.

For a four-literal $A_i$, order the incoming signal first, followed by its three payload literals. Replace a binary row $a\lor b$ by its two ternary copies with opposite signs on a fresh bit $z$. Replace a four-literal row by $(a\lor b\lor z),(\neg z\lor c\lor e)$ with a fresh $z$. If a raw row holds, use $z=0$ for its binary padding and $z=\neg(a\lor b)$ for its four-literal split. In the private model of a raw row all its literals are false. For either of its two physical clauses choose $z$ so that exactly that clause fails. Ternary rows pass through unchanged. Every resulting clause has three different variables, and the private models exclude duplicate clauses.

## Addresses and actual executed steps

The normalized source has $m\log(m+1)=O(n)$ for $m$ different remaining clauses by fixed-alphabet counting. The tree, its directed variables, and all local padding use $O(m+1)$ rows and names. Tries, dense indexing and printing therefore cost $O(n)$ native steps and bits, including files with long spellings or many duplicate clauses. [Native normalization](two-permutations-and-the-explicit-sat-clock.md)

Precompute the interval of leaves below each node in depth-first order and one representative leaf per subtree. The raw private model has at most two real sources, one directed signal start, and a constant default payload bit with at most three exceptions. A real source contributes to $m_{ab}$ exactly when it is on the $a$ side of $\{a,b\}$. A signal start $u\to v$ contributes on its own arc and on arcs inside the $v$ side directed away from $u$. These are constant numbers of interval tests. The private padding bit for any other physical row uses at most two raw literal queries by the rules above. Its override at the designated deleted clause is stored in that clause's descriptor. This implements any one bit query indexed by the deleted clause and the variable in $O(1)$ RAM steps after $O(n)$ preprocessing. It also implements the returned bit for a sign-change repair using the same private assignment. The table can have quadratically many entries; only queried bits are evaluated.

#### Packed access to all neighboring models

Order the payload names first. Allocate two contiguous banks of message variables, upwards and downwards, in preorder of the child endpoint in each bank. Allocate binary padding separately, and order the four-literal padding bits by their leaf position. The indices of the edges entering or lying below a given subtree form one preorder interval, and the four-literal leaves of that subtree form one interval in their own bank.

Each private assignment has a base packed vector evaluable at any one word by a constant number of masks, plus $O(\log(q+2))$ bit exceptions. The payload base is its default constant and has at most three exceptions. For messages take the base all-downwards and no upwards. A broadcast from an actual leaf changes orientations only on its route to the root. Two broadcasts have exceptions only on the union of their routes. For the downward relay fault the counterfeit signal is entirely downwards, so only the real leaf's route can deviate. For an upward relay fault $u\to v$, turn off the entire interval of downward arcs entering or below $u$ in the base. The remaining exceptional arcs all lie on the route from $u$ to the root.

Binary padding has base zero. A four-literal leaf pad is $z=\neg(p_i\lor\ell_{i,1})$ under the honest extension. With actual leaf sources, all but at most one leaf receive a signal, so there is at most one exceptional four-literal pad besides the deleted row's own override. With just the upward counterfeit, $z$ is zero outside the subtree of $u$; inside it the payload is constant. There it is one of two fixed packed arrays of first-literal signs, masked to that one subtree interval. The deleted row's own padding bit can be overridden in any case.

In $O(\log(q+2))$ steps per deletion, enumerate the indicated paths and the constant number of payload or pad exceptions. Use the bit evaluator above at those locations and set the corresponding differing bits in XOR masks. With initially zero work RAM, a mask for a pair (deletion, aligned word offset) may be stored directly at a reserved polynomial address, writing only the touched words. A power-of-two stride suffices; choose a fixed width coefficient large enough for this address range, as in the journal's unrestricted model. The total work is $O(q\log(q+2))=O(n)$. A packed read takes the base word XOR its mask. An unaligned read or a read crossing a row boundary uses a constant number of words. This simulates a read-only table, including on positive centers, without scanning a deleted neighbor's assignment. If the ASCII edit convention below reserves unused names, place zeros at those positions in the packed bases and remap exception addresses through $j\mapsto10j+2$.

#### Mandatory reads on plain SAT files

In ordinary ASCII DIMACS one can arrange **three word-disjoint one-bit positive neighbors per physical clause on every negative center**. Give variable $j$ the decimal name $10j+2$, reserve $10j+3$ without using it, and set the header range to include all of them. Flipping the low ASCII bit of the final digit of one occurrence changes just that occurrence to a fresh name. Extend the clause's private model by setting that fresh bit to satisfy the changed literal. The number of clauses and length are the same. Put enough whitespace between consecutive tokens that the designated bytes in different literal occurrences are farther apart than a word length. For the fixed RAM word-width coefficient, $O(\log(q+2))$ spaces per literal suffice and the length is $N=\Theta(q\log(q+2))$ bits. This ordinary DIMACS version has unused identifiers. With dense identifiers, reserve one position for each sign and toggle that byte between a space and a minus sign. The private model satisfies the changed clause in this version as well. If the DIMACS grammar permits a carriage return as token whitespace, the dense version may use that byte (0x0d) versus minus (0x2d) for a one-bit change. In a binary format with an explicit sign bit the same operation flips one bit. [ASCII grammar convention](one-edit-cubic-sat-reduction.md)

In the ordinary file-only RAM model, every deterministic solver correct on the center and these edits must observe all $3q$ designated original words on the negative run. A word overwritten before being read cannot distinguish its edited input. Until the first observation of the changed word the two executions agree, including loop counters, computed addresses and writes to workspace. Ordering the sites by first observation on the negative run also proves that the sum of the step counts on its $3q$ affirmative neighbors is at least $3q(3q+1)/2$ in the single-original-read instruction model. A literal edit preserves the file length. These are executed-step lower bounds independent of stored program size.

On this family a program which soundly rejects every negative within $C N^j$ steps gives unrestricted 3-SAT in $O(n^j)$ for $j=1,2,3$. Generate the center, simulate up to that deadline, and answer YES on timeout. The converse uses an ordinary SAT decider. On the center promise the same clock implication holds with the read-only packed table above, by simulating its oracle reads in constant time. The mandatory-observation argument uses plain files: an extra oracle whose contents also change at an edit could reveal the edit independently of any file observation.

The one-clause successor replaces $(a\lor b\lor c)$ by $(a\lor b\lor z)$ and $(\neg z\lor b\lor c)$. The old private model for this clause makes $a,b,c$ false and either of the two new rows can be made the unique failure. All other private models extend by taking $z=\neg a$. This increases the compulsory observations on negative centers from $3q$ to $3(q+1)$ after printing the next file. The ratio to encoded length remains of order $1/\log q$ for this argument. No property of the construction currently forces repeated computational work after the original word observations. [Existing one-clause lift and Lean proof](minimal-sat-cores-under-one-more-clause.md)

### Undoing every split without its history

There is one greedy simplifier for all seeds, including seeds of unbounded primal treewidth. Work on proper ternary clause **multisets** and keep multiplicities during simplification. Eliminate a variable if it appears just twice, in opposite signs, and its two clauses have the form $(a,b,z),(\neg z,b,c)$ with the three variables of $a,b,c$ distinct. Replace them by $(a,b,c)$. The result preserves SAT by existential projection. The terminal multiset is unique up to permutation and consistent complementation of variable names.

To see uniqueness, compare any two eligible pivots. If their two pairs of clause occurrences are disjoint, the two reductions commute, even if the resulting rows are equal. Sharing both clauses would put the second pivot in both residual literal pairs, contrary to the proper three-variable resolvent. If they share one clause, write it as $(x,y,b)$, choosing the signs of $x,y$ here. The other two clauses must be $(\neg x,b,a)$ and $(\neg y,b,c)$: $x,y$ cannot occur a third time, so $b$ is the shared literal for both reductions. Resolving $x$ leaves $(y,b,a),(\neg y,b,c)$. Resolving $y$ leaves $(x,b,c),(\neg x,b,a)$. The renaming $y\mapsto\neg x$ identifies the results, fixes all outside rows, and works even if $a,c$ are equal or opposite. Each step reduces the row count. Induction on that count, on signed-isomorphism classes, gives uniqueness. Confluence results for 1-singular Davis–Putnam reduction on minimally unsatisfiable clause-sets precede this argument. Here the local rule is exactly the inverse of the stated proper split on arbitrary ternary clause multisets. [Kullmann and Zhao](https://arxiv.org/abs/1202.2600) · [Abbasizanjani and Kullmann](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.03639) · [Lean proof of the overlapping reduction and the termination argument](../lean/SplitOverlapDiamond.lean)

Maintain incidence lists and a queue of candidate variables. A reduction changes two fixed-width rows into one. Recheck the bounded number of variables in those rows, including any whose occurrence count stayed the same but whose incident clause changed. The queue uses $O(q)$ operations after parsing. Following the original split history backwards is one permitted reduction sequence. Hence greedy simplification on any signed renaming or reordering of a descendant yields the irreducible form of the seed, up to signed isomorphism. For a reducible seed this claim identifies its normal form. For arbitrary huge identifiers the trie preprocessing costs $O(N)$. On the word-separated files with polynomially bounded names and $N=\Theta(q\log q)$, packed token parsing and a constant number of radix-sort passes take $O(q)$ native work. A fixed solver can fall back to the trie for longer spellings. [Packed token bound](scan-time-on-the-dense-list-projection.md#the-physical-cost-of-body-parsing)

After simplification, densely renumber the remaining names and deduplicate the core in linear row work. If it has $v$ variables, at most $O(v^3)$ proper distinct clauses remain. Any complete SAT algorithm on that core gives a globally correct solver on the original file. For example, for each fixed $0<\eta<1/\log_2(4/3)$, one fixed solver uses $O(q)$ time on all such compact descendants with $v\le (1/\log_2(4/3)-\eta)\log_2 q$, irrespective of their seed widths. Choose the fixed $\epsilon>0$ in the deterministic $(4/3+\epsilon)^v\operatorname{poly}(v)$ 3-SAT algorithm sufficiently small that the exponential term is at most $q^{1-\delta}$ for some $\delta>0$. Compact core serialization and the polynomial factor fit the remaining margin. This includes arbitrary split histories from every finite seed and matches the order of the plain-file probe bound on their negative centers. For arbitrary unrelated sign edits that cease to be split descendants, the existing bounded-treewidth result gives a separate fast path. [Moser and Scheder](https://arxiv.org/abs/1008.4067) · [Treewidth of clause-splitting histories](width-of-clause-splitting-histories.md)

A bounded-occurrence variant first gives every original literal occurrence its own copy, keeping its sign. For each original variable with at least two copies, link them by a cycle of implications, which forces equality. The tree construction now has **at most seven physical occurrences per variable**. A payload copy appears in one original leaf and at most two implication leaves. Its original leaf uses it at most three times including possible binary padding; an implication leaf uses it twice. A leaf's outgoing signal occurs at most three times in its blockers, twice in adjacent relays and twice in a padded sibling exclusion. A leaf's incoming signal occurs in its own defining relay at most twice and in at most four physical leaf clauses. An internal signal occurs at most twice in its defining relay, twice as support and twice in one sibling exclusion. The fresh properization bit occurs twice. The copy variant preserves the encoded size, native clocks and indexed model access. Its extra equality wiring need not preserve a bit projection for a variable-name-dependent source layout.

## Preservation of algebraic and circuit results

For any encoded source CNF, set all tree messages to zero and the two fresh source-unit variables to their satisfying constants. For each split or padding use its honest bit just given. Each output variable is now a Boolean polynomial of constant degree in a bounded number of original variables. A substituted violation indicator vanishes identically on the Boolean cube, or only occurs when its identified source clause fails. Point-indicator interpolation on those bits derives it in bounded degree from Boolean axioms and the appropriate source violation. Consequently a degree-$D$ polynomial-calculus or real sum-of-squares refutation pulls back to one of degree $O(D+1)$, with no field dependence for PC. Apply the construction to the witness-dependent-charge files. Every negative then has these private models, the native probe lower bound, PC degree $\Omega(N/\log N)$ and monomial size $2^{\Omega(N/\log N)}$ over all fields, and real SoS degree $\Omega(N/\log N)$. This uses explicit proper source clauses with their clause-violation polynomials. [Every-field charge reduction](witness-dependent-charges-and-the-negative-sat-clock.md)

The single-clause successor itself has a literal-only pullback $z=\neg a$: the first child is tautological and the second recovers the parent. After arbitrarily many such splits every substituted variable is a literal of the original system. Each child violation becomes an original violation or a degree-at-most-three multiple of a Boolean axiom. The source proof degree therefore persists up to an absolute additive constant independent of the number of splits. A bound linear in the *new* number of clauses also requires controlling the growth from the original size.

On the canonical list inputs of the charge reduction the signs, clause positions and dense variable range are fixed at each parameter. Keep their public ordering and names when building the clause tree, and number auxiliary wires consecutively above the public range. These inputs can be selected by their established $O(n)$ syntax check within the general compiler. The root partition and all properization choices are then fixed. Every occurrence of a data digit remains a copy. Decimal relabeling by $10j+2$ appends one fixed digit. Thus this restriction also retains the imported $\Omega(N(\log N)^\sigma)$ Boolean decision-circuit bound. Its separately known linear native recognizer is consistent with that bound. [List projection and source theorem](checking-the-sat-circuit-bound.md)
