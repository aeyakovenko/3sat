# Can an optimal score replace the arithmetic self-accelerator?

## The fixed point and the missing speedup

In the contracting case of the VP–VNP preprint, Theorem J.4 cuts a long weighted computation into a sum of products of values of shorter pieces, under the paper's contracting hypothesis. Theorem K.6 constructs a self-application with value V=1+V for a proposed accelerator. The sum is over accepting runs in a ring. With ordinary Boolean acceptance the same two branches give V=true OR V, which has the solution true. Replacing the outer aggregation by maximum and combining scores along a path by addition gives a different diagonal. If an accelerator reproduces the maximum integer reward of every designated clocked program and fits inside the self-application clock, give the self-application a baseline branch of score zero and an accelerator branch with bonus one. Its finite score must then satisfy V=max(0,V+1), which is impossible. This semantic argument assumes the accelerator really returns the optimum as a maximum over its runs with the stated clock and that the wrapper can increment the returned score. [Preprint, §2.4 and Appendices J–K](https://hexagonmath.org/pdf/2611.00954v1)

**Try to obtain the score using SAT.** For a polynomially bounded run, the question “can a run earn at least j?” has an existential computation certificate. A useful subtlety appears if scores live in {0,…,B} for B≥1, and all thresholds up to the true score may be certified. Guessing a successful threshold j and returning j+1 with wraparound does not yield a contradiction. At true optimum B, every j succeeds and j=B−1 returns B. Saturating the successor at B has the same defect. [Krentel on optimum values](https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-0000(88)90039-6)

Two deterministic threshold answers repair the diagonal. Define the optimum to be zero if there is no accepting run. Treat the threshold zero as true and B+1 as false. For each other j the threshold predicate is existential. Guess j, test that threshold j is true and j+1 false, and output (j+1) modulo B+1 on this branch. Exactly the optimum j passes, including at the endpoints. On self-application this gives a cyclic successor without a fixed point.

For an explicit budget, take a clock T(n)=nᵃ with fixed a>1, B polynomial in n, a uniform compiler for threshold queries of clocked nondeterministic programs p on x, and a hypothetical deterministic SAT machine with clock O(mᵈ) on m encoded bits. Suppose the compiler runs in G(n) time and writes at most M(n) bits on input (p,x,T(n),j), for each fixed p and all x of length n. A fixed diagonal program reads a self-delimiting target program p from its input and applies the two tests to that program on this same input. On inputs padded after its own code, the target program is the diagonal program. If n+G(n)+M(n)ᵈ ≤ T(n)^(1−δ) for fixed δ>0 at all sufficiently large n, the two tests and their fixed interpretation overhead fit inside the clock, and yield the contradiction above. Program codes and clock bounds here use the usual fixed finite instruction set with polylogarithmic overhead for fixed-program simulation. The case B=1 already recovers the ordinary decision diagonal. The construction singles out the required compression quantitatively. A generic explicit tableau writes a number of entries proportional to T and does not supply that bound, even at d=1.

## Test the proposed max-plus cut on an ordered tape layout

Consider a candidate replacement for a signed sum of decomposable terms. A header selects a balanced partition of time blocks. One part computes an arbitrary real score from its own data, the other does likewise, and their scores are added. Accept if some header reaches a threshold. The partition and the two score functions may all depend on the header. There are **four order-preserving streams** for which even exponentially many such choices can be necessary.

Let K=2ᵏ, k≥1. On vertices 0 through K−1 use the 2K directed arcs u→(2u+b mod K), b∈{0,1}. Sort the vertices in numeric order. Classify an arc by the highest bit of u and the appended bit b. In each of the four classes the source and destination orders agree, with no repeated endpoint on either side of that stream. Thus these streams satisfy the packet order in the preprint’s two-sided tape-automaton layout, which permits a read occurrence before its matched write. This is a statement about block incidence. Queue machines that require every write to precede its read have an extra scheduling constraint. The graph is the binary de Bruijn digraph. [Bisection widths of de Bruijn graphs](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0166-218X(98)00031-6)

Every vertex cut S of size between K/3 and 2K/3 crosses at least 8K/(9k) directed arcs, counting both orientations. Start at a uniform vertex and append k independent fair bits successively. The endpoint is independent of the start. Their membership in S differs with probability at least 4/9. At each step the directed arc traversed is uniform among 2K arcs. The union bound gives the claimed inequality. Exhaustive bisection enumeration at K=2,4,8,16 gave minimum numbers of crossing arcs 2,4,4,6 respectively.

At the two endpoints of each arc e place separate strings aₑ and bₑ of L≥1 bits. The total input length is N=4KL. A vertex owns its endpoint data, including both incidences of a loop if present. Take the desired bit to be

    f = XOR over directed arcs e of the binary inner product of aₑ and bₑ.

A word RAM can compute f in O(N) steps by addressing the two endpoints of each arc. On the stipulated four streams, a nondeterministic tape automaton can also guess the bits arriving from other blocks, match them to the sent copies, and keep a running parity. Fix either desired verdict h.

**Claim.** Every exact representation of [f=h] as an OR of threshold terms U(x from S)+V(x outside S) ≥ λ, with a separately chosen balanced S for each term, needs at least

    2^{8KL/(9k)} / [4(1+N ln 2)]

terms. Thresholds and local functions can use unbounded precision. This rules out headers of O(N^{1−ε}) bits on this interface for any fixed ε>0, already at L=1.

For one cut let t be L times the number of crossing arcs. Across its two parties the sign matrix (−1)^f is a 2ᵗ-order Walsh–Hadamard matrix with replicated rows and columns, up to local row and column signs. Indeed every crossing pair uses two fresh bits, and every internal inner product belongs to one party. The squared operator norm is 2^{N−t}, by orthogonality of the Hadamard rows and the numbers of copies. On any monochromatic rectangle A×B the sum of signs has magnitude |A|·|B|. Bounding the same sum by the operator norm times √(|A|·|B|) gives |A|·|B| ≤ 2^{N−t}.

The threshold term can exceed that rectangle size. Order its rows by decreasing U. If the ith row accepts dᵢ columns, the first i rows all accept those columns. Correctness of the OR makes this an h-monochromatic rectangle, so i·dᵢ ≤ 2^{N−t}. Summing over the rows bounds the area of the term by (1+N ln 2)·2^{N−t}. The harmonic factor has content. For the single t-bit inner product, accept when the last set position in the left string is strictly before the first set position in the right string (sentinels 0 and t+1). This sum-of-local-scores threshold covers 2ᵗ+t2^{t−1} zero inputs, while every zero rectangle covers at most 2ᵗ. Finally both verdicts of f occur on at least a quarter of the whole cube, since the expectation of (−1)^f is 2^{−2KL}. Use the area bound for each term, substituting the crossing lower bound that holds for its own partition. This proves the claim. Balanced rectangle covers and inner-product communication bounds have extensive precedent. The calculation here charges scalar additive summaries and ordered streams explicitly. [Bova, Capelli, Mengel and Slivovsky](https://www.ijcai.org/Proceedings/16/Papers/147.pdf)

The partition may even depend on q adaptive global bit probes made after reading the header. For a fixed header, condition on a complete probe transcript. Every unqueried bit remains uniform, and at most q of the t crossing bit-pairs have a fixed endpoint. The others give t−q or more independent Hadamard factors. Apply the preceding bound conditionally and average over transcripts for that header. A header length H consequently satisfies H+q ≥ 8KL/(9k)−O(log N). Word probes can be charged their full bit width here.

## Which step survives?

Maximization admits a strict-progress diagonal and a two-threshold SAT test. A direct reuse of the paper’s ring rerouting law as a maximum identity has no justification. Even the formal identity X=(X+1)−1 can have cancellation between the two constant terms. Giving generic valuations to the original variable preserves both constant exponents, so isolation on original variables need not prevent cancellation in a decomposition. The ordered-stream example shows why a new exact, positive two-piece cut of the stated form is also insufficient. It permits either polarity of the final verdict.

**Attack the obstruction by checking several partitions.** There is a compact certificate interface for the very same parity example. Color the nonloop arcs, treating orientations as distinct edges, so each color is an undirected matching. At most seven colors suffice by greedy coloring, since at either endpoint of an arc at most three other arcs meet it. Handle the loops in one additional color. For each matching separately choose a half-half vertex partition preserving every matched pair. This can be done for K divisible by four: group as many complete pairs as fit on the first side, filling unused slots with unmatched vertices. Assign the other vertices to the second side. Give the loop color any such partition.

Guess two bits per color. For each color and each side, check that the corresponding bit equals the parity of that side's local edge products of this color. Check that the XOR of all guessed bits is the desired verdict. All checks together are exact with at most sixteen shared bits, even though the one-partition-per-term lower bound grows almost linearly with N in its logarithm. The change is allowing a conjunction of tests with different partitions under one guess. This exposes a more specific possible route through the cut problem. Across the whole verification these checks still process all explicit edge data. For a succinct computation one must also certify that the records inspected under different partitions came from the same run. No such efficient shared consistency test has been derived.

The example lets all local input records vary independently and fixes the information visible to each party once its header is selected. The hierarchy input can be much shorter than the run. Moreover a real cut might compare bits adaptively across both parts or certify agreement in another quantifier round. The claim does not cover those access patterns. To rule out even linear-time SAT by this route, the missing construction is an exact threshold compiler saving a factor in the clock on the succinct, self-referential computations. Neither the score diagonal nor the stream bound supplies that compiler.

## Does a constant number of overlapping checks always suffice?

For each fixed r≥2 the following explicit Boolean function attacks that repair in a general block model. For every ordered r-tuple e of K block indices, give position j its own bit xₑⱼ in block eⱼ. Even when two positions name the same block their bits are distinct. There are N=rKʳ bits. Let f be the parity of the Kʳ products ∏ⱼ xₑⱼ. A RAM can evaluate it in Oᵣ(N) time by visiting the r positions of each tuple. A candidate certificate is a header of h bits followed by at most r Boolean checks. Each check sees the entire header and all data in a specified set of at most 2K/3 blocks. Its scope can vary with the header. All checks must pass. For either desired verdict, soundness and completeness require h=Ωᵣ(N).

Fix one header, pad with constant-true checks if needed, and write Uⱼ for the blocks visible to check j. Keep the t=∏ⱼ(K−|Uⱼ|) tuples with eⱼ outside Uⱼ for every j, so t≥(K/3)ʳ. Fix all bits outside those tuples arbitrarily. For each retained tuple group the jth bits into the jth party's string. Check j misses that entire string. The restriction of f is a fixed bit XOR the generalized inner product of the r independent t-bit strings. The passing set is a cylinder intersection in the number-on-the-forehead model. The generalized inner product discrepancy bound is a classical multiparty communication argument. [Babai, Nisan and Szegedy](https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-0000(92)90047-M)

For the bound used here write the sign of the generalized inner product as F and the checks as indicators gⱼ. Apply Cauchy–Schwarz successively r−1 times to |E[F∏gⱼ]|. To eliminate gⱼ, condition on all strings other than string j, bound gⱼ by one, square the inner expectation by introducing two independent copies of string j, and proceed to the next coordinate. After these steps gᵣ is independent of the last string and can be bounded by one outside its inner average. The resulting bound on |E[F∏gⱼ]|^{2^{r−1}} is the expectation of the absolute inner average of the product of the 2^{r−1} copies of F. That product is a character on the last string. At each position its coefficient is the product of the r−1 independent binary differences between paired copies. Each coefficient is one with probability 2^{1−r}, independently over positions. The character average has magnitude one precisely when all coefficients vanish, giving the bound (1−2^{1−r})ᵗ.

All passing inputs for this header have one sign, so its relative mass is at most (1−2^{1−r})^{t/2^{r−1}}, also after averaging over the fixed bits. Either target sign has mass tending to one half as K grows, by independence of the tuple products. Covering that mass with at most 2ʰ headers proves the claim. Unlike the de Bruijn example this construction gives no four-stream layout. It identifies a limit to boundedly many arbitrary partial views with a short common label. Succinctly evaluating cross-block predicates or taking a number of different views growing with the computation remains outside the bound.

This also permits adaptive choice of local scopes from communicated answers. If at most r local views are requested and they send a total of at most a bits to the coordinator on a fixed public protocol tree, fix a header and a path of replies. Agreement with this path imposes one predicate per local view on its prescribed scope. Apply the cylinder bound on that path and union over at most 2ᵃ leaf transcripts. Hence h+a=Ωᵣ(N). Here replies are transmitted on a binary protocol tree so their lengths and message boundaries give no extra uncharged information.
