# A native SAT solver that detects every nonzero error up to logarithmic-logarithmic rank

Put $E=AB-C$ for binary $d$-square matrices. There exist an absolute $c>0$ and **one uniform, globally correct SAT RAM** taking $O(N)$ steps on every file in the compact matrix-mismatch template for which
$$
       1\le\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F_2}(E)\le c\log_2\log_2 d,
       \qquad N=\Theta(d^2).
$$
The entries of $E$ may all be nonzero. The algorithm only returns SAT after finding a failed binary linear check on the product. If its checks pass, it continues with an ordinary complete SAT solver. Thus the runtime assertion is one-sided and independent of the number of nonzeros. Returning UNSAT in linear time on **all** correct products would already give a general uniform quadratic-time binary product verifier by imposing that clock. ## Generating the code within the clock

Use a polynomial-time constructible family of asymptotically good binary linear codes with bounded-fan-in encoder circuits of linear size and message dimensions in a doubling sequence. Spielman's explicit construction suffices. Suppose that producing the circuit at message dimension $b$ takes $O(b^a)$ machine steps for some fixed integer $a$ (increase $a$ to absorb polynomial overhead). Choose a fixed $L>a$. For the given $d$, choose an available $b$ of order $d^{1/L}$ with $b^L\ge d$ and use the $L$-fold tensor product of that one encoder. Circuit production at the small dimension takes $O(d)$ steps. Each of the $L$ coordinate axes can be encoded by applying the small circuit to all its lines. The number of bits at any intermediate layer is $O_L(d)$, and the work per axis is $O_L(d)$, including line enumeration. Thus a single $d$-bit vector can be encoded in $O(d)$ operations after generating the base circuit once. Zero pad its input to $b^L$. The resulting binary map $G$ has output length $M=O(d)$ and constant relative distance $\rho>0$. For the product distance argument, a nonzero vector slice yields a positive fraction of nonzero lines in the next axis, where each such line again has constant relative weight. Iterating gives the product of the component distances. All constants may depend on the chosen fixed $L$. [Spielman, *Linear-Time Encodable and Decodable Error-Correcting Codes*](https://www.cs.yale.edu/homes/spielman/Research/ITsuperc.pdf)

Pad $M$ with zeros to a square $M'=s^2$ with $M\le M'\le4M$; the side $s$ may be a power of two, using the least power-of-four length at least $M$. Form $GA$, $BG^\mathsf T$, and $GCG^\mathsf T$ in $O(d^2)$ word steps by encoding all rows or columns and padding to $M'$. Independently encode a nonzero row and then the surviving columns of $E$. The resulting $H=GEG^\mathsf T$ has at least $\eta M'^2$ ones for a constant $\eta>0$, and its binary rank is at most $r=\operatorname{rank} E$. There are at most $2^r$ row types. Splitting the ones by row type gives a rectangle $U\times V$ of ones with $|U||V|/M'^2\ge\eta 2^{-r}$. [Product-code measurement argument](sparse-product-verification-bit-circuits.md)

## Using the packed word to test a growing number of walk endpoints

On $(\mathbb Z/s\mathbb Z)^2$ take the eight Margulis–Gabber–Galil neighbors and their lazy version with eight identity labels. Its normalized symmetric walk has a constant nonprincipal absolute eigenvalue bound $\lambda<1$, independent of $s$, and degree $\Delta=16$. One label can be applied in constant word time by addition, shifts and masks. For the $t$-step walk, expander mixing guarantees an edge from $U$ to $V$ whenever $\lambda^t<\sqrt{|U||V|}/M'$. [Gabber and Galil](https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-0000(81)90040-4) · [Spectral bound for the chosen maps](https://isa-afp.org/browser_info/current/AFP/Expander_Graphs/Expander_Graphs_MGG.html)

Let $w=\Theta(\log N)$ and take $t=\lfloor\log_\Delta w\rfloor$. Then $\Delta^t\le w$ and $\lambda^t\le\lambda^{-1}w^{-\beta}$ for $\beta=-\log_\Delta\lambda>0$. For all sufficiently large $d$, $r\le c\log_2\log_2 d$ with a sufficiently small fixed $c>0$ implies $\lambda^t<\sqrt\eta\,2^{-r/2}$. Test all walk endpoints from every starting vertex. A depth-first enumeration takes $O(M'\Delta^t)$ word operations apart from the tests; it can enumerate the rooted tree including paths with repeated endpoints. At the pair $(i,j)$ check
$$
          (GA)_{i,*}\cdot(BG^\mathsf T)_{*,j}=(GCG^\mathsf T)_{ij}.
$$
Store the left rows and right columns in contiguous packed lanes. A parity table for blocks of $\lfloor (\log_2 d)/4\rfloor$ bits can be generated in $o(d)$ operations, and a constant number of table accesses evaluates the parity of an ANDed word. Using power-of-two lane capacities within a constant factor of $w$ also avoids integer division. Each dot product costs $O(1+d/w)$ steps. There are $O(dw)$ endpoints including multiplicity. Total native work is $O(d^2)$; all matrices can be held in $O(d^2/w)$ words besides the $O(d)$ scratch for one encoding. Address a packed element by a row-start word and a lane offset, so the ordinal of an individual bit in a constant-factor padded matrix need never fit one word. Tensor line strides can be enumerated by additions. Finite small dimensions can be handled directly.

The SAT program validates the complete fixed layout before using the decoded entries. If needed, try the power-of-two dimensions whose minimum possible encoded lengths fit the file, stopping each template attempt at its own length or first disagreement. Their $O(d^2)$ regeneration costs form a geometric series up to $O(N)$. A certified discrepancy implies that some erroneous entry exists even when its rank exceeds the displayed range. A failed equality check can also identify an erroneous coordinate: compute $E v$ for the right code vector of that check, then compute the product entries in one row where $E v$ is nonzero. The right vector can be obtained in $O(d^2)$ steps by encoding the $d$ standard basis vectors and taking the chosen output bit. No rank computation or advice is used by the program. The code-and-expander argument and the growing-rank time bound are manuscript results; priority for the native bound has not been established.
