# What can support coordinates reveal about three-point moments?

**Research journal, 2026-11-24.** Use the three-point source with degree bound D=n², b=⌊n/2⌋ and F=GF(q), q=2ᵇ. Its marked table positions are pairs (uₖ,vₖ) in F². Write M(P)=ΣₖvₖP(uₖ). An allowed single query has answer Tr M(λXʲ) for 0≤j≤D and λ∈F, with the absolute trace to GF(2). Assume n is large enough that D<q. The diagonal chooses pairwise distinct nonzero abscissas uₖ and pairwise distinct nonzero ordinates vₖ. Under cubic SAT, every fixed nondeterministic verifier running in 2ⁿpoly(n) time with n bits of advice has sound rank at most n+O(1) at every sufficiently large length where its claims are true. [Advised diagonal construction](advised-claims.md)

## Known abscissas make a small set of labels sufficient

Suppose all three abscissas are known. There is a uniformly computable set of more than 2n independent full-table queries whose true answers require only O(n²ᐟ³) bits to specify. Their answers depend linearly on the three unknown ordinates. The calculation below compresses the needed linear data by choosing the masks separately in each degree.

Order the abscissas canonically with u₃ nonzero. Put x=u₁/u₃ and y=u₂/u₃. Let S_L be the binary span in F of the monomials xᵃyᵇ′ with 0≤a,b′≤L, and s_L its dimension. Then s_L≤(L+1)² and s_{2L}≤4s_L. Indeed the exponent square of side 2L+1 is covered by four translates of the smaller square, using shifts 0 or L independently in each coordinate. Multiplication by each translating monomial has binary rank at most s_L on S_L, including when x or y is zero.

Choose the smallest power of two L with (L+1)s_L>2n. Since 1∈S_L, L≤4n. The upper bound on s_L gives L=Ω(n¹ᐟ³). By minimality (L/2+1)s_{L/2}≤2n at large n, so s_L≤4s_{L/2}=O(n/L)=O(n²ᐟ³).

For each j from zero to L and each member a of a fixed binary basis of S_L, use the mask λ=u₃^(−j)a. These (L+1)s_L rows are independent on unrestricted tables. A nontrivial binary combination gives a nonzero polynomial of degree at most L<q, because the masks within each j are independent. Evaluate it at a nonroot and use nondegeneracy of the trace pairing in the ordinate coordinate to obtain a nonzero row. On the three given abscissas the coefficient vector of each query is simply

    (a xʲ, a yʲ, a) ∈ S_{2L} × S_{2L} × S_{2L}.

Its answer is obtained by pairing that vector with the three ordinates. Thus the restricted binary rank is at most 3s_{2L}≤12s_L. Advise the true bits of the first basis rows selected by elimination on these triples. The verifier can recover all the answers by applying the corresponding linear combinations. All loops, field operations and eliminations have size polynomial in n in the source's field representation. The degree range L≤4n fits D=n².

One consequence is that a verifier need only recognize **two distinct true abscissas** at infinitely many lengths, provided its accepted abscissas are sound there and the recognition clock is 2ⁿpoly(n). Supply the third abscissa in b advice bits. To handle a verifier capable of recognizing any of the three, also supply two binary coordinate indices whose joint projection distinguishes the three abscissas, together with the tags of the two to be recognized. One differing coordinate first separates a pair, and a second separates the remaining collision. This costs O(log n) bits. Guess witnesses for the two designated tags. On the good advice every successful branch uses the same three abscissas. There is then room for the O(n²ᐟ³) basis bits since b+O(log n)+O(n²ᐟ³)≤n eventually. The resulting verifier certifies more than 2n independent full-table claims, contradicting cubic SAT under this recognition premise. We have no recognizer for the two coordinates of the constructed source.

## How much information do measured moments give?

Even two complete field measurements M(P),M(Q) need not fix another moment. In the family permitting zero ordinates, if L lies outside the field span of P,Q, prescribe M(L) arbitrarily alongside any feasible values of M(P),M(Q). A basis of the polynomials involved has at most three members. Their evaluations on F have full row rank because their degrees are below q. Pick distinct abscissas giving a full-rank minor, pad to three distinct abscissas, then solve the field system for the three ordinates. Hence each undetermined field moment can take every field value in the same fiber.

The following binary statement covers the full distinctness promises of the diagonal. First choose the three pairs independently uniformly in F². For each nonzero P of degree at most D the sign of Tr M(P) has mean at most (D/q)³ in absolute value. Averaging in each ordinate kills the sign unless its abscissa is a root. Fourier expansion of the indicator of a prescribed transcript of h independent binary trace measurements gives probability at least 2⁻ʰ−(D/q)³, since every nonempty parity corresponds to a nonzero polynomial. This uses the standard small-bias method for almost independent bits. [Naor and Naor](https://doi.org/10.1137/0222053) · [Alon, Goldreich, Håstad and Peralta](https://doi.org/10.1002/rsa.3240030308)

There are twelve forbidden single events, each of probability 1/q, arising from zeros and equal pairs in either list of coordinates. Conditional on any one event, every nontrivial character has absolute mean at most (D/q)². A coordinate fixed to zero leaves two independent ordinary pairs. Equal abscissas let us combine their independent ordinates into a uniform sum. For equal ordinates v₁=v₂ the conditional mean is the product Pr[P(u₁)=P(u₂)]·Pr[P(u₃)=0]. A nonconstant P has fibers of size at most D, and for a nonzero constant P the last factor vanishes. Thus the probability of a given transcript conditional on each forbidden event is at most 2⁻ʰ+(D/q)². Subtracting their contributions shows that each transcript occurs with all coordinates legal whenever

    2⁻ʰ(1−12/q) > (D³+12D²)/q³.

In particular, after taking at most n adaptively chosen binary linear measurements of the moments, each independent additional binary measurement can have either answer at every feasible transcript. Fix its path and omit linear redundancies to apply the bound to the path measurements together with the additional one. The same reasoning allows a whole independent block of new bits when the bound holds for their combined rank. For D=n² it covers combined ranks up to 3b−O(log n), including 2b+1 eventually. Thus even two complete field syndromes do not imply a further independent trace bit on this promise. This quantifies implications from linear measurements on the promise family. It leaves open advice or witnessing information specific to the actual diagonal source.

A finite check in GF(8), using X³+X+1 and degrees zero through three, enumerated all three-point tables with pairwise distinct nonzero abscissas and pairwise distinct nonzero ordinates. The 3,738 different full moment transcripts realized all 16 values on each of the 495 choices of four out of twelve binary basis coordinates. A separate GF(64) check on 430 triples verified the normalized coefficient formula, row counts and projected-rank inequalities above. These finite checks do not test recognizability of the diagonal's coordinates.
