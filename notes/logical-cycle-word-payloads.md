# Unpredictable logical input words with individually essential variable-name bits

The half-error rate under the best clause order also occurs when each predicted pair is an actual logical dependency in a 3-CNF. On every formula below, changing a single low-order bit of a variable name can reverse SAT. The number of such positions is $B\log_2 B$ for one verdict and $B\log_2 B/2$ for the other, counting only destination-variable positions. The two formulas for a fixed cycle and order have identical movable words and differ at one fixed sign bit. One correct unrestricted-workspace solver decides the entire family in $O(N/\log N)$ word steps. The construction tests whether prediction difficulty can force a time lower bound when the data are also relevant to the verdict.

## The physical formulas

Take $B=2^b$ vertices, with $b=2^p$ for arbitrarily large integers $p$. Set $\ell=b+p+4$ and use $4B+9$ explicit clause records. The variable bound $2^{\ell-1}-1$ gives $\ell-1$ index bits and one sign bit per literal. Including the self-delimiting header, $N=12\ell B+O(\ell)$ and $2^{\ell-1}<N+2\le 2^\ell$ eventually, since $8b<12\ell<16b$ with room to absorb the header. The RAM's aligned input words have $12\ell$ bits. Use the same arrangement of identical guard clauses at word boundaries as in the packing proof. Each movable word now contains two logical clauses and one fixed filler tautology between its guard fragments. Use three fixed suffix clauses after the last guard to pin $X_0=0$, pin a fresh $Z=0$, and use $(L_Y\lor Z\lor Z)$ to pin a fresh variable $Y=d$. Choose $L_Y=\neg Y$ for $d=0$ and $L_Y=Y$ for $d=1$. The variables $Y,Z$ use distinct slots outside the cycle, for example $2B,2B+1$. All other spare clauses are tautologies. Variable $X_i$ uses the actual binary index $B+i$, so changing one of its low $b$ bits replaces $i$ by a Hamming neighbor. [Aligned layout](word-permutation-positive-histories.md#making-a-physical-word-movable)

Let $f$ run through all directed Hamiltonian successor cycles on $[B]=\{0,\ldots,B-1\}$. Write $P(i)$ for the parity of the $b$-bit expansion of $i$. Pick $d=0$ for the satisfiable family and $d=1$ for the unsatisfiable family. Write $U_i=X_i$ for $i\ne0$ and $U_0=Y$. The word with source $i$ enforces
$$
 U_i\mathbin{\oplus} X_{f(i)}=P(i)\mathbin{\oplus}P(f(i)).
$$
Encode equality by $(\neg U_i\lor X_{f(i)}\lor\neg U_i)\land(U_i\lor\neg X_{f(i)}\lor U_i)$. For inequality invert the two destination literals. Each destination thus occurs once per clause. There is no separate per-word identifier or payload. The source variable encodes $i$ (with $Y$ naming zero), the destination encodes $f(i)$, and the pair determines the entire word. All $B!$ word orders are allowed. Splitting the source occurrence of zero into $Y$ turns the cycle into a spanning path from $Y$ to $X_0$. Summing its edge equations gives $Y\oplus X_0=0$ by telescoping. The two endpoint pins are consistent precisely when $d=0$. In that case $X_i=P(i)$ is the unique assignment on the path. Changing $d$ changes only the sign of $L_Y$ in the fixed suffix. Consistency of signed graph equations from vertex labels is classical. Here the literal layout permits counting precisely which index-bit edits reverse it. [Harary on signed graphs](https://doi.org/10.1307/mmj/1028989917)


## Single-bit changes on both verdicts

Remove the two clauses for the edge with destination $y=f(i)$. The remaining graph has two path components, one containing each pinned endpoint. This includes a possible isolated endpoint. Together the pins determine a unique assignment $Q$ on the path variables. Under $Q$ one of the removed clauses has its repeated source literal false, and the other clause is already satisfied by the source. Only the destination in the former clause can affect satisfiability of the restored formula. If $d=0$, that destination literal is true. If $d=1$, it is false. Flipping its **sign** alone reverses the formula verdict in either case. Consequently every movable physical word must be loaded before a sound signal, on every cycle and order.

Instead change one bit of its destination **name**, $y\mapsto y\mathbin{\mathrm{xor}}2^t$, leaving its sign, the other clause, and every other physical bit unchanged. Write $z$ for the resulting neighbor. The verdict reverses exactly if $Q_y\ne Q_z$. The two components and their pins still impose $Q$. In the positive family $Q=P$, so every one of the $b$ low name bits in the critical occurrence works at every word.

For the negative family order the vertices as $v_0=f(0),v_1=f(v_0),\ldots,v_{B-1}=0$. The physical path before deletion is $Y,v_0,\ldots,v_{B-1}$. If the omitted edge enters $y=v_j$, the assignment on the two components is
$$Q_{v_r}=P(v_r)\mathbin{\oplus}[r<j].$$
The component starting at $Y$ inherits its discrepancy of one, while the component ending at $X_0$ agrees with $P$ throughout. Since hypercube neighbors have opposite $P$ values, the critical target at $v_j$ is sensitive toward neighbor $v_r$ exactly when $r>j$. Each undirected hypercube edge therefore gives one sensitive position. This proves the counts $Bb$ and $Bb/2$, on **every** cycle. Since $N=\Theta(Bb)$, both are a positive fraction of the encoded input. ## Transfer of the prediction bound and the scan

For a fixed solver and predictor, a mask of missed discoveries and both true names on each miss reconstruct the entire cycle $f$ by simulation, even for a table-dependent physical order. The number of equally likely tables is now $(B-1)!$ and its base-two logarithm is $B\log_2 B-O(B)$. Thus the correction bound still forces more than $(1/2-\epsilon)B$ misses for almost every cycle, on every order, on both verdicts, for every sufficiently short combined predictor and signaling monitor. The failure fraction is $2^{-\Omega_\epsilon(B\log B)}$. Conversely the permutation code works for each table, including each Hamiltonian cycle, by arranging the fresh pairs so a single uniform past-aware predictor gets at least $(1/2-O(1/\log B))B$ exactly right. Totality and the single-word sensitivity just proved justify realizing this sequence on any fixed correct solver. Replacing $B^B$ by $(B-1)!$ in the counting proof costs only $O(B)$ bits. [Reconstruction and constructive upper bound](word-payload-order-rate.md)

A fixed solver can check the header and suffix pins, parse every logical word, and record the destination and sign for each source in directly addressed arrays. Check both clauses express the proposed XOR, all sources occur exactly once, and a walk of $B$ edges in the label graph from zero visits every vertex and returns to zero. Compare the XOR of the recorded signs to the XOR of the two endpoint pins. This decides the formula on a valid split path, even for arbitrary edge signs. On any failed format or cycle check, fall back to ordinary exhaustive SAT decision. For every constructed order, the physical scan and the successor traversal together take $O(B)$ steps. Thus the solver can pay one word step per load even when the first loads are hard to predict and a positive fraction of the variable-name bits are individually significant. The first unresolved time target for general SAT is still exclusion of deterministic $O(N)$ with unrestricted workspace.
