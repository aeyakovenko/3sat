# A SAT promise with many necessary cycles on every positive file

There is a deterministic linear-time, linear-encoded-length map from ordinary SAT to proper DIMACS 3-SAT with the following property. On each satisfiable output of length $N$, **every** Boolean table network of arity at most three representing its full assignment relation needs $\Omega(N/\log N)$ independent graph cycles. The bound persists after fixing any satisfying source assignment and existentially hiding the internal variables of the added clause gadgets. On every unsatisfiable output the relation is empty and has a forest presentation. Consequently deciding SAT on this promise has exactly the same $O(n)$, $O(n^2)$ and $O(n^3)$ RAM thresholds as ordinary SAT.

The size parameter counts encoded bits. Every named bit has one terminal in the network, each internal wire carries one bit, and arbitrary local truth tables and auxiliary wires are allowed. Independent cycles are counted by $|E|-|V|+c$ for $c$ components. External bits may be fixed or hidden by end caps without adding a cycle. ## A lower bound for any wiring of a product of Hamming codes

Let $C$ be the kernel over $\mathbb F_2$ of the $3\times7$ matrix whose columns, in order, are the binary expansions of $1,\ldots,7$. The columns are nonzero and pairwise distinct, and include a basis. Each syndrome thus has 16 words and minimum distance at least three. Take $q\ge1$ independent blocks with any prescribed syndromes. A network representing their product has cycle rank at least $q$, even if different blocks share auxiliary gates.

Cut the $b$ edges outside a spanning forest. Consider just the remaining forest, and color each real terminal leaf by its block. Ignore the cut ports when counting colors. Root each tree at a leaf and process it from its other leaves toward the root. Keep one attached piece at each processed child, with at most two leaves of each color. An internal vertex has at most two children. If their attached pieces can be joined without violating the bound, join them. Otherwise some color would occur at least three times. One of the two child pieces contains two leaves of that color. Cut its edge and finalize the detached piece, charging the cut to those two leaves. The other piece stays attached and satisfies the bound for every color. At a marked root leaf treat its one mark as an additional singleton piece. In case of overflow the child contains two of that color and its edge can be cut. Empty and one-vertex components need no cuts. Each charged pair is in a different finalized piece from every other charged pair. Since each color has seven leaves, at most three cuts can be charged to it. At most $3q$ additional cuts suffice.

Fix all the cut bits in the original graph, using the same value at both severed ports of each wire. The remaining feasible terminal patterns form a Cartesian product over the pieces, since all their other internal variables are independent. If two distinct words are feasible, splice the local pattern of the second word into the first at a piece on which they differ. In any changed block the new and first words differ in only one or two coordinates. Both must have the specified syndrome, contrary to the distance bound. Thus a cut-bit vector admits at most one word. There are $16^q$ possible external words and at most $2^{b+3q}$ cut-bit vectors. This proves $b\ge q$. The argument allows nondeterministic, nonlinear local tables. Cut bounds for normal graph realizations of codes have a substantial literature. Priority for this particular cycle estimate has not been established. [Forney, normal realizations](https://doi.org/10.1109/18.910573) · [Kashyap, edge and vertex cuts](https://arxiv.org/abs/0805.2199) · [Halford and Chugg, cyclic graphical models](https://arxiv.org/abs/cs/0611089) · [Forney and Gluesing-Luerssen, codes on graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.6264)

The proof also gives a bound after global restrictions. For any nonempty subset $R$ of this coset product, the same splice argument gives $b(R)\ge\log_2|R|-3q$. Impose any $k$ affine equations on the data and the uniquely determined parity auxiliaries, including equations that mix bits from every block. After the source is fixed, each auxiliary is affine in the data. Every *consistent* restriction leaves an affine subspace of dimension at least $4q-k$, so the surviving data predicate still needs at least $q-k$ cycles. For $k$ arbitrary binary observations, some transcript retains at least $16^q/2^k$ external words and has the same lower bound. This last claim concerns the largest transcript, whereas the affine statement holds on every feasible transcript.

## Explicit reduction

Write $n$ for the original encoded length and take $q=\max(1,\lfloor n/\lceil\log_2(n+2)\rceil\rfloor)$. First use the linear-time, linear-length normalization to proper 3-CNF with dense occurring names. Each source model on the occurring names has one extension in that normalization. Append $q$ independent blocks of seven fresh data variables. Choose one normalized variable $x$ and require syndrome $(x,0,0)$ in each block. If there is no such variable use the fixed zero syndrome in every block. The argument also permits a different source variable in each block. [Physical encoding and RAM normalization](exact-network-ceiling-for-ordinary-sat.md)

Each row of the Hamming matrix has four entries equal to one. For a zero target on data $a,b,c,d$, introduce $r=a\mathbin\oplus b$ and require $r\mathbin\oplus c\mathbin\oplus d=0$. For target $x$, use $r=a\mathbin\oplus b$, $t=c\mathbin\oplus d$ and $r\mathbin\oplus t\mathbin\oplus x=0$. Every ternary parity equation comprises the four proper clauses forbidding its four wrong assignments. Allocate distinct auxiliaries to different rows. Each choice of the seven data bits meeting the syndrome has exactly one assignment to the row auxiliaries. Each satisfying source assignment has exactly $16^q$ extensions to the complete file. A source contradiction remains a contradiction.

Fix any satisfying normalized assignment and hide all the newly added row auxiliaries. The projected relation on data is precisely the product just analyzed. Pinning and hiding terminals in an optimal network for the full file gives a network for that product. It follows that the original network also uses at least $q$ cycles. The $O(q)$ new clauses and names cost $O(q\log(n+2))=O(n+1)$ encoded bits and RAM steps to emit, including decimal index spellings. The distinct new names alone give the matching lower bound on output length for large $n$. Thus $N=\Theta(n)$ and the lower bound is $\Omega(N/\log N)$. On a malformed input one can apply the same padding to a normalized contradiction.

Define the promise by the image of this construction, or by its semantic consequence “empty relation or relation requiring at least $cN/\log(N+2)$ cycles” with a sufficiently small fixed $c>0$ and a harmless adjustment at bounded lengths. Any program deciding ordinary SAT works on the promise. Conversely the compiler followed by a promise solver decides ordinary SAT in $O(n+T(O(n)))$ steps for a nondecreasing bound $T$. This proves the three threshold equivalences, including at linear time.

## A section of the reduction and the cost it leaves open

For a given source assignment, set data position 1 of each block to $x$, all other data bits to zero, and assign the partial parities honestly. Position 1 has syndrome column $(1,0,0)$ with the low row first. Thus every added variable in this choice is a constant or a copy of a single source bit. For fixed zero targets the all-zero choice suffices. This section works on every source assignment, even one that violates an old clause. A decision procedure that knows the construction can discard the appended constraints. Even finding one extension adds only the time to write it. Large minimum cycle rank prices the ability to represent *all* extensions.

The product gives an explicit test of the proposed step from many cycles to elapsed time, even with arbitrary partial assignments supplied as input. In the zero-syndrome product, present each of the $7q$ pins by a two-bit field meaning free, fixed zero or fixed one. A deterministic word RAM decides extendibility **and can return a canonical extension** in $O(1+q/\log(q+2))$ steps on this packed layout. Pack a consecutive group of whole blocks in at most one half-word, processing $\Theta(\log q)$ blocks in a group for sufficiently large $q$. Tabulate each possible group spelling, checking its blocks against the 16 codewords and saving the least compatible word per block, or a failure flag. Use a group size additionally bounded by $(\log_2 q)/28$ blocks, so that setup costs $O(\sqrt q\,\mathrm{polylog}(q+2))$. Two aligned word reads suffice per group. A packed output uses the same order of time. On the fully pinned zero codeword, changing any single pin from fixed zero to fixed one makes the file inconsistent by minimum distance. With encodings 10 and 11 for these two pins the files differ by one physical bit. Every correct cold solver must read every word containing such a bit on the positive input. Flipping the pin whose word is first read last gives a negative input with the same scan lower bound. Thus both verdicts attain $\Theta(q/\log q)$ worst-case steps. The RAM in this test solves the full partial-assignment problem on a prescribed code, with $b\ge q$ at every size. This prices a concrete obstruction to converting even a cycle bound at every size into extra native steps. The packed pin vector and the explicit DIMACS representation have different lengths.

There is also a proof-complexity consequence. Under this literal-or-constant substitution all appended clause violation polynomials become identically zero on Boolean assignments. As formal polynomials, a surviving violation product contains opposing literals on the same variable and is a multiple of its Boolean axiom. Its degree is at most three. Substitution leaves all old clauses untouched. In polynomial calculus over any field it sends a degree-$D$ refutation of the augmented file to one of the old file of degree at most $\max(D,3)$: substitute lines, derive each image of an added axiom from the Boolean axioms, and simulate each multiplication by a variable using its constant or variable image. Boolean axioms for new bits substitute to zero or old Boolean axioms. For a real sum-of-squares identity the same argument replaces each such residual by its Boolean-axiom multiple, with at most a constant additive increase in certificate degree under the usual degree accounting. Squares remain squares. Hence, starting **after** the previous graph-flow compiler, the same family can simultaneously have $\Omega(N/\log N)$ minimum cycle rank on every positive file and $\Omega(N/\log N)$ PC degree over every field and real SOS degree on every negative file. Old proper dense clauses may simply be retained at this second stage without renormalizing. These are bounds on two different certificates. No bound relating either certificate to the time of a general RAM decider is used. [Negative-file theorem and proof-system conventions](witness-dependent-charges-and-the-negative-sat-clock.md)
