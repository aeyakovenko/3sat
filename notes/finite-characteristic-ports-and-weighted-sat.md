# Finite-characteristic ports give fast modular counting and one-sided randomized 3-SAT

Fix a prime $p$ whose characteristic admits ports. There is a constant $\delta_p>0$ and a deterministic algorithm that evaluates the weighted model count

$$
Z_F(z)=\sum_{x\in\{0,1\}^v:\,F(x)=1}\ \prod_{i:\,x_i=1} z_i
$$

over any explicitly presented degree-$r$ extension of $\mathbb F_p$ in $2^{O_p(v^{1-\delta_p})}\operatorname{poly}(m,v,r)$ bit operations. Here $F$ is an $m$-bit 3-CNF on $v$ variables, and an irreducible polynomial specifies the extension. Weights all equal to one give exact counting modulo $p$. Random weights give a one-sided decision algorithm in the same variable exponent.

The upstream enumerable contraction theorem gives $\eta>0$ for this characteristic. From the topology of a closed bit network of volume $s$ and degree at most $s^{1/20}$ it generates a formula in $2^{O(s^{1-\eta})}$ bit operations. The formula works over every field of the characteristic, uses prime-field constants, and requires at most as many field operations and entry queries. Arithmetic in the presented extension takes $\operatorname{poly}(r)$ bit operations for fixed $p$. [Enumerable contraction theorem](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/Circuit-lower-bounds-for-Hamiltonian-cycle-and-the-permanent-November-24-2026/build/compress.tex#L82-L92)

Choose $0<\alpha<1/4$ with $(1+4\alpha)(1-\eta)<1$. Use the tape paper's sparsification tree. At every petal child record the units that set all center literals false. Defer these units to the leaf, leaving the branching rules intact. Every satisfying assignment now occurs in exactly one leaf, as its values determine whether it takes the center or petal branch. Each leaf gains at most $2v$ distinct units. There are $2^{O(v^{1-\alpha}\log(v+2))}$ leaves, each with $O(v^{1+4\alpha})$ clauses. [Upstream branching and leaf sizes](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/The-logarithmic-exponent-of-integer-multiplication-on-tapes-November-24-2026/build/graphs.tex#L79-L162) · [Disjoint-leaf argument](tape-time-under-complement.md)

At a leaf connect each clause indicator to the participating variables by ternary equality trees. Attach the unary factor $(1,z_i)$ once per variable. Pair it with an all-ones unary factor if the variable is unused. Each variable assignment has a unique consistent copy labeling. The degree-three network therefore contracts to the leaf's weighted sum in volume $O(v^{1+4\alpha})$. Direct evaluation handles constant volumes. Sum the leaf contractions. Any fixed $0<\delta_p<\min\{\alpha,1-(1+4\alpha)(1-\eta)\}$ gives the time bound above.

For decision take the smallest positive $r$ with $p^r\ge8(v+1)$. Deterministically enumerating monic degree-$r$ polynomials and testing each by trial division up to degree $\lfloor r/2\rfloor$ takes polynomial time in $v+1$ for fixed $p$. Choose each $z_i$ independently and uniformly from a fixed subset of $2^{\lfloor\log_2(p^r)\rfloor}$ elements using fair bits. Unsatisfiable inputs always give zero. On a satisfiable input the distinct assignments contribute distinct squarefree monomials with coefficient one, making $Z_F$ a nonzero polynomial of degree at most $v$. Schwartz–Zippel bounds the chance that it evaluates to zero by $1/4$. [Schwartz, polynomial identity testing](https://doi.org/10.1145/322217.322225)

Consequently, a hypothesis excluding one-sided randomized $2^{O(v^{1-\delta})}\operatorname{poly}(m)$ algorithms for every $\delta>0$ rules out ports in every positive characteristic. The upstream deterministic algorithm rules out characteristic-zero ports under the same hypothesis. For each fixed $p$, either the weighted algorithm above exists or the no-ports tape theorem yields $\mathrm{NTIME}_{\rm tape}(n)\ne\mathrm{coNTIME}_{\rm tape}(n)$. Its witness is binary transpose equality on machines with a fixed finite number of one-head sequential tapes.
