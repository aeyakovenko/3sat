# How many independent bits can an affine neighbor table carry?

A fixed-width name table has m slots. Each slot contains a k-bit name. In every table at most D occurrences of any real name are allowed. The Q legal real names and one optional blank symbol are fixed. The blank can occur arbitrarily often. Suppose the output bits are affine functions over the binary field of arbitrary independent input bits, and every output table is legal. With a blank allowed, the rank of the map is at most
```
log₂ binom(DQ+m,m) ≤ DQ+m.
```
In particular an always-valid, densely indexed graph of maximum degree three, given by three reciprocal neighbor slots for each of V vertices, carries at most 6V independent affine bits in its adjacency array. The dimension has order V even on connected simple trees with a specified leaf and a fixed optimal set that activates that leaf alone.

When the blank has a separate tag and the real labels fill an affine cube, a piecewise formula gives the exact dimension, including when there are arbitrarily many slots. [Tagged-blank theorem and finite audit](tagged-blank-affine-capacity.md)

This limits a direct way to transfer the list circuit theorem to the [one-light optimization problem](one-false-read-and-a-subcubic-graph.md). The obstruction concerns dense reciprocal arrays produced on *all* raw inputs. Giving sparse names in an edge list changes the alphabet and which records must be emitted.

## Delete one slot

Fix one member c of the affine family. For each slot i, let Uᵢ be its possible values when every other slot is fixed to its value in c. Thus |Uᵢ|=2^{dᵢ}, where dᵢ is the dimension lost upon deleting slot i. A given real name belongs to at most D of the Uᵢ. Indeed, all variations realizing it in their respective slots can be added to the same basepoint. Their supports are disjoint, so the combined legal table realizes every such occurrence simultaneously. Thus
```
Σᵢ (2^{dᵢ}−1) ≤ Σᵢ |Uᵢ excluding the blank| ≤ DQ.
```
Some dᵢ is at most log₂(1+DQ/m). Project away that slot. The surviving affine family still satisfies the multiplicity cap, even though its partial tables need no longer form graphs. Apply the argument afresh with one fewer slot. Summing the losses gives at most log₂ of the product of (1+DQ/j) from j=1 to m. That product is the asserted binomial coefficient. Empty tables have rank zero. Keeping the integer floors yields a bound independent of the number of blank slots. A loss of at least j at k remaining slots requires k≤DQ/(2^j−1). Thus rank is at most the sum over j≥1 of min(m, floor(DQ/(2^j−1))). It is less than 1.607·DQ for positive DQ. For the numerical estimate sum the first ten reciprocals directly and bound the rest by 2⁻¹⁰/(1−2⁻¹¹).

Without a blank there is an **exact formula** when Q=2^w and the alphabet is the full binary w-cube. For 0≤m≤DQ the maximum rank is
```
Σ_{j=1}^w min(m,D·2^{w−j}).
```
The deletion argument now gives Σᵢ 2^{dᵢ}≤DQ. At k remaining slots the smallest loss is at most min(w, floor(log₂(DQ/k))). Counting its integer levels over k=1,…,m gives the upper formula. For attainment distribute the m slots among D independent banks of sizes differing by at most one. Inside each bank take an affine family of injective lists attaining Σ_j min(bank size, 2^{w−j}). A name appears at most once per bank. At each level j the sum over banks is exactly min(m,D·2^{w−j}), by the balanced sizes and the integral cutoff. Ranks of independent banks add. This uses the earlier injective-table construction on the full cube. [Injective construction and proof](affine-injections-and-one-sided-validity.md)

## How this affects the list reduction

The additive periods of distinctness on l records of b bits, l≤2^b, are exactly the common translations of all records. For unequal shifts on two rows start with distinct entries differing by the XOR of those shifts, then extend to a distinct list. The two entries collide after translation. As a result any affine map through which this decision factors, allowing arbitrary postprocessing, has rank at least (l−1)b. [Proof and Lean check](affine-capacity-of-explicit-permutation-tables.md)

Set L=lb and take the hard parameters l=2^k, b=2k. An affine encoder of the entire raw list into a legal dense subcubic neighbor table, a supplied switch mask, a target vertex and a binary radius can have rank at most 6V+V+O(log(V+1)). Rank is subadditive across these output fields. Thus V=Ω(L), forcing Ω(L log L) physical bits in this format. The list bound has exponent σ≤1, and alone gives no superlinear gate count in this longer output length. The argument also applies to a supplied set given as a fixed-capacity array of sorted distinct IDs and a canonical blank, by the same multiplicity estimate. Arbitrary data in ignored padding is excluded by the specified encoding. Nonlinear preprocessing and tensor contractions that sum over auxiliary bits require separate analyses. [List exponent ceiling](list-and-matrix-costs-for-ram-and-tapes.md)

The same length obstruction holds when only one fixed verdict of the full raw distinctness problem must produce valid simple reciprocal arrays, provided the final decision still computes distinctness on every raw list. Here use the ordinary width w=ceil(log₂(V+1)) per slot, with one canonical blank. If V≥L the size claim already holds. Otherwise w+3<b for large k. Any out-of-range real name can be certified by fixing one field (at most w binary affine equations). A repeated nonblank name in the same neighbor row can be certified by equating two fields and fixing one bit that differs from the blank. If one legal label occurs in at least four different owning rows, at least one incoming pointer lacks a reciprocal slot in the row it names. Fix that pointer and one mismatch bit in each of the three potential reciprocal fields. This costs at most w+3 affine equations. On every member of the resulting nonempty affine subspace, the output fails simple reciprocal validity.

Every affine subspace of raw lists of codimension below b meets both verdicts at these hard parameters. For existence of a collision, a subspace consisting entirely of distinct lists has codimension at least l·log₂ l−l+1 by the injective capacity formula. For existence of a distinct list, a union bound inside an affine subspace of codimension d gives collision probability at most (binom(l,2)+2^d−1)/2^b, which is less than one for d<b and l=2^k, b=2k. The bound follows by grouping dual constraints by the pair of record positions they support. Thus any of the above local failure certificates would contradict validity throughout the promised verdict class. The multiplicity estimate applies everywhere; global connectivity or correctness of the supplied switch set is unnecessary for this step. [Affine subspaces meeting both verdicts](affine-injections-and-one-sided-validity.md)

For an order-matching lower example on the dimension, take a long path of spine vertices s₀,…,s_{k−1}, each with its own private leaf aᵢ. Fix r=a₀, and keep the first two attachments fixed. For disjoint pairs of remaining spine vertices, independently swap their two private leaf labels. The vertex set, sizes, all degrees, and the two parts up to the chosen labels stay as in the initial tree. For bipartiteness only the absence of odd cycles is needed. If fixed bank tags for the bipartition are desired, pair even-index spine vertices with even and odd with odd. In each reciprocal adjacency slot each bit chooses between two constants according to one swap bit. The neighbor order can be canonical by numbering leaves above the spine. Each bit is recoverable from its own pair of slots. This gives affine rank Ω(V). To light r alone, the leaf equations force only s₀ selected on the spine. The spine equations then force just the leaf attached to s₁ among the leaves. That fixed, unique solution is valid under every swap. The graph names carry linearly many bits even with a constant answer on the family.

The dimension estimate explains the loss in a direct bit projection to always-valid explicit dense graphs. It is an information bound on a class of encoders. It establishes no unrestricted graph circuit upper bound or SAT time lower bound.
