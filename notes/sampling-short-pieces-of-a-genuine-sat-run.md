# Sampling short pieces of a genuine SAT run

Fix a deterministic SAT RAM taking at most \(T=C N(\log_2(N+2))^a\) steps on files of \(N\) encoded bits, for a fixed integer \(a\ge0\). Round the public clock up and pad halted runs by a deterministic stutter with no memory events. Partition the clock into consecutive blocks of \(R=\lceil(\log_2(N+2))^{3a+9}\rceil\) steps, shortening the final block. Use the logarithmic-word RAM and the fixed-width binary or decimal DIMACS encoding of the local transfer. For each block, supply the genuine entry and exit controllers, including the registers and instruction positions. The writable memory at entry is private to the original computation. [Packed computation model](packed-band-transfer.md)

Fix a uniform predictor \(P\). Given the real read-only formula, the block index, its time bounds, its two controllers and a uniformly random tape, it proposes a sequence of work-load replies. CPU replay from the entry controller, including access to the actual input, interprets its proposal. Equivalently, the predictor can emit full rows that the replay validates. Time and random-tape length are bounded by \((\log(N+2))^{d}\) for a fixed \(d\), on all arguments including forged controllers. A shorter or adaptively accessed random tape can be padded to a public cap \(r\). Give every correct proposal for the same sequence of native steps credit, even if the predictor ignores different portions of the tape. Write \(\pi_j\) for its probability of producing the genuine block on this input and let
$$
                    I_P(F)=\sum_{j=1}^{p}-\log_2 \pi_j .
$$
A zero probability contributes infinity. The probabilities are mathematical quantities and need no algorithm for evaluating them.

**Theorem.** On each verdict separately, the ratio of \(I_P(F)\) to \(N/(\log(N+2))^{2a+8}\) is unbounded on arbitrarily long files. This implication assumes the stated near-linear SAT solver. Its work space is unrestricted. The same conclusion holds with the least local cost from any fixed finite pool of predictors chosen separately at each block. At the stated block length one can include a predictor supplying independent uniform work replies at every step, with sufficiently many random bits, giving every genuine block positive probability.

## Naming a likely outcome of each sampler

Fix one actual input and its \(p\) target blocks. For a block \(j\), let \(W_j\subseteq\{0,1\}^r\) be the random tapes producing that block. Assume all \(W_j\) are nonempty and put \(s_j=-\log_2(|W_j|/2^r)\). Choose a uniformly random binary \(r\)-by-\(r\) matrix \(A\) and a uniform \(r\)-bit vector \(b\), independently. The map \(f(x)=Ax+b\) has uniform, pairwise independent values on distinct inputs. Indeed \(A(x-x')\) is uniform for \(x\ne x'\) and \(b\) makes \(f(x)\) independent of the difference. Invertibility of \(A\) is unnecessary. [Carter and Wegman on universal hashing](https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-0000(79)90044-8)

Set \(h_j=\lceil s_j\rceil+\lceil\log_2(2p)\rceil\). If \(h_j<r\), use the \(2^{h_j}\) indices with a zero high prefix. The number of them whose images lie in \(W_j\) has expectation at least \(2p\). Its variance is at most its expectation by pairwise independence, so the probability of missing \(W_j\) is at most \(1/(2p)\). A union bound gives a single pair \(A,b\) working simultaneously for all these blocks. Record one successful index in \(h_j\) bits. If \(h_j\ge r\), give a genuine seed directly in \(r\) bits. This second mode also covers equality and does not rely on \(f\) being onto. With \(r=0\) no seed is needed. Encoded lengths and a mode bit per block fit one extra word per block since \(r\) is polylogarithmic.

Consequently all genuine blocks can be specified by one shared \(O(r^2+r)\)-bit map and
$$
             \sum_j s_j + O(p\log(N+2))
$$
further bits, including the controllers and a directory of packed offsets. For a finite pool, use a common cap \(r\) and put the chosen predictor index in its block record. The same matrix proof applies to the resulting \(W_j\). The checker evaluates only the proposed index, multiplies the binary matrix by a vector directly, and enforces all predictor and replay clocks.

## Checking the proposed trajectory

An existential chart gives the shared map, controllers, offsets and indices. Once fixed, **each block has a single decoded trajectory**. A local replay verifies the actual instruction and input semantics, checks the endpoints, and reports its work events in the machine's ordered slot convention. Public CPU state starts genuine and the terminal signal must have the requested sign. Malformed records or an invalid replay fail. Global memory consistency has the following universal–existential test.

* A challenger may pick any one block. Verify the block including its relevant endpoint and directory checks. Neighboring directory offsets can be checked at this same block, including initial and final offsets and the maximum local length.
* Alternatively the challenger names a work-read slot and a putative latest earlier store slot, or the initialized zero as its source. Replay the denoted blocks. An out-of-range challenge, a mismatched address, a slot of the wrong kind, a source out of order, or agreement of the proposed source and read values discharges the challenge. Otherwise demand the block and time of a strictly intervening store to the read's address. Replay that block to verify it. For the initialization challenge the interval starts before the first event.

An honest computation passes. If a read is inconsistent, naming its actual previous store on the decoded history, or initialization, leaves no intervening-store response. This checks all cross-block changes to memory, even though the predictor can propose arbitrary words and can depend arbitrarily on the supplied endpoints. The test therefore certifies a genuine run on every input. It needs at most three block replays for a challenge and has quantifier order existential chart, universal challenge, existential response. An input-specific prior obtained from hypothetical edited prefixes is covered when its proposed local transcript is generated within the charged time and advice bounds. Sampling an edited file and executing an arbitrarily long prefix uses additional work.

## Predicting within an edit radius

The same conclusion holds for the probability of **coming close** to each genuine block. Fix an integer \(s\ge0\), put \(\rho=\lfloor(\log_2(N+2))^s\rfloor\), and use blocks of \(R_s=\lceil(\log_2(N+2))^{3a+s+9}\rceil\) instructions. Write the genuine block as one token per instruction, specifying the active work-load slots and their replies, with fixed padding. A token costs \(O(\log N)\) bits. Allow \(P\) to propose any syntactically encoded token string within its clock. Count a seed as a hit whenever the minimum number of whole-token insertions, deletions and substitutions to obtain the genuine string is at most \(\rho\).

Use these enlarged sets of successful seeds \(W_j\) in the same shared affine map. Alongside the chosen index give a script of at most \(\rho\) edits correcting the predicted string to the genuine one. Each edit uses \(O(\log N)\) bits for operation, position and literal token. Invalid scripts fail validation, and applying a script by successive copying takes only a fixed polynomial in \(\log N\), since the predictor clock, block length and radius are all fixed powers. Decode the repaired string before performing every CPU or memory check. Thus the cost beyond \(\sum_j-\log_2\pi_j\) is \(O(p(\rho+1)\log N+\operatorname{polylog}N)\) bits for these enlarged probabilities \(\pi_j\). In the hierarchy reduction below \(p(\rho+1)=O(H/L^{2a+6})\) with the new \(R_s\). The same bound \(N/\log^{2a+8}(N+2)\) on the total information would therefore give the same contradiction on either verdict. This includes samplers that propose sequences that individually obey the native semantics but typically require some edits to reproduce the actual execution.

## The clock

Write \(H\) for the source nondeterministic time bound, \(m\) for its public words, and \(L=\lceil\log_2(H+2)\rceil\). Apply the bounded acceptance reduction with padded formula length \(N=\Theta(HL^3)\). Formula words can be generated from the original input in polylogarithmic time, including decimal by using the elementary digit generator. Suppose on all sufficiently large files of the required sign that \(I_P(F)=O(N/\log^{2a+8}(N+2))\). Guess a capped chart of \(g\) words. The block count and the preceding coding bound give
$$
  p=O(H/L^{2a+6}),\qquad
  g=O\!\left(p+I_P(F)/L+\operatorname{poly}(L)\right)
   =O(H/L^{2a+6}) .
$$
Only the hypothetical cap is used to allocate the chart. The checker does not compute \(I_P\). Guessing the words takes \(O(g)\) steps. A single challenge–response replay takes \(\operatorname{poly}(L)\) steps with random access to the chart, formula generator and original input. The fixed polynomial depends on \(a,d\), the predictors and the guest instructions. It does not multiply \(g\).

For fixed chart and challenge, eliminate the existential response with a materialized SAT computation. The bounded nondeterministic reduction with \(M\) public words and \(h\) computation steps has \(O(h L^3+M L^2+\operatorname{poly}(L))\) encoded bits and the same construction-time bound. Here \(h=\operatorname{poly}(L)\), \(M=O(g+m+\operatorname{poly}(L))\), hence this SAT call takes \(O((g+m)L^{a+2}+\operatorname{poly}(L))\). Eliminate a failing challenge by one ordinary SAT simulation and complement its answer. This yields a nondeterministic verification of the desired sign in
$$
 O\!\left((g+m)L^{2a+5}+\operatorname{poly}(L)\right)
   =O\!\left(H/L+mL^{2a+5}+\operatorname{poly}(L)\right).
$$
All virtual guest addresses and any wider host used for the finite number of hierarchy simulations obey fixed polynomial caps. Each chart and replay verifier is a fixed bounded program. The local sampler here can assign high probability to one history among many, and each call receives both true controllers. The shared map charges the probabilities of the actual blocks, including when the sampler has enormous support.
