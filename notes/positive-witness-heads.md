# Travel and crossing information on an actual SAT witness

**Research journal, 24 November 2026.** Assume explicit SAT takes $O(N\log^a N)$ deterministic time on the packed logarithmic-word RAM for fixed $a\ge0$, where $N$ is the number of encoded formula bits. Fix a sound SAT witness verifier with random read access to the formula, an endmarked binary certificate tape with one head moving by at most one position per step, and ordinary private workspace. The verifier may be nondeterministic. Fix a polynomial cap on its time and certificate length. Let $s$ be the number of bits sufficient for its maximum explicit private configuration on an accepting run, counting stored addresses if private memory uses sparse random access. Let $D$ count unit moves of the certificate head.

There is a family of **satisfiable** 3-CNFs, one for each sufficiently large source padding length, and a constant $C$ such that every accepting run within the caps, on every certificate, satisfies
$$
  1+s+\sqrt{(D+1)(s+\log_2(N+2))}
    \ \ge\ B_N,\qquad B_N=N\,2^{-C\sqrt{\log_2(N+2)\log_2\log_2(N+4)}} .
$$
For complete polynomial-time verifiers the clock can be taken large enough that accepting witnesses exist. There is a more local conclusion. When $s$ is below the allowed budget, some interval of $\Omega(B_N)$ **consecutive positions** in that particular certificate has expensive crossing lists at every cut. Even with the formula and cut index available, each list lacks a description of at most $B_N$ bits within the fixed decoding bounds below, after enlarging $C$. The certificate, run and interval can vary. A fixed list decoder is chosen before the family. The proof also permits two independently positioned certificate heads if the charged space contains their current window, or handles a bounded number of changes of active head as described below.

## Checking two neighboring crossing lists at a time

Write $U=N^{O(1)}$ for the clock. Charge private configurations via a fixed, replayable encoding, with local simulation in $O(s+\operatorname{polylog}N)$ bits and polynomial time. In the literal tape model this is the usual list of tape contents, head positions and control. Public formula and certificate positions, time, and the endmarkers take $O(\log U+\log N)$ additional bits. Let $L$ be the bounded certificate length, including endmarkers as prescribed. A crossing list at a cut contains every move between its two adjacent positions, sorted by strictly increasing arrival time. Each entry gives the direction and complete post-move private state. The certificate position after crossing is specified by the cut and direction. Use exterior cuts with empty lists. For succinct lists use a fixed deterministic decoder taking $F$, cut, description and ordinal as input, returning the indexed entry or a sentinel. A length field, explicit caps including at most $U$ entries, and a default on timeout define every proposed list. Constants for the clock and decoder workspace are fixed first. The decoder may use polynomial time and $O(B+\operatorname{polylog}N)$ scratch when the allowed budget is $B$.

Here is a single nondeterministic small-space tape simulation for the following certificate mode. Guess an end time $E\le U$, the certificate length, a terminal certificate position, and a private-space cap at most $B$. Process its positions in increasing order. Guess the next block's right endpoint, no farther than $B$ cells past its left cut, and a description of its right crossing list of at most $B$ bits. Keep the preceding right description as the next left description, including its cut and count. Both exterior cuts have the mandatory empty list. Guess the bits in the current block, respecting endmarkers. Only two crossing descriptions are live, even on a tape with many blocks.

Merge the two decoded lists by arrival time. Check directions, strictly increasing times, valid states, clock bounds, and at most one crossing at a particular time in this block. A visit starts at an incoming crossing's reported time and state, or at the genuine time-zero state for the block containing the initial head. It ends with the transition through the specified outgoing cut, or at the proposed terminal horizon if the head finishes in the block. Check that the events are exactly a sequence of such visits for this block. Replay every visit nondeterministically with the guessed local certificate bits. Every nonfinal move stays inside until the required exit. At exit compare the entire post-state, cut, direction and arrival time. At a terminal visit check the terminal head location, time and accepting state. A terminal arrival at time $E$ allows a zero-step final visit. A block entered at time zero and never left handles stationary runs. Blocks with no visits and no endpoint obligations pass. Controller decoding, private space, physical input probes and time limits are enforced on every replay.

For soundness, join the block replays in time order starting at time zero. An exit and its neighboring entry are supplied by the identical cut description. Hence the next replay starts with exactly the state produced by the preceding one, even when the adjacent blocks were verified far apart in time. Each crossing strictly increases the time since the previous entry. No replay can leave a block without a matching entry in the neighbor, and any completed path must stop at the checked accepting terminal. Taking the guessed bits in disjoint spatial blocks defines one actual immutable certificate. Local nondeterministic choices concatenate along this one path. This establishes acceptance even for dishonest descriptions, without requiring their uniqueness or a bound on the number of possible certificate strings. The converse holds whenever an actual accepting run has a partition with these descriptions. Generating and rescanning every local list and checking visits takes polynomial time and $O(B+\operatorname{polylog}N)$ space on tapes. The number of cells, blocks, list entries and scans, and the native per-step simulation cost all have fixed polynomial caps. [Finite visit concatenation and one-bit reflection lemmas](../lean/WitnessHeadVisits.lean)

If every interval of $B$ consecutive internal cuts contains a cut with a short crossing description, all gaps between consecutive such cuts (including the exterior cuts) have width at most $B$, with the usual unit-index rounding. The sweep then accepts. For literal lists, each crossing uses at most $c(s+\log(N+2))$ bits. If the size cap rules out every partition on a true run with small $s$, it contains $B-O(1)$ successive cuts each crossed at least $\Omega(B/(s+\log(N+2)))$ times. Summing their crossings gives the claimed travel inequality. Include a fixed literal-list mode alongside the chosen decoder to obtain both conclusions on the same family. The [earlier weighted-travel bound](crossing-bits.md) paid for one chronological directory at a translated grid. The two-list sweep permits separately encoded long lists and requires only their local overlap. Classical single-tape simulation and crossing-sequence results supply precedents for this use of spatial locality. [Liśkiewicz and Loryś](https://doi.org/10.1137/0219034) · [Williams on tape speedups](https://people.csail.mit.edu/rrw/qbf-lower-bd.pdf)

## Why the formulas are satisfiable

Use the signed bounded source on $x$ of length $n_0$ with deadline $H=n_0^2$ and explicit locally available 3-CNF $F_x$ of length $N=\Theta(H\log^3 H)$. The sweep uses the true formula. Its physical input contains just $x$, caps and fixed controls. Formula lookup costs polynomial time and polylogarithmic space using the local compiler. Under the assumed SAT time, bounded tape reachability decides deterministically whether that sweep has an accepting computation in time
$$
 (n_0+B+\operatorname{polylog}N)\,
 2^{O(\sqrt{\log N\log\log(N+4)})}.
$$
Take $B=\lfloor N\,2^{-C_0\lceil\sqrt{\lceil\log_2(N+2)\rceil\lceil\log_2\log_2(N+4)\rceil}\rceil}\rfloor$, rounding logs and square roots by integer arithmetic, and use sufficiently large $C_0$. The deterministic source program signals if and only if the answer is **no**. It finishes within $o(H)$ on both answers, including its fixed interpreter overhead. To avoid any circular choice of constants, the same program can read a positive integer $C_0$ from a separate, length-delimited field in the source padding. Its resource bound above is uniform in that fixed coefficient: the sweep and its local polynomial clocks are unchanged, and the threshold arithmetic on $O(\log N)$-bit integers is within the bound. Choose the field after fixing the programs and their reachability constants. [Packed diagonal and virtual 3-CNF](packed-band-transfer.md) · [Reachability with a growing number of alternations](linear-quantifiers.md) · [Near-linear clock extension](pebbling-live-producers.md)

At each sufficiently large padding length put this deterministic program in the interpreter's description field, with the same coefficient. By the defining reduction, its formula is satisfiable precisely when the source program signals by the deadline. It signals precisely when the small-space witness sweep rejects. Any acceptance by the sweep, in contrast, proves actual satisfiability by the soundness of the witness verifier. These implications force the formula to be satisfiable and the sweep to reject. Consequently the local band assertion holds for **every actual accepting path** with private state and both decoder resources within the fixed budgets. Adjusting constants in $C$ covers floor functions, fixed space factors and polylogarithmic bookkeeping. A verifier that has no genuine accepting path at the cap satisfies the assertion vacuously there.

There is also an efficient way to obtain one genuine assignment on this particular family. Given the padding, run the bounded interpreter's deterministic successful computation. Record its rows, sort the access records, assign the Beneš routing switches by alternating colors on the even cycles at each recursion level, and evaluate all remaining local gates of the reduction. The depth of the routing recursion is $O(\log H)$ and it handles $O(H)$ record positions per level. All steps, including generating the explicit formula and filling its Tseytin variables, use $N\operatorname{polylog}N$ deterministic RAM time on this fixed family. This construction uses the hypothesized solver inside the deterministic source program. It separates the cost of *finding* a witness here from the cost of *checking* a witness with one locally moving head.


## Quantifying over proof systems at the three SAT exponents

The positive reflection gives a separate consequence even at $k=2,3$, where the growing-depth tape speedup above is unavailable. Fix a sound nondeterministic RAM proof checker $P$. It can access the formula through a local generator and its short public source $x$, or use the description of that generator as its statement. Soundness means that an accepting proof for the supplied description implies that its explicit formula is satisfiable. Guessing a proof bit or word as it is needed counts as a step; any shared read-only proof that must be materialized to ensure consistent later queries is paid for at its encoded length. Fix the program *before* choosing the hard family.

Suppose SAT takes $O(n^k)$ deterministic time, $k=1,2,3$. For each sufficiently large source padding length there is a locally generated satisfiable 3-CNF of size $N$ on which $P$ has no accepting proof with guessing, materialization and verification cost at most $N^{1/k}/\log^{A}N$ for a sufficiently large fixed $A$. To see this, set $H=n_0^d$ with fixed integer $d>k$. On an arbitrary source input test existence of a $P$-acceptance within a public budget $b$, emulating any requested words of $F_x$ locally. This yields a bounded nondeterministic RAM test on $x$ taking $(b+1)\operatorname{polylog} H$ steps. Its ordinary explicit SAT formula has at most $(n_0+b+1)\operatorname{polylog} H$ bits, including the physical input words. Materialize the formula and use the assumed solver once. The full deterministic clock is $(n_0+b+1)^k\operatorname{polylog}H$. Taking $b=N^{1/k}/\log^A N$ with $N=\Theta(H\log^3 H)$ gives $o(H)$ and a computable integer cap. One can take the logarithmic exponent from the same length-delimited field as above. Signal precisely on absence. On its own padded description the deterministic source again gives satisfiability if and only if no such fast proof exists, and a successful proof implies satisfiability. Its satisfiable assignment can be generated from the padding by the same $N\operatorname{polylog}N$ constructor. This is an all-sufficiently-large-source-length bound for a checker and its own family. The [earlier nondeterministic hierarchy argument](succinct.md) gives a related infinitely-often assignment-circuit lower bound in a different resource measure.

For example, $P$ can check a proof in a fixed sound formal theory that a declared guest verifier accepts only satisfiable descriptions, then simulate one accepting guest computation. Program, derivation and simulation are all charged. Only soundness of the chosen system for these claims is needed. The result gives one family for this whole certified collection. Conversely, giving the family itself to a new verifier bypasses an old hard family: guess its padded source length, compare the explicit input to its locally generated formula by scanning, and accept every match at sufficiently large lengths. The sources here consist of a fixed program, coefficient and prescribed padding, so their words can be regenerated with polylogarithmic scratch. For a complete verifier, use the ordinary assignment check as a second option. This family-specific shortcut is sound once positivity has been established. Applying the construction to the enlarged verifier changes the family, and inserting an arbitrary allegedly sound verifier into the guessed mode would invalidate the signed proof. Proving the soundness of each member inside the original system is a separate condition. Finite versions of this distinction occur in lower bounds on proofs of bounded consistency. [Pudlák](https://users.math.cas.cz/~pudlak/improved.pdf)

## Two heads, three heads, and batching

For a single head which reverses direction at most $p$ times, every cut has at most $p+1$ crossings. Thus on the same positive family every accepting path has $1+s+(p+1)(s+\log N)=\Omega(B_N)$. For a fixed number of heads, a separate sufficient condition for the sweep is a short directory of **switches of active head**. Normalize each primitive step to operate on one head; current scanned bits of stationary heads may be cached as part of the private state. Split the time line when the active head changes. Guess each switch's time, all head positions, the private configuration and the initial and final state. Check in each epoch that inactive positions stay put. A distinguished active pointer teleports at a switch from the old head's position to the new head's position, while preserving every original head position and the specified state. When checking a block, merge the applicable epoch-start and epoch-end events from the directory with its crossing lists. Charge both ends of each switch and give a fixed order to same-time exit and entry obligations, or give each switch one separate padded step with a checked index and successor. Gluing across a switch now uses the shared directory, and the same immutable block bits answer queries from *any* head. If a run has $p$ switches, charging $O((p+1)(s+\log N))$ bits for this directory and $O(\sqrt{(D+1)(s+\log N)}+s+\log N)$ for the cell blocks and literal crossings suffices, where $D$ counts actual unit moves by active heads. Indeed if $K$ crossing entries fit the per-cut budget, at most $D/K$ cuts have more than $K$ entries; hence no all-heavy interval is longer than $D/K$. The positive diagonal can include this mode for any fixed verifier with several heads. If the heads instead always fit within a common position window of width $w$, store the seen window with its positions and use one certificate head. One implementation keeps the left endpoint as its physical head, reads a fresh fringe cell by a trip of at most $O(w+1)$ moves whenever the window shifts, and charges all such trips to its actual $D$.

For explicit 3-CNF there is a deterministic verifier using **three monotonically advancing heads on one shared certificate**, $O(\log N)$ workspace and $O(N\operatorname{polylog}N)$ time and certificate length. The first array contains a truth bit alongside every literal occurrence in clause order, including its true variable index. Scan it against the formula and require every clause to be satisfied by its three indicated bits. The remaining arrays give the successive merge-sort levels of these records by variable index, with occurrences as tie-breakers if desired. Two heads skip to the starts of the two source runs, each moving forwards over a single level with jumps implemented by scans. The third scans the proposed output segment, checking each deterministic merged record. Skip the unused alternate source runs when going to the next pair, carrying at most a constant number of records and counters. At a level boundary both source heads advance to the next source level and the output head is already positioned for the next output level. The initial formula-order validation can be performed with the third head as it moves to the start of the first output level, leaving the others at the beginning. Check equality of all truth bits within each variable group while scanning the final sorted output. Format, level lengths and endmarkers are verified. Only $O(N\operatorname{polylog}N)$ total head movement is needed. Any genuine satisfying assignment supplies this one shared certificate, and acceptance defines an assignment to every occurring variable. This verifier can switch active heads almost every record. Distinct independent streams could vary a source record between comparisons. Grohe, Koch and Schweikardt study how the number of scans and internal memory affect sorting in an external-memory model. [Grohe, Koch and Schweikardt](https://arxiv.org/abs/cs/0505002)

With **two heads making just one reversal each**, the same asymptotic time, space and certificate length suffice. Tag every output record of each proposed merge by the half-run it came from, using a fresh tag per level. Compare records without these tags. Advance the output head past the first level, validating it in formula order. During one forward traversal of *all* consecutive pairs of levels, compare each left source half-run in order with the left-tagged records of its corresponding output run. Require that both filtered lists end together at that run boundary. The source head stays one level behind the output head throughout the sweep. On the final output level check that it is sorted with consistent variable truth bits. At the ends of the penultimate and final levels respectively, reverse both heads. In a single backward traversal of *all* level pairs, compare the right source half-runs to the right-tagged outputs in reverse order with the same boundary check. The two filters are disjoint and exhaustive, so every output run is a permutation of the two input runs. A genuine sorted merge passes both checks. Fixed sizes, counters and endmarkers enforce level boundaries in either direction. This costs $O(N\operatorname{polylog}N)$ total movement.

Consequently each head crosses **every physical cut at most twice**. Its whole chronological crossing list, even recording both head positions and the entire private state, has only $O(\log N)$ literal bits. A two-head extension based just on short marginal crossing lists would apply on every satisfiable formula. The one-head gluing proof needs all symbol reads in a visit to come from the retained block.

The same scan verifies nondeterministic word-RAM computations bounded by $t$ steps, up to polylogarithmic overhead in time and certificate length. The first level contains the chronological log of the guessed computation, with the control, registers, time, address and data of each access. Verify local transitions, physical input probes, initial state and accepting termination while scanning the first level. Constantly many accesses per instruction can be split into constant substeps with ordered timestamps. Sort those access records by address and then time via the same tagged levels. At the last level require each read to return the most recent write, or the prescribed initial value at that address. Local transitions and the address-sorted check together give an actual computation. A RAM conversely guesses a bounded certificate once and simulates its two heads, paying linear time in the certificate length plus the time of the verifier on the ordinary logarithmic-word model. Thus the two traversals already support verification of general near-linear RAM histories within polylogarithmic factors.

Even **two monotonically advancing heads** suffice at a polynomial clock. Use rows of a left-to-right bubble sort as the certificate. The source head streams one row, retaining its current carry record in logarithmic space. It reads a new record, sends the smaller record to comparison with the next physical output cell under the other head, and retains the larger as carry. Flush the last carry at the end of the row. Each pass moves the largest remaining record to its sorted suffix. After at most as many passes as records, the sorted row permits the variable-consistency check. Enforce the row sizes and count and validate the first row against the formula. The heads advance from each source/output pair directly to the next, using $O(N^2\operatorname{polylog}N)$ certificate bits and time.

Two forward-only certificate heads have a direct FIFO interpretation. A nondeterministic simulator guesses each fresh certificate symbol when the leading head first reaches it. It keeps in a queue the interval already passed by the leader and still accessible to the trailing head, dequeuing as the latter advances. Both roles can be exchanged when the heads coincide; the currently scanned bits, public length and positions fit in the controller. Conversely, a pair of forward heads verifies an enqueue by comparing the appended symbol with the bit on the leading head and advancing it; the trailing head retrieves that same symbol at dequeue. Controller counters enforce emptiness and the endmarkers. Fixed alphabets cost a constant factor. Classical single-queue simulation lower bounds of Li, Longpré and Vitányi concern machines with one-way physical input and their stated internal stores. Here the formula is freely reread and the verifier has logarithmic private workspace. [Li, Longpré and Vitányi](https://doi.org/10.1137/0221042)

For an elementary single-head comparison, restrict to $m$ clauses and variables densely named in $[v]$ (or use their sorted list of occurring names, regenerated by polynomial-time scans). Group at most $b$ clauses at a time, store one satisfaction flag for each, and sweep the entire truth assignment once per group. For each variable position and its bit, rescan the group's clauses on the immutable formula and update their flags wherever that variable occurs. Standard counters cost $O(\log N)$ bits. Alternating sweep directions or rewinding gives $D=O((1+\lceil m/b\rceil)(v+1))$ and $s=O(b+\log N)$ at polynomial time. Taking $\log N\le b\le m$ gives $D(s+\log N)=O(m(v+1))$. Families with $m$ and $v$ both proportional to $N/\log N$ permit this product to be close to quadratic in encoded length up to logarithms. The lower bound lacks the factor needed to contradict that general-purpose construction. Extending the cheap sweep even to two forward-only heads at arbitrary polynomial clocks, or to two heads making one reversal each at near-linear clocks, would supply a route to the unrestricted linear-time target. The proof gives no such simulation.

## Exactly how much a shared assignment digest can save

Consider the unsatisfiable width-two chain $x_1\land \bigwedge_{i<m}(\neg x_i\lor x_{i+1})\land\neg x_m$. Splitting the two occurrences of each variable into tracks $u,v$ gives locally accepting pairs exactly when $u_1=1$, $v_m=0$ and $v_i\le u_{i+1}$ for $i<m$. For every nonzero $\delta\in\mathbb F_2^m$, let $j$ be its first set position. Put $u_i=v_i=1$ before $j$, and $u_i=\delta_i,\ v_i=0$ thereafter. This pair passes locally and $u\oplus v=\delta$. Thus any common $\mathbb F_2$-linear digest permitting equality only on nonaccepting pairs must have rank $m$. The unrestricted optimum has **exactly $m+1$ distinct values**. The Hamming weight suffices because $|u|\ge1+|v|$, and the $m+1$ prefix-one strings form a clique: for any $j>k$, $(1^j0^{m-j},1^k0^{m-k})$ passes locally. Weight can be streamed with $\lceil\log_2(m+1)\rceil$ bits.

In general, suppose the verifier takes two complete assignments to *all* variables, picking a fixed side at each literal occurrence. For an unsatisfiable $F$ with $m$ clauses, any assignment can be colored by its first violated clause. Two assignments of the same color falsify that clause under every splice, so $m$ colors always suffice in this model. Pair-dependent values supplied on a separate auxiliary track need their own justification. Computing a violated-clause color from one assignment pass within a small space budget is a separate search problem. Under near-linear SAT the signed argument forces a family on which a small deterministic single-pass color selector cannot have short code and polynomial time with sublinear-power memory: one can preguess the selector's output clause, generate an arbitrary assignment sequentially, and retain its at most three indicated bits. Checking for an out-of-range answer or a selected clause that is true takes only the selector's space plus counters. Absence of any such execution certifies UNSAT, since a satisfying assignment defeats every total selector. On an input with such a selector genuine absence supplies the needed honest certificate. The falsified-clause search problem is also characterized by read-once branching programs in work on regular resolution. [Atserias et al.](https://ilariobonacina.github.io/publications/2021-ABDLNR-jacm.pdf) The negative signed diagonal is the one in [the projected-transcript argument](projected-trace-lists.md). This does not rule out selectors whose witness-access patterns themselves require large memory. [Finite chain and gluing lemmas](../lean/WitnessHeadVisits.lean)
