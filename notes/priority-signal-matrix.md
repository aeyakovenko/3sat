# Which trial outcomes determine the hard SAT sign?

At stage $i$ of the [pinned construction](priority-pinned-sat.md), write $X_{e,i}=1$ if requirement $e$ would signal in its allotted interpreter ticks on the zero input of length $2^i$. Set inactive entries to zero. These are raw trials: an eligible program can be run with that budget even if an earlier stage retired it or another program preempted it. Its behavior is independent of the retirement list. The real schedule scans stages in order. At each stage it takes the first unretired rank with entry one, if there is one. The winner retires and determines the color; an empty stage has color one. For the question at scale $j$, this gives a rectangle of $a_j$ ranks and $j$ stages with $a_j=O(\log\log(j+4))$.

## Recovering the schedule in rank order

Process ranks in increasing order. Give rank $e$ its earliest signal stage not already assigned to a smaller rank, or no stage if there is none. This gives exactly the original awards. For rank zero the first signal is its actual award. Assuming the smaller ranks have been assigned their true stages, a still-unretired $e$ wins at its earliest remaining signal: no smaller rank wins there, a larger one cannot preempt it, and any earlier signal of $e$ was preempted. This proves the claim by induction. Only the first $e+1$ eligible signals of each rank are needed: at most $e$ distinct earlier-rank awards can block them. Truncating to $e$ does not suffice when each of its first $e$ signals is blocked. This is a serial allocation with a common order of preferences on the stages. The signal positions themselves may be expensive to locate. [Serial allocation in the matching literature](https://www.tayfunsonmez.net/wp-content/uploads/2013/10/AbdulkadirogluSonmez-Econometrica98.pdf)

## Exact certificates from partial trial information

Suppose a chart supplies a partial assignment to the raw entries, with a sound witness for each entry it discloses. Here undisclosed active entries can vary independently. There are at most $(j+1)^{a_j}$ proposed schedules: each rank chooses its prospective winning stage or no stage. Discard proposals assigning a stage twice or putting an inactive rank at a stage. For each stage in each surviving proposal, read off the ranks still unretired in the proposal. A proposed winner imposes a one on its raw entry and zeros on all smaller still-free eligible entries. An empty stage imposes zeros on every still-free eligible entry. These are all the constraints. Each concerns one entry, and entries at different stages are independent. Consequently the proposed schedule is possible exactly when none of these requirements contradicts a supplied entry. To see sufficiency, fill the required entries as prescribed, complete the rest arbitrarily and induct through the stages. A possible schedule of the wrong color is therefore a complete test for failure of the proposed certificate.

Equivalently, for each wrong-color schedule form the set of its required entries that disagree with the actual signal matrix. One may assign arbitrary nonnegative costs to disclosing individual true entries. The least total cost of independent disclosures certifying the sign is exactly the minimum weight of a set meeting every such disagreement set. This equality is in the disclosure model. A proof that correlates multiple undisclosed entries can use a different representation. Enumerating schedules, including the polynomial-in-$j$ work per schedule, takes $2^{O(\log(j+2)\log\log(j+4))}=2^{o(j)}$ operations. No enumeration of certificates or complete matrices is needed in the deterministic test of a supplied chart. [Finite audit of equivalence, sharp truncation, and minimal disclosure](../audits/priority_signal_matrix_audit.py)

There is a faster implementation. Sweep stages and store the set of reachable masks of retired ranks. From each current mask and each full signal column consistent with this stage's disclosures, compute its first unretired active one or the empty outcome. Insert the resulting next mask. At the target stage also record the sign on the transition. Start with the zero mask. Induction on the sweep proves the exact set of possible signs, since each column can be chosen independently given the supplied information. Both the old mask and column range over at most $2^{a_j}=O(\log(j+4))$ values, so the check takes $O(j(a_j+1)4^{a_j})$ elementary word operations when column membership is given by a truth table. This handles **arbitrary within-stage correlations**: give a sound list of allowed whole columns at each stage, containing its true column, and test all schedules consistent with these lists. Joint proofs connecting different stages require maintaining their extra shared state. For entrywise disclosures each column can instead be scanned once per old mask, branching only on possible winners, for $O(j(a_j+1)2^{a_j})$ operations using word masks and an indexed reachable-set array.

Put $M=2^j$. A fixed nondeterministic verifier can guess a chart and check its disclosed facts with a specified sound method, then perform this enumeration. Verification of the facts costs what their witnesses and their indices actually require. If for one color the total cost is $O(M^{1/k-\epsilon})$ on infinitely many true scales, a public bounded version of the verifier gives an ordinary 3-CNF whose SAT decision under $O(N^k)$ takes $o(M)$. The false color never has a chart passing both checks. The native signed theorem gives a contradiction for $k=1,2,3$. The additive cost of enumerating schedules fits all three thresholds. A compressed promise about the first $e+1$ signals still needs a sound way to certify their positions or their consequences. [Ordinary packed compiler](packed-band-transfer.md)

## Why checking the real clock entries individually is too expensive

Fix two never-signaling, nonhalting program texts paired with the two different requested colors. Both eventually become active and neither ever retires. At every sufficiently late stage, no smaller rank than either fixed rank still wins: there are only finitely many such ranks and each can win only once. Fix the actual history before this stage, as well as every raw entry at this stage except that of the fixed rank requesting the opposite of the true sign. Changing just that entry to one gives this earlier rank the award and flips the sign. This argument holds for the actual schedule at late stages of both colors, including the rare stages. Thus every independent disclosure certificate needs this true zero, even given the entire earlier history for free.

If a trial is exposed only by point queries asking whether a pulse occurred at one of its allotted clock positions, every one of its $\lfloor M/2^{e+1}\rfloor$ positions must be queried on a sound accepting transcript for that sign. Otherwise place one pulse at an unqueried position in that same trial. The other data and the answers on the transcript agree but the sign changes. With statistical completeness $p$ and soundness $s<p$, fixing the true-input prover and coupling its coins with each one-pulse modification gives at least $(p-s)\lfloor M/2^{e+1}\rfloor$ expected distinct probes on the true run. The result allows any finite collection of permanently known silent texts to be exempt: choose two later never-signaling, never-halting texts of the two colors outside that list. There are infinitely many syntactically different ones.

Even a computably enumerable catalog of correct permanent silence claims $(e,i_0)$, meaning no timely raw signal for any $i\ge i_0$, must miss a never-signaling rank of each color. Given arbitrary machine $P$, compile a detector that simulates $P$ on its specified input up to $i$ steps and signals if it halts, idling forever otherwise. Halting gives a timely signal at every sufficiently large scale. If the catalog had an entry for every such detector built from a nonhalting $P$, waiting for its rank to appear would semidecide nonhalting. This argument concerns permanent claims. A proof about one stage can use its source code and certify silence directly. The [synchronized-trials argument](synchronized-priority-trials.md) tests that stronger possibility on the actual priority program.
