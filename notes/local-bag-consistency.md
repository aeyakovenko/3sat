# How much do joint gate tests improve the gap?

The [referee game](referee-certificates.md) has a circuit of h copy-and-OR stages where the wrong output satisfies every individual gate inequality to within O(2⁻ʰ). Suppose a prover must instead give a joint distribution for every set of at most k gate variables, supported entirely on assignments obeying the locally present gates and pins. Measure the error by the largest total variation distance between any two distributions projected to their common coordinates. On this circuit the best error is of order k/h for 3≤k≪h, and has an exact value for infinitely many sizes. This is a statement about the maximum marginal inconsistency, before any conversion into a bounded game payoff.

## A robustness estimate for a tree decomposition

Use bags containing every constraint scope and satisfying the running intersection property on a tree. Thus all bags containing a given variable form a connected subtree. Assign each constraint to one containing bag. For an arbitrary distribution at each bag b, let p_b be its probability of violating at least one constraint assigned there. For each tree edge e let δ_e be the total variation distance between the two distributions on its shared coordinates. If the constraints have no global solution, then

    1 ≤ Σ_b p_b + Σ_e δ_e.

To see this, draw the root assignment with its designated law. For each parent-child edge, maximally couple the two designated separator laws, conditional on the already sampled parent separator. Extend the sampled child separator by the child's conditional law. Choose arbitrary values for zero-probability conditioning events. Every node still has its specified marginal, and the separator mismatch probability is exactly δ_e. If all edges agree, the running intersection property makes the assignments a single global assignment. Unsatisfiability forces at least one mismatch or local violation, giving the inequality by the union bound. Exact gluing on trees and exactness of suitable Sherali–Adams relaxations at bounded treewidth are classical. [Wainwright and Jordan](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~jordan/sail/readings/wainwright-jordan-fnt.pdf) · [Bienstock and Ozbay](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1572528604000039)

There is also a fixed choice of one event per separator which works for every collection of bag distributions. Orient the tree away from its root. On an edge e define F_e to be the separator assignments that have **no** satisfying extension in the entire child subtree. Add an empty parent separator at the root, whose sole assignment belongs to F because the instance is unsatisfiable. For each deterministic bag assignment, the indicator of F on its parent separator is at most the indicator of a local violation plus the sum of the F indicators on its child separators. Indeed, if the bag is locally valid and each child admits an extension, the extensions can be pasted along the bag. Take expectations per bag and sum. The resulting bound is the display above with each δ_e replaced by the signed difference Pr_parent(F_e)−Pr_child(F_e). The events can be computed by ordinary bottom-up dynamic programming. Giving every event as a table can take O(B·2ᵏ) bits for B bags of size at most k; the argument supplies no uniformly short circuit for all of those tables on the hierarchy computation.

## Matching bounds for the copy-and-OR circuit

There are variables x₀,…,xₕ and y₀,…,yₕ₋₁, with pins x₀=0 and xₕ=1. The gates impose yᵢ=xᵢ AND 1 and xᵢ₊₁=xᵢ OR yᵢ. The constant one is a literal; the copy gate has two variable positions. Let ℓ=⌊(k−1)/2⌋ and assume 1≤ℓ<h. Let η be the infimum of the worst pairwise overlap distance over systems of legal bag distributions. Then

    1/(⌈h/ℓ⌉−1) ≤ η ≤ min(1, ℓ/(h−ℓ)).

For the lower bound, partition the h stages consecutively into q=⌈h/ℓ⌉ blocks, each of at most ℓ stages. Every block uses at most 2ℓ+1 variables. Its bag law gives all its x variables the same bit with probability one. Write pᵢ for this bit's probability of one in block i. The endpoint pins give p₁=0 and p_q=1. Neighboring blocks share their boundary x, so each difference |pᵢ₊₁−pᵢ| is at most the maximum overlap error. Telescoping gives the lower bound.

For the upper bound choose a uniform cut J∈{1,…,h}, set xᵢ=1 exactly when i≥J, and set yᵢ=xᵢ. The pins and all copies hold. Only the OR gate at stage J−1 fails. For each bag separately condition J to avoid exactly the stages whose complete OR scope occurs there. Any t≥1 such scopes use t distinct y variables and at least t+1 distinct x variables. The bag therefore excludes at most ℓ cut positions, and every remaining assignment is locally legal. Consider the two sets A and C of cuts allowed for two bags. The total variation distance between their uniform laws is 1−|A∩C|/max(|A|,|C|). Its numerator after taking this common denominator is at most ℓ, and its denominator at least h−ℓ. Mapping both cut choices to bits on the overlap can only decrease that distance. This works for arbitrary pairs of bags, including nonconsecutive groups of gates. When ℓ divides h the bounds coincide at 1/(h/ℓ−1). For ordinary equality links xᵢ=xᵢ₊₁ the identical proof uses ℓ=k−1. [Finite arithmetic check in Lean](../lean/LocalBagConsistency.lean)

Some nondivisible cases have the same exact lower endpoint. For ordinary equalities write h=mℓ+r with 1≤r<ℓ. There are q=m+1 runs of candidate cuts {jℓ+1,…,jℓ+r}, for 0≤j≤m. If r=ℓ−1, put uniform mass on the qr listed cuts. Any k=ℓ+1 vertices cover at most r of these edges. Covering r+1=ℓ would use at least two runs and hence at least ℓ+2 vertices. Thus each bag excludes at most 1/q of the prior cut probability. Conditional laws on the allowed sets then have distance at most (1/q)/(1−1/q)=1/(q−1), by the same uniform-set calculation. For copy-and-OR, the same conclusion holds when k=2ℓ+1 and r=ℓ−1, since covering ℓ OR scopes in two runs uses at least 2ℓ+2 variables. Together with the divisible case, this covers every h in range for copy-and-OR at k=5 and for ordinary equalities at k=3.

The sufficient condition on prior mass has an exact obstruction. The remaining h−r stages after the first r partition into m contiguous ℓ-stage blocks, each coverable by one bag. Hence the first r cuts must carry at least 1/q altogether if all bags forbid at most 1/q. For ordinary equalities, a bag containing the first r edges and any further edge uses at most r+3 vertices. If r≤ℓ−2 this forces every remaining edge to have zero mass, a contradiction. For copy-and-OR the same argument costs at most 2r+4 variables, so it applies whenever 2r+4≤k. This obstruction concerns the mass condition, not every system of bag laws.

A rounded-grid proposal also fails for some nondivisible sizes. On an ordinary chain with h=10, k=4, set the probability of one at coordinate i in bag B to min(3,⌈(i−e_B(i))/3⌉)/3, where e_B(i) counts the fully present edges ending at or before i. This contracts every internal bag edge and fixes both pins. Couple all coordinates of each bag by one uniform monotone threshold. On their shared coordinates (4,8), bags {3,4,8} and {4,5,6,8} have one-probabilities (1/3,1) and (2/3,2/3). Their overlap distributions have total variation 2/3, above the target 1/3. Numerical LP minimization, using every bag of at most k variables and every event on every overlap, found the lower endpoint at (h,k)=(5,3),(7,3),(7,4),(8,4),(10,3) for ordinary equality chains. The (7,4) result falls outside the proved cases. Whether the lower endpoint always holds is unresolved here.

If an LP compares both bags to a separately named separator marginal with tolerance η per comparison, direct pairwise discrepancies can be at most 2η by the triangle inequality. A per-coordinate tolerance on individual separator assignment probabilities can also be weaker than total variation by a factor exponential in separator size. These conventions matter before using a marginal gap in the referee game.

For this circuit, even testing every constant-size bag pair with exact local support leaves inverse-length inconsistencies possible. A tree decomposition supplies a positive inverse-bag-count guarantee, but the known tableau for the actual coded source has exponentially many gates. With a hypothetical O(m) SAT solver on m encoded bits, a quasilinear encoding of one sampled empirical predicate of cost H would take H·polylog(H) deterministic time. This allows H up to 2ⁿpoly(n) in the earlier verifier, compared with 2^(n/3)poly(n) under cubic SAT. The path calculation still concerns inconsistent local distributions on a small, explicitly evaluable circuit. It supplies neither suitable separator descriptions nor an empirical referee within this larger budget for the actual hierarchy computation, and yields no exclusion of linear-time SAT.
