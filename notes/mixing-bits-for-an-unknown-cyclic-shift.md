# A cyclic shifter from a finite rational matrix calculation

For some absolute $\delta>0$, the function $(x,s)\mapsto (x_{j+s\bmod n})_{j<n}$ for $n=2^k$ has bounded-fan-in Boolean circuits of size $O(n(1+k)^{1-\delta})$. The bits of $s$ are ordinary input wires. The argument below uses a finite binary XOR network, rational matrices with dyadic entries, and arithmetic on public address labels. It gives a separate proof of a weaker exponent than the recent finite-Fourier paper's convolution theorem. An application to ordinary SAT encodes this specific unknown address operation as a proper named formula of length $o(nk^2)$, using the local digit construction. An unrestricted SAT running-time lower bound would require a different conclusion. [Local digit compiler](locally-encoding-an-unknown-xor-shift.md)

The architecture of changing address coordinates along a finite mixing network comes from the explicit Fourier and tape work, the characteristic-swapped zeta construction, and the independent undirected network-coding work. In particular Li, Li and Liu give a different rational-matrix rank certificate in their Lemma 5.3. We give the finite certificate used here explicitly. Using indefinite rational labels, rather than relying on Euclidean embeddings or a field change in a Fourier kernel, is convenient for the particular incidence table below. The contribution here is the direct transfer from a finite rank defect to a shift by a **variable** cyclic address with binary output data, including the carry and shared-parameter accounting. ## The finite scalar computation

Take $h=22$, $d=h+1$, $v=\binom h3=1540$ and $m=d^3$. Let $D=\mathbb Q^{d}$ with symmetric bilinear form $\operatorname{diag}(I_h,-1)$. For each three-subset $T$ of $[h]$ set $t_T=(1_T,1)$ and write $\ell_T$ for its line. The inner product is $|S\cap T|-1$, and $\langle t_T,t_T\rangle=2$. Call distinct triples neighbors if their intersection has size one. Their lines are then orthogonal and their sum is nondegenerate. All of these decompositions and their dimensions persist over $\mathbb F_3$: the norms are units and every complementary summand of a nondegenerate subspace is nondegenerate.

Data values live in $\mathbb F_2$. Let $G$ be the $h$-by-$v$ incidence matrix of elements and triples; put $R=G^{\mathsf T}$. The $(S,T)$ entry of $RG$ is $|S\cap T|\bmod2$. Hence $RG=I+N$ for the neighbor adjacency matrix $N$. One local invocation has $v$ source bits $x_T$, $v$ target bits $y_S$, one auxiliary $a_{ST}$ for each ordered neighboring pair, and $h$ center auxiliaries $c$. Let $(Vx)_{ST}=x_T$ and $(Ja)_S=\sum_{T\sim S}a_{ST}$, so $JV=N$. Perform these eight updates in order, each using the old values on its right:
\[
 y{+}{=}Ja,\quad y{+}{=}Rc,\quad a{+}{=}Vx,\quad c{+}{=}Gx,\quad
 y{+}{=}Rc,\quad y{+}{=}Ja,\quad c{+}{=}Gx,\quad a{+}{=}Vx .
\]
The net change to $y$ is $(RG+JV)x=x$; every auxiliary regains its initial value. Each update uses binary XORs with fixed finite fan-in expansion. In a side update, group each $S$ and its incident side auxiliaries, or each $T$ and its incident side auxiliaries, according to which bank is touched. A center update groups its affected bank with the center. All subgates of a group have the common label specified below, so its temporary binary-XOR implementation carries no rank cost internally.

Give each triple of triples an $X,Y$ pair in a $v^3$ grid. In the first coordinate perform the forward invocations from $X$ to $Y$ for each fixing of the other coordinates. In coordinate two perform the inverse invocations from $Y$ to $X$, with update order reversed; in coordinate three again use forward invocations from $X$ to $Y$. By the local identity, at every position the successive pair values are $(X,Y+X),(Y,Y+X),(Y,X)$. Each invocation gets its own fresh center and side wires initialized with arbitrary data, which are all restored. At every stage the order in which physical wire groups are touched is $Y$-side, $Y$-center, $X$-side, $X$-center, $Y$-center, $Y$-side, $X$-center, $X$-side. In the reverse invocation the local side indices are transposed, which preserves the neighbor relation.

## Matrices on nodes and their cost

Let $\mathcal H=D^{\otimes3}$ with its tensor bilinear form. Each node label is a nondegenerate subspace $U$ represented by its rational projection $P_U$ along $U^\perp$. For $U\subseteq V$ the rank of $P_V-P_U$ equals $\dim V-\dim U$. This holds for the rational form above regardless of signature, because $V=U\perp(V\cap U^\perp)$.

In stage $j=1,2,3$, write $E$ for the tensor of factors before $j$ and $F$ for the tensor after it. For a fixed invocation, the marked triple lines in preceding factors specify a line $P\subseteq E$, with $B=P^\perp\subseteq E$, and the following lines specify $Q\subseteq F$. An empty product is a one-dimensional scalar space. Below **every displayed label is tensored with $Q$**. Group labels for the eight updates, numbered $0,\ldots,7$, are
\[
\begin{array}{c|l}
0 & B\otimes \ell_S\\
1,4 & B\otimes D\\
2 & (B\otimes D)\perp(P\otimes\ell_T)\\
3,6,7 & E\otimes D\\
5 & (B\otimes D)\perp(P\otimes\ell_S^\perp).
\end{array}
\]
Here the physical $X$ index is $T$, the physical $Y$ index is $S$. A bank entry with own line $\ell$ has incoming label $E\otimes\ell$ on $X$, $B\otimes\ell$ on $Y$. Its outgoing labels are $E\otimes D$ on $X$ and $(P\otimes\ell)^\perp_{E\otimes D}$ on $Y$. Replacing $E$ by $E\otimes D$, $P$ by $P\otimes\ell$ shows these are exactly the next stage's incoming labels after including the next factor of $Q$. In the first stage the sources are the line $U$ of all three marked factors on $X$ and zero on $Y$; the last sinks are $\mathcal H$ on $X$ and $U^\perp$ on $Y$. Each auxiliary starts at zero label and ends at $\mathcal H$.

To check all ranks, $X$ visits rows $2,3,6,7$, monotonically, and $Y$ visits rows $0,1,4,5$, monotonically. A side auxiliary visits $0,2,5,7$ monotonically because $\ell_T\subseteq\ell_S^\perp$ on neighbor pairs. A center auxiliary visits $1,3,4,6$; its single decline from $E\otimes D$ to $B\otimes D$ has dimension $d$, including the one-dimensional factor $Q$. Every used space is an orthogonal sum of tensor products of nondegenerate factors, built from $B,P$, $Q,Q^\perp$ and either one triple line and its complement or the two orthogonal neighbor lines and their common complement. This verifies the asserted projections and rank differences also in characteristic three. Telescoping the endpoint dimensions therefore charges each physical bank wire $m-1$, each side wire $m$, and each center wire $m+2d$. Let $W$ be the total number of independent scalar wires, and $S$ the sum of differences along every edge. Since every stage uses $v^2$ invocations and each center has $h$ entries,
\[
                 S=Wm-2v^3+6v^2hd<Wm
\]
by $v=1540>3hd=1518$. This strict inequality is exact; the many side wires cancel from the difference.

For an actual source–sink pair, the bank exchange pairs $0$ with $\mathcal H$ or $U$ with $U^\perp$. The resulting difference of projection matrices is $I$ or $I-2P_U$, respectively; $(I-2P_U)^2=I$. The auxiliaries give $I$. Every endpoint difference is invertible. The labels have denominators powers of two: a marked line projection divides by 2, an orthogonal complement subtracts its projector from the identity, and products and orthogonal sums use products and sums of the projectors. Thus every label matrix makes sense modulo $3^f$ at every $f\ge1$.

## From edge ranks to unknown digit translations

Write $R_f=\mathbb Z/3^f\mathbb Z$. For a vector $b$ let $(\tau_b x)(u)=x(u+b)$ for data indexed by $u\in R_f^m$. Work on $W$ arbitrary bit arrays sharing a vector of **unknown** shifts $s\in R_f^m$. Give a gate labeled $U$ the frame $\tau_{P_U s}$. A framed change along the edge $U\to V$ is the translation $\tau_{(P_V-P_U)s}$. Pointwise XOR and a common address translation commute, so intermediate frames cancel. Relative to its input, each output is translated by $(P_{\rm out}-P_{\rm in})s$. The initial and final changes of variable for that paired wire are public: for $A=P_{\rm out}-P_{\rm in}$ use $g(u)=x(A^{-1}u)$ at the input, then read the output at $Au$. This produces $x(u+s)$ for every role, even when the marked source frame is nonzero. The scalar permutation of roles is also public.

For any edge matrix $H=P_V-P_U$, its rank $r$ over $\mathbb Q$ equals its rank over $\mathbb F_3$, by the direct-summand dimension calculation. Choose $r$ columns and $r$ rows with minor determinant a unit modulo 3. Over the rationals one has $H=H_{:,J}(H_{I,J})^{-1}H_{I,:}$, and this reduces unchanged to $R_f$. This is a sum of $r$ products $u_i v_i^{\mathsf T}$, where each $u_i$ has a unit coordinate. Public changes of basis over $R_f$ send such a $u_i$ to a coordinate vector: exchange its unit coordinate into the first spot, scale that unit and clear the other coordinates. Consequently each factor's translation by $u_i(v_i^{\mathsf T}s)$ is a single scalar cyclic shift of length $3^f$ on all its fibers. The shorter shift parameter is **the same on every fiber**. Computing it from the $m$ unknown digit controls costs $\operatorname{poly}(f)$ Boolean gates, since $m$ is fixed and constant rational coefficients can be reduced modulo $3^f$ in the public circuit description. A zero-rank edge needs no shift. Outside the $S$ directional calls, a fixed number of pointwise and wiring operations has linear Boolean gate cost in array volume.

## Carries, exact batches, and the clock recurrence

A cyclic shift by $s$ on $n=3^k$ bits uses base-$3^f$ digit additions. Set $f=\lfloor k/m\rfloor$. The $m$ full digits have $f$ trits and, if present, one highest short digit has $r=k-mf<m$ trits. First do the independent digit shifts, without carries, operating on all full digits by the matrix scheme and on the bounded high digit by ordinary multiplexers. All $r$ trits are spectators during the full-digit scheme.

For the array $z(j)=x(D_s(j))$ after these independent digit additions, define $c_i(j)=[j_{\rm low}+s_{\rm low}\ge 3^{fi}]$ for the carry into a noninitial digit $i$, using the original less significant digits. Define $E(j)$ by adding just $c_i(j)$ to each digit independently, omitting a final overflow. Then $D_s(E(j))=j+s\bmod 3^k$. As an address map, compose these conditional **fixed one-position** digit shifts from highest to lowest to keep every carry test's lower digits original. Array pullbacks execute those single-digit maps in the reverse order. Each is a fixed adjacent-source choice controlled by its carry, costing $O(n)$ gates for all data indices. All predicates comparing the unknown $fi$-trit threshold $3^{fi}-s_{\rm low}$ to every public lower index use $O(3^{fi}+\operatorname{poly}(k))$ gates: compute the threshold digits and its overflow flag as usual, then grow the ternary comparison prefix tree with a constant amount of work per node. There are at most $m$ carry positions. Every outside cost, including conversion of control representations, is $O_m(n)$ on a batch of any fixed size. The same input parameter is shared across the batch.

Make the batch size $W_*=3^a=L W+b$ for a sufficiently large fixed $a$. Run $L$ copies of the finite scheme and ordinary independent $m$ digit shifts on the $b<W$ remaining arrays. The total directional count is $S_*=LS+bm$, with $S_*/W_*<m$. For all sufficiently large $k$ a direction's $3^{k-f}$ length-$3^f$ fibers divide into exactly $3^{k-f-a}$ batches of $W_*$ fibers. Each such batch shares the same shorter parameter as required. Let $T(k)$ count gates for a whole batch of genuine cyclic shifts with a common $k$-trit shift, using trivial circuits for bounded base widths. For large $k$ the fully charged recurrence is
\[
             T(k)\le C W_*3^k+S_*3^{k-f-a}T(f).
\]
After division by $W_*3^k$ the multiplier is $\lambda=S_*/W_*<m$. Unrolling yields $T(k)=O(3^k(1+k)^\theta)$ for some fixed $\theta<1$ (take $\log_m\lambda$ if $\lambda>1$). A single shift initializes the other $W_*-1$ arrays to constants. For an ordinary binary length $N=2^b$ choose the smallest $3^k\ge2N$. In a ternary array set positions $0,\ldots,2N-1$ to two consecutive copies of the $N$ input values and the rest to zero. For every $j,s<N$, position $j+s$ is valid without wraparound in this larger array and gives exactly $x_{j+s\bmod N}$. Convert the shared binary shift to ternary in $\operatorname{poly}(b)$ gates and retain the first $N$ outputs. Fixed rearrangements are wiring, and bounded fan-out can be enforced by binary copy trees with linear overhead in the number of gate uses and outputs.

The finite certificate is an algebraic existence argument. The first width where its upper bound beats an ordinary binary shifter can be extremely large. It imposes no limitation on arbitrary RAM solvers. ## A Boolean circuit printer and the named ordinary file

For any requested binary address width $r$, a deterministic printer constructs this particular gate list in $2^{O(r)}$ time. The finite incidence, labels, unit minors, and directions are constants independent of $r$. Hardwire them or compute them once by exact finite arithmetic. At every recursive node the address modulus has $O(r)$ bits. Compute all fixed coordinate changes, basis inverses and scalar coefficients modulo that power by elementary polynomial-time integer operations. To expand a public subarray permutation, enumerate its inputs, compute their related indices in $\operatorname{poly}(r)$ time each, and keep the resulting wire labels in arrays. Each local bit operation, including the circuit for a shared modular linear form in the unknown shift, has an explicit polynomial-time constructor. All batches and recursion sizes are public. The recurrence charges linear local volume per node and a total $O(2^r(1+r)^\theta)$ over nodes, after the ternary padding and final selection. Allowing $\operatorname{poly}(r)$ extra printing work per charged operation, plus polynomial work on the $O(r)$-bit gate indices, still gives $2^{O(r)}$. This is a printer for the smaller exponent proved here, with $\theta<1$ as above.

Consequently the local digit compiler can take $r=\max(1,\lfloor\log_2(2+k)\rfloor)$ instead of searching for optimal templates. Its total is $O(2^k(1+k)/(1+\log(2+k))^{1-\theta})$ gates, ordinary encoded length $O(2^k(1+k)^2/(1+\log(2+k))^{1-\theta})$, and packed emission time bounded by that gate count plus polynomial setup in $k$. Use the printed small kernel on the shorter last digit as well. A polynomial number of public slabs, polynomial-time individual bit addressing, and physical degree $O(1+\log(2+k))$ follow from the fixed microgate slabs and carry trees. All these estimates are for a single fixed circuit constructor, with constants determined by the displayed finite gadget. There is a different trade if a $2^{o(k)}$-time raw bit indexer is allowed. For $k$ large take $r=\lfloor k/\log_2 k\rfloor$. In each stage use one slab dimension for the microgate ordinal of the fixed $r$-bit kernel, padded to a power of two, instead of one separate slab per microgate. Its $O(r+\log r)$ header bits select the gate type and local operand ports; even an arbitrary lookup on these bits has Boolean degree $O(r+\log r)$. Use wire names with this local ordinal in a separate field and affine unchanged fiber indices, and expose a stage's output bank under its own tagged names before the next stage. Dummy padded template entries may define private fixed bits by the same fixed clause profile. Output and initial input references insert the kernel's local data-coordinate port, with at most $r$ varying bits. Thus this alternative has $\operatorname{poly}(k)$ intervals of degree $O(r+\log k)=o(k)$. Its gate count is $O(2^k(1+k)^\theta(1+\log k)^{1-\theta})$; its small template and an individual requested bit can be computed in $2^{O(r)}=2^{o(k)}$ steps. Prebuilding the template once and traversing its fixed stages and copy fibers gives packed production within the gate budget plus $2^{O(r)}$ setup. No polynomial-time raw-bit lookup is claimed for this wider choice.

## The conditional lower bound for the noncyclic bit shifter

Afshani, Freksen, Kamma and Larsen's ICALP 2019 Theorem 2, under the undirected multiple-unicast conjecture, gives $\Omega(n\log n)$ **Boolean gates** at bounded fan-in and fan-out for the ordinary shift: given independent $n$ bits and a binary selector $\ell\in\{1,\ldots,n\}$, output $2n$ bits with the input beginning at position $\ell$ and zeros elsewhere. Their proof explicitly includes the zero condition. Set $N$ to the least power of two at least $2n$, put the input in the first $n$ positions of an $N$-bit zero-padded array, and apply the cyclic gather with scalar $-(\ell-1)\bmod N$. The nonzero window cannot wrap, since its last zero-based position is at most $2n-2<N$. Reading the first $2n$ positions gives exactly their output. Conversion of the binary selector to the signed modular offset costs $O(\log n)$ gates. Expanding unbounded wire uses into binary copy trees costs linear overhead since each gate has bounded fan-in and there are $O(n)$ outputs. Hence the sub-$n\log n$ shifter here is also an explicit Boolean-circuit route to the failure of that conjecture. Two October 2026 manuscripts had already given independent network-coding counterexamples. Refuting the conjecture alone would not supply this circuit.
