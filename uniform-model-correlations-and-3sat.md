# 3-SAT hardness when a uniformly random solution has nearly independent bits

Consider proper 3-CNFs. On a positive file take the **uniform distribution over all satisfying assignments**, including the auxiliary variables. Require each occurring variable to be exactly unbiased and every distinct pair to have absolute correlation at most $\delta$, where the sign of bit $x$ is $(-1)^x$. A decision procedure must distinguish every such positive file from every unsatisfiable file.

* For every fixed $0<\varepsilon<1$ this promise is NP-hard at $\delta=M^{-1+\varepsilon}$ for $M$ clauses. The same holds with the encoded bit length in the exponent. At $\delta=c/M$ for each fixed $c$, the parity-defect algorithm gives a deterministic polynomial-time promise procedure.
* For some $a>0$, circuits for this uniform-solution promise with $\delta=(\log_2 N)^{-a}$ require $\Omega(N(\log N)^a)$ bounded-fan-in gates at every sufficiently large byte-aligned DIMACS length $N$. The gate lower bound uses the November 24 list theorem.
* At any fixed positive $\delta$, a linear-time, linear-encoded-length reduction preserves the general SAT deadlines $O(N),O(N^2),O(N^3)$. The reductions do not establish any of those time exclusions. The circuit-hard projection below even has a globally correct SAT RAM running in $O(N)$ on its entire image.

The earlier compiler gives the near-independence property for a chosen distribution. Under uniform sampling its adjacent state pairs have correlation at least $31/64$ in absolute value. Here the number of extensions of a legal transition changes its probability under uniform sampling, and each new physical bit stays exactly fair. Priority for this quantitative uniform-model theorem is unestablished. There is a direct link to the ambiguity question. If there are $K$ solutions and the uniform distribution has fair singletons, then $K$ is even. For any pair its signed correlation is $4s/K-1$, where $s$ counts solutions setting both bits to zero. Thus below bias $2/K$ all such correlations must vanish. Then every satisfying assignment meets the odd parity equations, and solving those equations suffices for promised decision. Exact pairwise independence is compatible with exponentially small solution density. For $v=2^r-1$ variables indexed by the nonzero $r$-bit vectors, impose $x_u=x_{u-2^j}\oplus x_{2^j}$ on each non-power-of-two index $u$, clearing its highest bit $2^j$. Each is a proper ternary XOR. For $r\ge2$ all variables occur and there are exactly $2^r=v+1$ models, the evaluations of the nonzero linear functionals on a uniform seed. The constant and the $v$ sign columns are mutually orthogonal on the model set, so $v+1$ is the minimum at those lengths. [One-sort decision at two solutions](one-sort-for-bounded-ambiguity-sat.md)

## A ternary constraint with four or two extensions

Fix three old literal truth values $l,y,z$. If $l=0$ require $y=z$, using the two clauses $(l\lor\neg y\lor z)$ and $(l\lor y\lor\neg z)$. Call $l=y\oplus z$ the preferred case. The other legal case is $l=1$, $y=z$.

Introduce six fresh bits $A,B,C,D,E,F$. Impose
$$
 F=E\oplus l,\qquad D=B\oplus y,\qquad D=C\oplus z .
$$
Each equation takes four proper ternary clauses. On $(A,B,F)$ allow the four even-parity triples and $100$. On $(A,C,E)$ allow the four even-parity triples and $010$. Each of these tests takes three proper clauses excluding its three forbidden words. Including the two old clauses, this is a twenty-clause relation on nine bits. A complemented old endpoint is substituted literally in all occurrences.

In the preferred case $C=B\oplus l$ and the two residual tests have the same parity. They cannot both use their exceptional triples, which require opposite values of $A$. Thus $B,E$ are independently free, $A=B\oplus E\oplus l$, $D=B\oplus y$ and $F=E\oplus l$. In the other legal case $C=B$ and the two tests have opposite parity. If the first takes its exception then $A=1,B=0,E=1,D=y$. If the second takes its exception then $A=0,B=1,E=0,D=1\oplus y$. The extensions are bitwise complements on all six new coordinates. Every auxiliary bit is fair conditional on **any** legal old triple, under uniform extension.

In the preferred case the masks on $(A,B,C,D,E,F)$ are respectively $(B\oplus E,B,B,B,E,E)$, up to fixed offsets from the old bits. A pair with different masks has zero conditional sign product. A pair with equal masks has conditional sign product equal to one old sign. Indeed the pairs among $B,C,D$ have differences $l,y,l\oplus y=z$, and $E,F$ have difference $l$. This last property prevents the multiplicities from introducing a permanently correlated auxiliary pair. ## The logical formula and its physical clauses

Given a proper source formula, take $t\ge1$ copies. Each position $i$ has an activation $a_i$ and local source variables. As in the earlier compiler, include an odd-negative source clause unguarded and an even-negative clause guarded by $\neg a_i$. For each local source variable $x$ also include $a_i\lor\neg x$. With activation zero the whole block has exactly the all-zero logical assignment. With activation one its local assignments are precisely the original models.

Enforce exactly one activation by scanning in **both** directions. In each scan set the initial parity bit to zero and the final bit to one. At position $i$ enforce $p_{\rm next}=p_{\rm prev}\oplus a_i$. Make a separate logical copy $p'=p_{\rm prev}$ and forbid $(p_{\rm prev},p',a_i)=111$. Once the scan has seen a one it cannot see a second one, and its terminal value forces a first. The opposite scan has the same logical verdict. Using both scans makes the number of extensions independent of the selected position, as proved below. Use distinct bits for successive boundaries and the copy in each scan. For $t=1$ the initial and terminal bits are still different coordinates.

Represent each logical bit by the XOR of its own three physical shares. For a hard linear equation on distinct logical bits, take all of their shares in three successive layers, one share per group in each layer. Use a cyclic sequence of state bits with exact XOR increments given by the raw bits. Identify the closing state with the initial state or its complement according to the required parity. Four proper ternary clauses impose each link. This works for a pin, an equality and a three-bit equation, with exactly two state completions on legal raw values.

Each other logical test forbids one tuple on $q=2,3$ or $4$ distinct groups. Its forbidden tuple has odd weight, including $01$ for the inactive pin and $111$ for the scan comparator. Enumerate its $4^q$ forbidden raw words $p$. For each use a fresh complemented state cycle on the $s=3q$ shares in layer order. The literal $l_j=x_j\oplus p_j$ says that coordinate differs from the raw forbidden word. At the link from $y_j$ to $y_{j+1}$ impose the conditional relation in the previous section with $k$ independent sets of its six auxiliary bits. The two clauses imposing the hard implication can be shared. A nonzero literal vector has a state extension by flipping on one of its true coordinates. A zero vector cannot reach the complemented closing state. All physical triples use three distinct variables.

These tests prove the existential verdict equivalence directly. They also specify the multiplicity of each extension, which affects uniform sampling.

## Distribution induced by the uniform measure

Let $d_j=y_j\oplus y_{j+1}$ with the complemented closing state included. Each legal path flips at an odd subset of its true literal positions. Call a true position with $d_j=0$ an omission. Put $\lambda=2^{-k}$. Conditional on the shares, the weight of a path in the uniform solution measure is proportional to $\lambda^e$, where $e$ is its number of omissions. This follows from $4^k$ extensions on a preferred link and $2^k$ on an omitted link. The initial state is still an independent fair bit, since the multiplicity only sees the increments.

In one passing logical test the product of the partition functions of all $4^q$ raw-pattern cycles depends only on the decoded bits. In fact a change of raw encodings preserving the decoded values translates the forbidden raw fiber to itself and permutes the $4^q$ difference words. The wide cycles have disjoint private states. Thus their product stays constant even if other logical tests share some raw bits. Hard XOR cycles always have two extensions. Conditional on **all** decoded values, the shares of different groups are consequently independent and each group is uniform on its four raw encodings.

The selected position is uniform as well. Fix a source model at the active position. At any inactive position the two preceding scan parities, in forward and reverse order, are zero and one in some order. Its two comparators therefore see the tuple $000$ once and $110$ once. The active position sees $001$ twice. All other local tests at inactive positions see the same fixed values, and all hard scans have constant multiplicity. The product of all extension counts for this source model is independent of its position. Sum over source models. Their relative weights need not agree.

Whenever a local test is in its inactive position its raw truth vector in **each** cycle has odd parity. For the source tests and inactive pins the all-zero decoded tuple differs from the odd forbidden tuple in odd parity. For a comparator the parity of $(p,p',a_i)$ is exactly $a_i$ because of the duplicate. With an odd literal vector, taking no omissions is legal, and any nonempty legal omission set has even cardinality. Hence the chance of any omission in that cycle conditional on the shares and inactive decoded values is at most $2^{12}\lambda^2$, by summing the weights of all nonempty possibilities relative to the no-omission choice. For a fixed local cycle, let
$$
                   P_t=1/t+2^{12}\lambda^2 .
$$
The probability that this cycle is active or has an inactive omission is at most $P_t$. In particular, each link is omitted with probability at most $P_t$.

Every old share is exactly fair, as is every state even given all shares, by its independent initial bit. Two distinct shares have zero correlation. A state paired with a share or a state of another cycle has zero correlation, using independence of the initial bits given the shares and increments. Two different hard-cycle states have zero correlation because the layer order ensures the interval between them splits a share group, whose proper nonempty subset has fair XOR conditional on its decoded value. For two states of one conditional cycle, on the inactive sector compare the interval parity of their increments with the ideal interval parity of all its literals. The latter has zero mean by the same split-group argument *before conditioning on any omission event*. Replacing it can change the sign expectation by at most twice the omission probability. An active sector has total mass $1/t$. Thus the absolute old pair moments are at most $1/t+2^{13}\lambda^2$.

Now take any new auxiliary bit. It is fair conditional on the old assignment by the explicit extension table. Its pair with an old bit or a bit from another copy of the link gadget has zero correlation by conditional independence. Within one gadget consider two distinct auxiliary bits. On preferred triples their conditional product mean is either zero everywhere or agrees everywhere with a fixed old literal sign. In the latter case that old sign has mean zero **without conditioning on the link**, and the difference of conditional means has magnitude at most two on omitted triples. In both cases the unconditional pair bias is at most $2P_t$. This proves exact fairness of every physical coordinate and pair bias at most $2/t+2^{13}4^{-k}$ for the **uniform distribution on all satisfying assignments**. Choosing $k=\lceil\log_4(8192t)\rceil$ makes the bound at most $3/t$. There is also a sharper local observation. The identities $B\oplus D=y$, $C\oplus D=z$, $E\oplus F=l$ and $B\oplus C=y\oplus z$ hold on *all* legal extensions. Other distinct-mask pairs contribute zero on preferred triples and have absolute mean at most the omission probability. Since $l$ is unbiased, $\mathbb E(-1)^{y\oplus z}=2\Pr[\mathrm{omission}]$. Thus inserting the six bits does not increase the maximum of the old pair biases. [Finite partition and two-scan checks](uniform_pair_bias_filter_audit.py)

## Bit length, circuits and what can be concluded about RAM time

With $h\ge1$ source clauses after deduplication and at most $3h$ occurring variables, the physical formula has $M=O(th\log(t+2))$ clauses and $O(M)$ variables. Write it in $O(M\log(M+2))$ bits. At fixed $t$ this is linear time and length in original DIMACS bits by the digit-trie and clause-deduplication argument in the earlier note. Taking $t$ a sufficiently large polynomial power of $h+1$ gives $3/t\le M^{-1+\varepsilon}$ for every fixed $\varepsilon>0$. A strict exponent margin absorbs either logarithmic encoding factor. At the inverse-clause-count endpoint the bound on the expected number of even clauses applies to the uniform witness distribution itself, giving the stated promise algorithm.

For the circuit assertion take the fixed-layout list-to-SAT projection at width $w$. Its $H=\Theta(2^w)$ physical source clauses have fixed signs and token boundaries, and its varying decimal digits are copies of list input bits. Keep the physical clauses. All added inactive pins can be emitted for the whole public interval of occurring source variables, and all scan and filter clauses have fixed types. Label the $3t$ original-variable shares by appending decimal digits specifying their block and share index. Use other state bits to fill the public identifier holes as in the earlier projection. The new filters also have fixed names. Thus all output bits are still constants or copies. For $t=\lceil C(3w)^a\rceil$ and $k=\lceil\log_4(8192t)\rceil$, each unpadded output length is
$$
         L_w=\Theta(2^w w t\log(t+2)).
$$
The lower estimate follows also from the number of distinct private filter bits. Whitespace padding to any intermediate byte-aligned length $N$ preserves the promise, with $\log_2 N<3w$ eventually. Choose $C\ge3$. If the list gate exponent is $\sigma>0$, the resulting circuit bound for any $0<a<\sigma$ is
$$
         \Omega\!\left(\frac{N(\log N)^{\sigma-a}}{\log\log N}\right)
$$
with pair bias at most $(\log_2 N)^{-a}$. In particular take $0<a<\sigma/2$ to obtain the headline bound. For rational $a$ in that interval the template can again be generated and checked in $O(N)$ word steps, recovering the list from the repeated digits and using radix sort for its verdict. This gives a single correct SAT program running in linear time on the very files used for the gate bound.
