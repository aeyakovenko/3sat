# A distributed charge family with a sharp native word bound

Fix the connected 10-regular expander on $t=13s^2$ vertices from the [explicit charge construction](witness-dependent-charges-and-the-negative-sat-clock.md#a-simple-graph-generated-within-the-encoded-clock), taking $s=5^j$. Its ten neighbors are computable in constant word work per vertex and $t\equiv1\pmod6$. For each of the $6^t$ vectors $b\in(\mathbb Z/6)^t$ give every directed half-edge a residue, with opposite orientations summing to zero. Use one *global* free selector $a\in\{2,3\}$. At vertex $v$ require outgoing sum $a b_v$. On any connected graph a prescribed divergence has a flow exactly when its total vanishes. For sufficiency set off-tree values to zero and process tree leaves inward, satisfying each leaf and subtracting its contribution at the parent. Hence the graph is feasible exactly when $B=\sum_v b_v$ belongs to $\{0,2,3,4\}$ modulo six. The negative charges have residues 1 and 5, exactly $2\cdot6^{t-1}$ vectors. Each coordinate can be changed to reverse the verdict on *every* vector, since varying that coordinate visits all six totals.

Use a fixed array of local Boolean checks for domains, reversals and the ten-term vertex sums. Three input bits encode a charge by its usual binary representation, in the range 0 through 5. Each local check has bounded size and can use definitional gate clauses with honest local witness values. Convert each clause of width at most three to a pair of not-all-equal triples with one private Boolean witness. For a clause $u\lor v\lor w$, the triples are $(u,v,d)$ and $(\neg d,w,F)$, where $F$ is the false palette vertex. Short clauses can repeat a literal here. Use the palette triangle, complementary literal pairs adjacent to its third color, and a private triangle with one edge to each respective literal per not-all-equal triple. Its three colors can be assigned iff the three literal colors are mixed. Fix the palette's color names when specifying the local extension. A witness for the two triples and each small triangle is then a fixed total truth table on boundedly many bits, with a default on impossible patterns. Using $F$ in the second triple fixes its interpretation relative to the charge pins.

Start with an edge to the true palette vertex $T$ from each positive charge-bit vertex $q$, so it must be false. For this charge bit also include a distinct new leaf $l$ joined only to $F$. The switched incidence pattern replaces $(q,T),(l,F)$ by $(q,F),(l,T)$. It forces $q$ true and its fresh leaf extends in either state. Include all these edges when forming the two-path encoding, without peeling the leaves. In the baseline blue order record the positions of the incidence data vertices belonging respectively to $T$ and $F$ for those two edges. Swap those two blue entries to encode 1. The places occupied by their red labels remain fixed. In blue the labels consequently acquire the opposite splitter centers, while in red their inequalities to the $q$- and $l$-endpoints stay in place. Thus in the coloring constraints they implement precisely the stated incidence change. Every charge bit uses two distinct incidence slots, including when several bits see the same palette vertex. Renaming the two data labels by their new blue owners restores the original splitter description. This renaming introduces no dependency on source assignments.

For each negative vector and each coefficient field, choose $p=3$ in characteristic two and $p=2$ otherwise, and fix $a=6/p$. Put $\lambda=B\bmod p$, which is nonzero. Since $t\equiv1\pmod p$, the demands $b_v-\lambda$ sum to zero in $\mathbb Z/p$. Fix opposite half-edge shifts $\delta$ with these divergences by the tree argument. Map a source half-edge value $z$ from the all-one mod-$p$ Tseitin system to $(6/p)(\lambda z+\delta)\bmod6$. Source reversal and source unit divergence imply the target reversal and charge checks on their respective neighborhoods. The scaling by $\lambda$ also covers total residue 5 in the mod-three branch. Fix all charge bits, their leaves and the selector to the indicated values and choose honest values for each of the bounded gates. This is a local substitution of the type in the [all-fields Tseitin proof](every-field-refutations-for-path-augmentation.md). Combining it with constant-local coloring extensions through *both* order constructions gives degree $\Omega(t)$ for ordinary PC on the final coloring CNF over every field on every one of these negative permutations. The clause axioms use violation indicators and the Boolean axioms. The degree-size inequality gives total monomial size $2^{\Omega(t)}$, since the final number of variables is $O(t)$. The same local interpolation transfers the real SoS degree bound if the real sign-system version is used. One globally correct program attains $O(t)$ word steps on this entire family. Fix the emitter once with an identical unoptimized gate, triangle and pin allocation for every local check of a given kind. With fixed degree its numbers of variables, edges and emitted positions depend only on $t$. Generate the source layers with dense consecutive identifiers, fixed port counts and fixed incidence lists. All graph, gate and order arrays and the baseline switch-position table take $O(t)$ word work. The geometric growth of $t=13\cdot25^j$ makes the total work for testing earlier size candidates $O(t)$ as well. Alternatively the fixed linear item counts and successor padding determine the candidate from the file length. Read one designated low field per charge bit in the expected baseline and swapped forms, reject this recognition attempt on an illegal value or three-bit pattern, and check the *entire* input by comparison with the regenerated candidate. Distinct old entries give distinct candidate values in that field. Only after equality succeeds, return the verdict from the sum modulo six. On a failed match, use any total decision procedure. ## The verdict itself has full degree on one binary cube

Set $b_1=3x_1$, $b_2=2x_2$, and $b_i=x_i$ for $3\le i\le t$, with Boolean switches $x_i$. Every physical bit in either canonical format is a constant, one switch or its complement, since differing fields for distinct coordinates are disjoint. The exact multilinear polynomial for the verdict on this same cube has degree $t$ over **every field**. To see the characteristic-independent coefficient, write $f=(1,0,1,1,1,0)$ in residue order and $\Delta_jg(r)=g(r+j)-g(r)$, with subscripts modulo six. Boolean Möbius inversion makes the coefficient of $x_1\cdots x_t$ equal to $(\Delta_1^{t-2}\Delta_2\Delta_3 f)(0)$ over the integers. Starting with $\Delta_2\Delta_3 f$, successive $\Delta_1$ applications give the three-cycle
$$
 (-1,-1,0,1,1,0),\qquad (0,1,1,0,-1,-1),\qquad (1,0,-1,-1,0,1).
$$
Since $t\equiv1\pmod3$, the coefficient is exactly $1$. It survives in every characteristic. Physical-bit polynomials agreeing with SAT even just on these files therefore have degree at least $t$, by the affine one-bit projection and multilinear reduction. The same upper bound of $O(t)$ word steps still applies. This conclusion concerns what the computation outputs as a function of each independent charge, in addition to the formal refutations required on the negative inputs.
## Adaptive affine queries and multiplication gates

On the fixed binary cube of charges with weights $3,2,1,\ldots,1$, the coefficient of $x_1\cdots x_t$ in the unique multilinear verdict polynomial is the integer $+1$. Fix $q=p^a$. Permit one query to return *any* affine form over $\mathbb F_q$ of the physical input bits, with its coefficients freely chosen from all previous answers. Each physical bit on the cube is constant, $x_i$, or $1-x_i$, so every query restricts to an affine form of the $t$ Boolean switches. All computation on the answers is free.

A correct deterministic query tree has depth at least $\lceil t/[a(p-1)]\rceil$. Fix a basis over $\mathbb F_p$. The indicator that one query has a specified answer is the product of the $a$ factors $1-(L_j-u_j)^{p-1}$ for its scalar coordinate forms. On each root-to-leaf path all coefficients have become constants, and the product of the answer indicators selects precisely the inputs following that path. The sum of these products over accepting leaves has degree at most $a(p-1)$ times the tree depth. Multilinear reduction on the Boolean cube cannot raise degree. The top coefficient forces the bound. This proof allows arbitrary nonuniform trees and arbitrary global measurements chosen adaptively.

The depth is **exactly** $\lceil t/(2a)\rceil$ over $\mathbb F_{3^a}$ on this same cube. First measure $x_1-x_3$ and $x_2-x_3$ in $\mathbb F_3$. Two distinct Boolean triples giving the same answers differ by a nonzero constant ternary vector, so the only collision is $000$ with $111$. Both have weighted sum zero modulo six. The remaining $t-3$ unit-weight switches can be paired since $t$ is odd. Each pair sum in $\mathbb F_3$ reveals its integer value in $\{0,1,2\}$. These $(t+1)/2$ prime-field measurements determine the total charge modulo six. Pack up to $a$ answers into one extension-field query using the basis. Each switch can be read as an affine form of a physical bit, since its distinct coordinate field values differ in at least one bit controlled only by that switch. In characteristic two, packing the raw switches in groups of $a$ attains the lower bound $\lceil t/a\rceil$.

A parity restriction of the six-valued family gives a shorter sharp example. On $m$ independent coordinates put charges $3x_i$ with one additional fixed unit offset. Total residue is one on even weight and four on odd weight, giving the Boolean parity predicate. Over $\mathbb F_3$ its multilinear polynomial is $(1-\prod_i(1-2x_i))/2$, whose top coefficient is nonzero for $m>0$. Pair sums give exactly $\lceil m/2\rceil$ ternary-affine queries. More generally one prime-field sum counts up to $p-1$ input bits without wraparound for any odd $p$, attaining $\lceil m/[a(p-1)]\rceil$ after packing.

There is a separate lower bound for division-free straight-line arithmetic circuits over $\mathbb F_q$ with arbitrary affine gates, scalar multiplications, and binary nonscalar multiplications, where the output must equal the Boolean verdict. If there are $s$ nonscalar multiplications, simulate a circuit on an adaptive tree maintaining every evaluated wire as an affine form on that branch. At a multiplication, measure one operand. Its value is now constant on the child, so the product becomes a scalar multiple of the other affine operand. At the end each leaf outputs an affine form. Choose an $\mathbb F_p$-linear projection $\mathbb F_q\to\mathbb F_p$ fixing 1, so the projected output is still the Boolean verdict. Sum over leaves the corresponding path indicator times the projected affine output. Its degree is at most $a(p-1)s+1$, giving $s\ge\lceil(t-1)/[a(p-1)]\rceil$ on the physical cube. On the parity subfamily use $m$ in place of $t$. This counts nonlinear processing even when every affine combination of the input bits is free. The specified primitives and exact output convention are essential. A RAM can maintain the mod-six total with word instructions.

The argument uses exact multilinear degree and the usual decision-tree indicator expansion. Parity decision trees for mod-three predicates over the binary field have extensive earlier literature, including exact bounds. The contribution claimed here is the application to the fixed charge files and, in characteristic three, the matching query schedule for their mixed weights. A single measurement over a prime field of order greater than $5t$ can also recover the whole integer sum for this family. Both facts locate the dependence on the fixed field in the lower bound.
